Falkner is a town on Mississippi Highway 15 in northern [Tippah County, Mississippi](/tippah-county/), between [Ripley](/communities/ripley/) and [Walnut](/communities/walnut/). Just south of town, Highway 15 meets Mississippi Highway 370, the route west to Ashland in Benton County.

## The name

The town is named for **Colonel William Clark Falkner**, the 19th-century Ripley railroad builder, novelist and Confederate officer who was the great-grandfather of the novelist William Faulkner. The railroad Colonel Falkner built ran north out of Ripley through this part of the county, and the town that grew along it took his name. More of that history is on the [Ripley page](/communities/ripley/).

## Schools and the Eagles

Falkner is served by the **North Tippah School District**, whose central office is located at 20821 Highway 15 in Falkner. The town's school is **Falkner Attendance Center**, a K-12 campus competing as the **Eagles**.

Falkner volleyball, football and basketball are covered through the season by RCTV19 on the [Tippah County sports page](/topics/tippah-county-sports/).
