Chalybeate is a community in northern [Tippah County, Mississippi](/tippah-county/), on Mississippi Highway 354 near [Walnut](/communities/walnut/). It was first listed as a **census-designated place** in the 2020 U.S. census.

## Where the name comes from

The community was originally called **Chalybeate Springs**, for an iron-rich spring found there. *Chalybeate* means "impregnated with iron," and mineral springs like this one were once believed to have real medicinal value — enough that they drew visitors and, in many Southern towns, supported a resort trade.

The settlement grew out of an earlier community called **Jonesborough**, founded before 1840, which makes this one of the older continuously settled corners of the county.

## Schools

Chalybeate is served by the **North Tippah School District** through **Chalybeate Elementary School**, which enrolls students in kindergarten through eighth grade. Students continue on to high school elsewhere in the district — see the [Walnut](/communities/walnut/) and [Falkner](/communities/falkner/) pages.

District news is collected on the [Tippah County schools page](/topics/tippah-county-schools/), and school sports are covered on the [Tippah County sports page](/topics/tippah-county-sports/).
