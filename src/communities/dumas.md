Dumas is a town in the southeastern part of [Tippah County, Mississippi](/tippah-county/), a few miles from the Prentiss County line. It is one of the county's five incorporated municipalities and one of its quietest — a rural community of farms, churches and homes rather than a commercial center.

Dumas sits at roughly 34°37′35″N, 88°50′23″W. Its population has been remarkably stable — it grew by just 0.2 percent between the 2010 and 2020 censuses.

## Schools

Dumas is served by the **South Tippah School District**, headquartered at 402 Greenlee Street in [Ripley](/communities/ripley/) (662-837-7156). District students attend school in Ripley, [Blue Mountain](/communities/blue-mountain/) or [Pine Grove](/communities/pine-grove/) depending on attendance zone.

School news, student honors and district decisions are collected on the [Tippah County schools page](/topics/tippah-county-schools/).
