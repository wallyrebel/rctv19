Blue Mountain is a small town in the southern end of [Tippah County, Mississippi](/tippah-county/), at the intersection of Mississippi Highways 2 and 15. It is, more than anything else, a college town: the town grew up around the school that is now **Blue Mountain Christian University**, and the campus still sets the rhythm of the year here.

The name refers to the bluish morning cast of the surrounding hills.

## Blue Mountain Christian University

Founded in **1873**, the institution spent most of its history as Blue Mountain College and now operates as **Blue Mountain Christian University**, a private liberal arts university supported by the Mississippi Baptist Convention. The town incorporated four years after the college opened, and the two have been bound together ever since.

The university's teams — the **Toppers** — compete in the Southern States Athletic Conference in the NAIA, fielding volleyball, men's and women's soccer, softball, golf, cross country and more. RCTV19 covers the program through the season on the [Blue Mountain Christian page](/topics/blue-mountain-christian/).

## Schools

The town is served by the **South Tippah School District** through **Blue Mountain School**, whose teams compete as the **Cougars**. District news and student achievements are collected on the [Tippah County schools page](/topics/tippah-county-schools/).
