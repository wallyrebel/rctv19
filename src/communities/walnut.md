Walnut sits in the northern part of [Tippah County, Mississippi](/tippah-county/), where U.S. Highway 72 crosses Mississippi Highway 15 — the main route between Memphis and Corinth. That junction has made Walnut the county's northern gateway and given a town of a few hundred residents an outsized amount of through traffic, truck stops and roadside business.

## History

The community was settled in 1872 under the name **Hopkins** and renamed **Walnut** in 1876. The older business district grew up along what is now Mississippi Highway 354, away from the four-lane; the newer commercial strip follows U.S. 72.

## Schools and the Wildcats

Walnut is served by the **North Tippah School District** through **Walnut Attendance Center**, a K-12 campus whose teams compete as the **Wildcats**.

Walnut football has been one of the more consistent programs in the county. Head coach **John Meeks**, who took over in 2012, is the winningest football coach in Tippah County history, and every one of his career victories has come at Walnut. RCTV19 follows the Wildcats through football, volleyball and basketball season on the [Tippah County sports page](/topics/tippah-county-sports/).

## Community services

The **Walnut Fire Department**, at 27880 Highway 15, doubles as the site of regular food pantry distributions run by North Mississippi Primary Health Care and the Mid-South Food Bank. Those distributions are announced on RCTV19 as they are scheduled — see the [community events page](/topics/community-events/).

## Nearby

[Chalybeate](/communities/chalybeate/) lies just outside Walnut on Highway 354, and [Tiplersville](/communities/tiplersville/) and [Falkner](/communities/falkner/) are the next communities south. [Ripley](/communities/ripley/), the county seat, is about 12 miles south on Highway 15.
