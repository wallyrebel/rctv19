Pine Grove is an unincorporated community in [Tippah County, Mississippi](/tippah-county/), east of [Ripley](/communities/ripley/). Like many rural Mississippi communities, its center of gravity is its school: for most of the county, "Pine Grove" means **Pine Grove School** and the **Panthers**.

## Pine Grove by the numbers

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| **County** | Tippah |
| **Status** | Unincorporated community |
| **Area code** | 662 |
| **School district** | South Tippah School District |
| **School** | Pine Grove School — the Panthers |

Because Pine Grove is unincorporated and is not a census-designated place, the Census Bureau does not publish a separate population figure for it.

## Pine Grove School and the Panthers

**Pine Grove School** is one of the South Tippah School District's campuses, alongside the three Ripley schools and [Blue Mountain School](/communities/blue-mountain/). The district office is at 402 Greenlee Street in Ripley (662-837-7156).

Panther volleyball and basketball are a regular part of RCTV19's coverage through the season, including the county tournament and district play against [Falkner](/communities/falkner/), [Walnut](/communities/walnut/) and Blue Mountain. Recent results are on the [Tippah County sports page](/topics/tippah-county-sports/), and district and student news is on the [Tippah County schools page](/topics/tippah-county-schools/).

## County services

Pine Grove has no municipal government; Tippah County offices in Ripley handle tags, taxes, deeds and voter registration, and the sheriff's department provides law enforcement.
