Tiplersville is an unincorporated community in northern [Tippah County, Mississippi](/tippah-county/), between [Walnut](/communities/walnut/) to the north and [Ripley](/communities/ripley/) to the south. It has no separate municipal government — county government serves it directly — but it keeps its own ZIP code, its own churches and a name that has been on Tippah County maps for well over a century.

Tiplersville sits at roughly 34°53′44″N, 88°54′27″W. Because it is unincorporated, the U.S. Census Bureau does not publish a separate population count for it.

## Schools

The community falls within the **North Tippah School District**, which serves the Tiplersville, [Walnut](/communities/walnut/), [Falkner](/communities/falkner/) and [Chalybeate](/communities/chalybeate/) communities from a central office at 20821 Highway 15 in Falkner.

District news and student achievements appear on the [Tippah County schools page](/topics/tippah-county-schools/); school sports are on the [Tippah County sports page](/topics/tippah-county-sports/).

## County services

With no city hall of its own, Tiplersville residents rely on Tippah County offices in Ripley for tags, taxes, deeds and voter registration, and on the county sheriff's department for law enforcement.
