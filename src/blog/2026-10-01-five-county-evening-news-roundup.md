Thursday's reports bring a sports title, a college band event, a hotel opening and a local archives invitation. The hotel ribbon cutting happened Tuesday; its completed outcome was reported Thursday. Times are Central.

## Union County: New Albany volleyball takes division title

The New Albany Lady Bulldogs won their division championship with a **3–0 sweep**, [Union County News reported Thursday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/new-albany-volleyball-team-wins-division-championship-in-straight-sets/), citing team officials. The report did not name the opponent or specify the match date, so those details remain unconfirmed here.

## Prentiss County: Regional band championship set for Saturday

Northeast Mississippi Community College in Booneville says it will host the **43rd Annual Regional Band Championship on Saturday, Oct. 3**, [Prentiss County News reported Thursday](https://prentissnews.com/local-news/northeast-mississippi-community-college-to-host-43rd-annual-regional-band-championship/). Bands from across the region are expected. The college said its itinerary is subject to change and that it will post weather-related updates if needed. Check the organizer's current schedule before traveling.

## Alcorn County: Corinth hotel opens after ribbon cutting

Corinth's **TownePlace Suites by Marriott** is open following a **Tuesday, Sept. 29, ribbon cutting** hosted by developer Delta Lodging Group, [Alcorn County News reported Thursday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-towneplace-suites-opens/). The extended-stay hotel on South Parkway Street has **89 suites**, according to that report. RCTV 19 previously listed the planned ceremony; Thursday's report confirms that it occurred and the property opened.

## Tippah County: Archives invites visitors during October

The Tippah County Historical & Genealogical Society marked the start of **Archives Month on Thursday** with an invitation to research local family history at the Tippah County Archives, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-county-archives-month/). The archives are at **201 Union St. in Ripley** and list weekday hours of **9 a.m. to 2 p.m.** The collection includes county, will and marriage records, according to the report. Confirm hours with the society before making a special trip.
