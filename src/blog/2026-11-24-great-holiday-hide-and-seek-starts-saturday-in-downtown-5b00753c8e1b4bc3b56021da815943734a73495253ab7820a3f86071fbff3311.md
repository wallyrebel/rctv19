Ripley Main Street's holiday scavenger hunt begins Saturday, November 28.

Participants can pick up a Brownie Card at Brown & Covington, then search for Brownie hidden throughout downtown shops. After finding Brownie in at least eight locations and collecting stamps, hunters return their card to Brown & Covington for a free gelato and a chance to win Downtown Ripley Moolah.

The hunt runs through December 28. Participants are encouraged to photograph Brownie at each shop and tag both the shop and Brown & Covington on social media.
