# Holiday Shopping Events Coming to Downtown Ripley

Downtown Ripley is preparing for the holiday shopping season with several promotional events scheduled throughout November and December.

**Upcoming Shopping Events:**
- Pink Friday: November 20
- Black Friday: November 27
- Shop Small Saturday: November 28

**New Brownie Scavenger Hunt**

New this year, a character named Brownie will be hidden in retail shops throughout town starting November 28. Shoppers who locate Brownie in at least 6 different shops will receive a free scoop of gelato at Brown & Covington. The scavenger hunt runs through December 28.

Local organizers are encouraging residents to shop downtown and support local businesses during the holiday season.
