## Prentiss County: Industry leaders discuss a possible apprenticeship chapter

Regional industry leaders met Thursday at Northeast Mississippi Community College in Booneville to discuss a possible local chapter of the Federation for Advanced Manufacturing Education, [Alcorn County News reported Friday](https://alcornnewsms.com/mississippi-news/nemcc-fame-chapter-booneville/). The FAME model combines college classes with paid work at manufacturers. Northeast did **not** announce a launch date, employer partners or an agreement; the gathering was exploratory.

## Alcorn County: Theatre group announces permanent closure

Corinth Theatre-Arts announced Friday that it is closing permanently and immediately, [Alcorn County News reported](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-theatre-arts-announces-permanent-closure-the-final-act/), citing the group's statement. The board cited breaches of trust and financial problems. The announcement follows the group's failed appeal of its eviction from the city-owned South Fulton Drive playhouse, which this roundup covered earlier in the week. Friday's closure announcement is a new development; the article does not identify the people or transactions behind the board's concerns.

In a separate Thursday result, Corinth beat South Pontotoc **63-0** in its division football opener, [Alcorn County News reported Friday](https://alcornnewsms.com/sports/corinth-football-south-pontotoc-shutout/), citing the Corinth Touchdown Club. The game had been moved forward from Friday because of the forecast; the earlier schedule change was reported in a previous roundup.

## Tippah County: Walnut schedules Halloween candy event

The Town of Walnut announced Friday that **Sweet Treats on Main** is planned for **Saturday, Oct. 31, starting at 6 p.m.** on downtown Main Street, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/walnut-sweet-treats-on-main/). The town is inviting businesses, churches, families and other groups to help distribute candy. Participants can contact Walnut Town Hall at **662-223-4405** to sign up. This is an announcement of a future event, not a report that it has taken place.
