## Union: New Albany and Myrtle exit playoffs

New Albany lost 42-7 at Louisville in Friday's Class 4A football playoff game, according to [MaxPreps' team results](https://www.maxpreps.com/ms/new-albany/new-albany-bulldogs/football/). Myrtle fell 34-8 to North Side in its Friday playoff game, according to a [MaxPreps game recap](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/1goD3UdWV02tjKtt0KGroA/football-recap-myrtle-falls%2C-snapping-its-eight-game-win-streak--how-to-watch.htm). Both are new results since the previous roundup; the earlier playoff wins are not repeated here.

## Prentiss: Baldwyn falls to East Webster

Baldwyn lost 26-0 at home to East Webster on Friday in the Class 2A playoffs, [MaxPreps reports](https://www.maxpreps.com/ms/baldwyn/baldwyn-bearcats/football/). MaxPreps lists the result as final.

## Alcorn: Corinth and Kossuth fall

Corinth lost 27-13 at home to Kosciusko in Friday's Class 4A playoff game, according to [MaxPreps' results](https://www.maxpreps.com/ms/kosciusko/kosciusko-whippets/football/). Kossuth lost 53-7 at Winona in a Class 3A playoff game, according to a [MaxPreps recap](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/o7QO1TPF60yL5BMY8cjuiw/football-recap-tough-loss-for-kossuth--how-to-watch.htm).
