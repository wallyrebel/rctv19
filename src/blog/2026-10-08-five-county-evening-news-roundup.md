Thursday evening's edition covers developments verified since the morning roundup. Times are Central. Weather conditions and event plans can change.

## All five counties: Flood Watch issued

The [National Weather Service in Memphis issued a Flood Watch at 12:13 p.m. Thursday](https://www.weather.gov/wwamap/wwatxtget.php?cwa=meg&wwa=all) for **Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties** from **late Friday night through Sunday morning**. This is a new alert since the morning edition's forecast. A watch means flooding is possible; it does not mean flooding has occurred.

The weather service says the eventual remnants of Hurricane Isaias could bring **local rainfall amounts up to 4 inches** across its broader watch area, mainly east of the Mississippi River. It warns that runoff could affect streams, low-lying areas and places with poor drainage. That figure is a regional possibility, not a forecast total for each of the five counties. Residents in flood-prone areas should monitor [current NWS alerts](https://www.weather.gov/meg/) and be ready to act if a warning follows.

## Alcorn County: Corinth approves theater lease

Corinth's Board of Mayor and Aldermen approved a **one-year lease** Tuesday, Oct. 6, for the Corinth Dramatic Society to use the city-owned playhouse at **303 South Fulton Drive**, [Alcorn County News reported Thursday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-dramatic-society-playhouse-lease/), citing the *Daily Corinthian*. The city budget also allocates **$12,000** to the group. The lease follows the closure of Corinth Theatre-Arts, the building's former tenant. The report does not give a date for the Dramatic Society's first production there.

## Tippah County: Bids sought for road resealing

The Tippah County Board of Supervisors is taking bids to **reseal 21.084 miles of roads** under State Aid Project SAP-70(20)S. Proposals are due **Thursday, Oct. 15, at 10 a.m.**, according to the [state procurement listing](https://www.ms.gov/dfa/contract_bid_search/Bid/Details/46663) and [Tippah News report published Wednesday afternoon](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-news-2026-10-07-road-resealing-bids/). This is an open solicitation, not a contract award or notice that work has started. The project documents identify the roads as A through M; the linked notice does not establish a start date.

## Prentiss County: Firefighters assist near county line

Firefighters from Prentiss County joined Tishomingo County departments and the Mississippi Forestry Commission on Wednesday evening to fight multiple roadside fires along Highway 30 near Paden and the Prentiss County line, [WTVA reported](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/07/firefighters-battling-spot-fires-along-highway/). [Alcorn County News reported Thursday](https://alcornnewsms.com/mississippi-news/highway-30-fires-out-paden/) that the fires were out and the road had reopened, citing a Tishomingo County News update. The fires were in **Tishomingo County**, and no official agency has confirmed their cause.

## Union County: Festival adds basket-making details

A [Union County News report published Thursday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/basket-making-showcased-at-mississippi-hills-heritage-and-folklife-festival-in-october/) adds details to the previously announced Mississippi Hills Heritage and Folklife Festival in New Albany. It says **J.D. Jones** is scheduled to demonstrate basket making Oct. 22 and 23, and **Jamie VanOekel** on Oct. 24. The community day remains scheduled for Saturday, Oct. 24, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. These are planned demonstrations, not completed events.
