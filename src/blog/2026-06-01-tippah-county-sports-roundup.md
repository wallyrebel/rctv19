High school and college sports from Tippah County and Blue Mountain Christian, June 1–7, 2026.

## Blue Mountain's Phillip Laney Honored with SSAC Coach of Character Award

*June 1, 2026*

<img src="/assets/img/posts/blue-mountains-phillip-laney-honored-with-ssac-coa-1780326265475.webp" alt="Blue Mountain's Phillip Laney Honored with SSAC Coach of Character Award" loading="lazy">

Phillip Laney, a coach at Blue Mountain Christian University, has been awarded the SSAC Coach of Character Award, a prestigious recognition that celebrates excellence both on and off the field.

The award was presented at the SSAC conference's annual banquet, honoring Laney's outstanding commitment to embodying the core values that define exemplary coaching. According to the award criteria, Laney was recognized for his exceptional demonstration of integrity, respect, and servant leadership throughout his coaching career.

The Coach of Character Award represents one of the conference's highest honors, reserved for coaches who go beyond winning games to positively impact their athletes' lives and communities. By selecting Laney for this distinction, the SSAC acknowledged his dedication to developing student-athletes as individuals while maintaining the highest standards of personal and professional conduct.

Laney's recognition reflects Blue Mountain Christian University's commitment to values-based athletics and highlights the institution's emphasis on character development alongside athletic achievement.

## BMCU Softball Team Honored with SSAC Champions of Character Award

*June 1, 2026*

<img src="/assets/img/posts/bmcu-softball-team-honored-with-ssac-champions-of--1780326260894.webp" alt="BMCU Softball Team Honored with SSAC Champions of Character Award" loading="lazy">

Blue Mountain Christian University's softball program has earned significant recognition beyond the playing field, receiving the SSAC Mayor Todd Strange Female Champions of Character Team Award.

### Recognition of Excellence

The honor highlights the team's exceptional commitment to integrity, sportsmanship, and community service. The award, named after Mayor Todd Strange, represents the Southern States Athletic Conference's dedication to recognizing student-athletes who exemplify character and leadership both on and off the diamond.

### Historic Season Achievement

The recognition comes during what has been described as a historic season for the BMCU softball program. The team's achievement of the Champions of Character award underscores that success extends far beyond winning games—it encompasses the values and principles that define the student-athletes as individuals and representatives of their institution.

### Community Impact

The award reflects the team's dedication to making a positive impact within their community and university. By receiving this honor, BMCU's softball program demonstrates that athletic excellence and personal character development go hand in hand, setting an example for other programs across the conference.

This recognition serves as a testament to the coaching staff's emphasis on developing well-rounded student-athletes who understand the importance of integrity, leadership, and service to others.
