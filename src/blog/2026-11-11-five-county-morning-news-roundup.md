## Alcorn: Corinth parade, closures and garbage routes today

Corinth's Veterans Day parade is **scheduled** to start at 10 a.m. Wednesday, Nov. 11, on Fillmore Street and end at Trailhead Park, [Alcorn County News reports](https://alcornnewsms.com/corinth-news/corinth-veterans-day-closings-guide/). Lineup begins at 9 a.m. behind First Baptist Church. The Corinth VFW plans a 1 p.m. soup-and-sandwich lunch, free for veterans, with the public welcome for a small donation. These are plans, not a report that the events have happened.

City and Alcorn County offices are closed for the holiday. Corinth's Wednesday garbage routes move to Thursday, Nov. 12; Thursday and Friday routes are scheduled for Friday, Nov. 13, according to the [same local guide](https://alcornnewsms.com/corinth-news/corinth-veterans-day-closings-guide/).

## Alcorn and Prentiss: September jobless rates edge up

Preliminary, **not seasonally adjusted** September estimates put unemployment at 3.2% in Alcorn County, up from 2.9% in August, and 3.2% in Prentiss County, up from 3.1%, [Alcorn County News reports from federal labor data](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/alcorn-county-unemployment-september-2026/). Both rates were lower than in September 2025: 3.4% in Alcorn and 3.5% in Prentiss. The report says the number of employed residents also rose month to month in both counties, so the rate increases should not be read as a count of jobs lost. These figures describe September, not current November conditions.

## Union: New Albany names holiday giveaway winners

New Albany Main Street announced 18 winners of its $2,000 Main Street Money giveaway following Sunday's holiday open house, [Union County News reports](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/new-albany-main-street-names-18-holiday-giveaway-winners/). Meg Alexander won $500; Reese Hill and Kim Day won $250 each. The [full winner list](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/new-albany-main-street-names-18-holiday-giveaway-winners/) includes prize pickup information at Bead Shack Kids. This is a follow-up to the open house itself, covered in Tuesday evening's roundup.
