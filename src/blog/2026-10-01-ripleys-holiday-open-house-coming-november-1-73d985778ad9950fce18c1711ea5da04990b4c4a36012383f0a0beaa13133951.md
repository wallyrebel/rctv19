Ripley is hosting a Holiday Open House on Sunday, November 1 from 12–4 PM, bringing together local shopping, dining, and holiday festivities downtown and throughout the community.

The event will feature opportunities to shop local and find unique gifts, explore restaurants and businesses around town, and visit pop-up shops at The Cut-Off. Organizers emphasize the event as a chance to get an early start on Christmas shopping while supporting local businesses.

Vendors interested in setting up pop-up shops at The Cut-Off during the event can sign up through the vendor registration link. The community is encouraged to tag friends and make plans to spend the afternoon in Ripley.
