## Tippah: Ripley coach selected for fellowship

Ripley High School football and powerlifting coach Jamal Braddock has been selected for the Tomorrow's 25 Coaching Fellowship, [Tippah News reported Saturday evening](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-sports/ripley-coach-braddock-tomorrows-25/). The school said the program brings coaches from several states together for leadership development. The report does not give a date for his first session.

## Union: Ingomar homecoming and New Albany shopping event

[Union County News reported](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/ingomar-high-school-announces-2026-homecoming-court-2/) that Ingomar High School announced its 2026 homecoming court. The ceremony is scheduled for Nov. 20 at 5 p.m. in the school gymnasium; the source lists the maids and escorts.

The same publication [reported a Turkey to Tinsel event](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/turkey-to-tinsel-event-planned-for-downtown-new-albany/) in downtown New Albany for Nov. 26, from 8 to 11 p.m. Organizers are encouraging holiday shopping at local businesses and Christmas pajamas.

## Prentiss, Alcorn and Benton

No distinct, meaningful new county development was verified for this edition. Saturday's Alcorn teacher recognition and the Prentiss and Alcorn parade dates were covered in the previous roundup.
