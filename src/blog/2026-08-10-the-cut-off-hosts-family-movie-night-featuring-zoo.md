# The Cut-Off Brings Community Together for Movie Night Under the Stars

Ripley residents are invited to join a festive movie night this Friday, August 14, at The Cut-Off, located at 204 South Commerce Street. The outdoor screening event will begin at dusk and promises an enjoyable evening for families and friends alike.

## Event Details

The featured presentation will be **Zootopia 2**, the latest installment in the popular animated franchise. Attendees are encouraged to arrive early, bring their families, and invite friends to enjoy the film together under the evening sky.

## Community Support

The event has been made possible through the generous sponsorship of **Rebel Services, LLC**, demonstrating the local business community's commitment to providing entertainment and fostering community engagement in Ripley.

## What to Expect

With its family-friendly atmosphere and outdoor setting, this movie night offers an ideal opportunity for residents to gather, socialize, and enjoy quality entertainment. The casual nature of the event makes it accessible for attendees of all ages, from young children to adults.

For those planning to attend, arriving early is recommended to secure a comfortable spot for viewing. The event is free and open to the public, making it an excellent choice for families looking for affordable weekend entertainment.
