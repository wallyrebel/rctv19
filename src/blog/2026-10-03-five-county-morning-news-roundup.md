Saturday morning's check centers on Friday's verified football finals and two local notices that were not in our Friday evening edition. The parade is scheduled for November; its application is open now. Scores are as listed by MaxPreps, not RCTV 19's firsthand game reports. Times are Central.

## Union County: New Albany parade applications open

The [New Albany Main Street Association](https://www.newalbanymainstreet.com/2026parade) is accepting entries for the New Albany and Union County Christmas Parade, scheduled for **Monday, Nov. 30, at 6:30 p.m.** downtown. The organizer lists a **Nov. 16 entry deadline** and a $20 entry fee; submitting the application alone does not complete registration until payment is made. Groups planning to participate can review staging rules and apply through the organizer's page.

## Prentiss County: Sheriff warns of jury-duty scam; Booneville wins

The Prentiss County Sheriff's Department warned Friday about callers posing as federal law enforcement officers, claiming residents missed jury duty and demanding payment, [Alcorn County News reported](https://alcornnewsms.com/police-departments/prentiss-county-jury-duty-scam/) from the department's announcement. The department urges people to hang up, withhold personal or financial details and contact the sheriff's office directly if they need to report a call. The report lists the department's number as **662-728-6232**.

In Friday football, Booneville won **44-22** at Kossuth in a district game, according to the [MaxPreps final](https://www.maxpreps.com/ms/booneville/booneville-blue-devils/football/). The result also affects Alcorn County, where Kossuth is located.

## Alcorn County: Alcorn Central wins at Belmont

Alcorn Central won **36-20** at Belmont on Friday in a district football game, according to the [MaxPreps game result](https://www.maxpreps.com/ms/football/game/alcorn-central-glen-vs-belmont/10-2-2026/?c=c3bd9264-459f-4f71-ab61-92b6a10cbcac&tab=Matchup). Kossuth's loss to Booneville is reported in the Prentiss County section above.

## Benton and Tippah counties: Walnut beats Ashland

Walnut defeated visiting Ashland **36-14** Friday, according to [MaxPreps' Walnut results page](https://www.maxpreps.com/ms/walnut/walnut-wildcats/football/). The matchup connects Tippah County's Wildcats and Benton County's Blue Devils. In another Tippah result, Ripley fell **22-10** at home to Clarksdale, according to [MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/ms/ripley/ripley-tigers/football/).
