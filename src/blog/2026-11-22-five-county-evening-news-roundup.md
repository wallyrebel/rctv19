## Alcorn: Corinth cemetery seeks wreath sponsors

Volunteers plan to place wreaths on veterans' graves after an **11 a.m. Dec. 19 ceremony at Corinth National Cemetery**, 1551 Horton St. Organizers are seeking sponsors and volunteers for the event, [Alcorn County News reported Sunday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-wreaths-across-america-ceremony/). Its report said the cemetery's [Wreaths Across America location page](https://www.wreathsacrossamerica.org/pages/14872/overview) showed about 1,190 wreaths sponsored as of Sunday morning, against a goal of about 8,000 graves. That count may change; check the location page to sponsor a wreath or register to help.

## Prentiss and Alcorn: Duck season opens Friday

Mississippi's first **2026–27 duck, merganser and coot season segment runs Nov. 27–29**, according to the [Department of Wildlife, Fisheries, and Parks season table](https://www.mdwfp.com/wildlife-hunting/hunting-seasons-and-bag-limits). Later segments run Dec. 4–6 and Dec. 9–Jan. 31. The statewide dates apply to hunters in Prentiss and Alcorn counties, where [Alcorn County News highlighted the approaching opener Sunday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/mississippi-duck-season-opens-nov-27/). Hunters should check the agency's current licensing, location rules and species limits before going out.

## Tippah and Benton: Cooler Thanksgiving outlook

A [National Weather Service Memphis discussion issued Sunday afternoon](https://marine.weather.gov/product.php?format=TXT&glossary=0&issuedby=MEG&product=AFD&site=HNX&version=1) expects the cold front to pass through the Mid-South by evening, with cooler air Monday and Tuesday, another chance of showers Wednesday, and a cool, dry Thanksgiving. [Tippah News' midday Ripley forecast](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/ripley-thanksgiving-forecast-2026/) also included an Ashland outlook for Benton County. Forecast timing and temperatures can change, so check the latest local forecast before holiday travel.

No distinct, meaningful new Union County development was verified for this edition.
