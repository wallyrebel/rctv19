Pink Friday is coming to downtown Ripley and surrounding areas on Friday, November 20, from 4:00 to 7:00 PM, according to Ripley Main Street.

Local shops will offer special deals and extended hours for the event. Ripley Main Street encourages residents to shop at their favorite local businesses during Pink Friday rather than waiting for Black Friday sales.
