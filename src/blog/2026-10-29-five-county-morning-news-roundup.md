School arts results and a new scholarship plan lead this morning's roundup. The marching championships took place Saturday, Oct. 24; the reports and district account cited here were published later. A fresh National Weather Service forecast also updates Tippah County's Halloween weekend outlook.

## Prentiss: Booneville band takes seventh straight state title

The Booneville High School Blue Devil Band won the **Class 3A state marching championship** in Brandon on Saturday, Oct. 24, its **seventh consecutive title** and 17th overall, [Alcorn County News reported Wednesday](https://alcornnewsms.com/public-service/booneville-band-seventh-state-championship/), citing the Booneville Banner-Independent. The report says Booneville finished more than four points ahead of Seminary and placed first in color guard, overall effect, visual performance and musical performance. The championship is a Saturday result newly reported in this roundup, not a Thursday competition.

## Alcorn: Alcorn Central and Kossuth earn top-five band finishes

The [Alcorn School District's Tuesday championship report](https://www.alcornschools.org/article/3217641) says **Alcorn Central placed third** and **Kossuth placed fifth** among Class 3A bands at the same state contest. The district says Kossuth's color guard placed second in its class, its highest finish to date. Both school bands had earned All Superior ratings at the North Regional State Marching Evaluations before advancing. These placements were reported earlier this week and had not appeared in RCTV 19's previous roundup.

## Union: District marks 20 years of theatre and plans scholarship

Union County Schools is marking **20 years** of its theatre program, founded by Ken Basil and Clint Reid to bring students from the district's four schools together, [Union County News reported Wednesday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/union-county-schools-mark-20-years-of-theatre-program/), citing the district. The school system recognized the founders during a recent production of *South Pacific* and says plans are underway for a **Basil/Reid Theatre Scholarship** for a district senior. The report describes a plan; it does not announce an application date or award amount.

## Tippah: Updated forecast shows a Saturday shower chance

The [National Weather Service forecast for Ripley updated at 6:15 a.m. Thursday](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=34.73&lon=-88.95) calls for a high near **80°F Friday** and a **30% chance of showers after 1 p.m. Saturday, Oct. 31**, with a high near 77°F. Saturday night's rain chance is 60%. The forecast is newer than [Wednesday evening's Tippah News forecast](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/tippah-county-halloween-forecast/), which cited a 40% Saturday afternoon chance. Residents planning outdoor Halloween events should check the live NWS forecast again before leaving; these are probabilities, not a report of rain already falling.

No separate meaningful new **Benton County** development with enough verified detail was found for this edition.
