The NAIA named 22 Blue Mountain Christian University student-athletes as 2026 Fall Sports Scholar-Athletes, recognizing their academic accomplishments.

Cross country accounted for more than half the Toppers on the list released Nov. 18. Six men and six women earned the award. Blue Mountain also had five recipients in men’s soccer, three in women’s soccer and two in volleyball.

The volleyball recipients were senior Marissa Gerleman and junior Abigail Williams. The three women’s soccer honorees were sophomore Krisslyn Boyles, junior Jaelyn Cox and senior Sophie Young.

The NAIA requires a cumulative grade-point average of at least 3.5 at the athlete’s institution, at least sophomore standing and a full year at the school. The athlete must also be on the sport’s eligibility certificate. Blue Mountain’s 22 honorees are named on pages 14 and 15 of the [NAIA’s official list by institution](https://www.naia.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/2026-Fall-Scholar-Athletes_Institution.pdf#page=14). The association also published its [award criteria and national totals](https://www.naia.org/news/featured/2026-naia-fall-sport-scholar-athletes/).
