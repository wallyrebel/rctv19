A newly reported Booneville board decision, a Benton County school event and state school ratings lead Saturday evening's update for Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties. The board met Tuesday, Oct. 13, while the school event and football games took place Friday, Oct. 16.

## Prentiss: Booneville plans historic-building grant application

Booneville aldermen approved **applying for a $1 million state grant** to renovate a historic hardware building, [Alcorn County News reported Saturday](https://alcornnewsms.com/public-service/booneville-aldermen-promotions-grant/), citing the *Booneville Banner*'s account of Tuesday's board meeting. If awarded, the city would provide a 25 percent match. The board also promoted Shawn Presley to Firefighter II and Vance Raines to Firefighter I, according to the report. The application is a plan; funding has not been awarded.

## Benton: Hickory Flat students explore local careers

Hickory Flat Attendance Center's Friday Discovery Day drew **more than 400 people and over 21 large vehicles**, [Tippah News reported Saturday](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/hickory-flat-discovery-day-careers/), citing the Benton County school's posts. The Benton and Marshall County sheriff's departments and New Albany Light, Gas & Water were among the participating groups. Students saw public safety equipment and a utility bucket truck as part of the career exploration event.

## Tippah and Benton: all three districts receive B grades

South Tippah, North Tippah and Benton County school districts each earned a **B** in Mississippi's 2026 accountability ratings, [Tippah News reported Saturday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-benton-school-grades-2026/) from the state education department's results. Ripley and Blue Mountain high schools each earned an A. The ratings reflect the 2025–26 school year and use higher score thresholds this year, so readers should avoid treating a letter-grade change alone as a like-for-like measure of performance.

## Tippah: Ripley and Falkner win Friday football games

Ripley beat Byhalia **42–6** on Friday to end a six-game losing streak, while Falkner won **44–8** at Ashland in its region opener, [Tippah County Sports reported](https://tippahsports.com/ripley/ripley-ends-six-game-skid-falkner-rolls-in-region-opener/). The same report says Walnut lost **41–6** to Hamilton. These are Friday results, not Saturday games.

No distinct, meaningful new **Union** or **Alcorn County** development with enough verified detail was found for this edition. The Union County volleyball titles and Alcorn County's Kossuth final were covered in earlier roundups.
