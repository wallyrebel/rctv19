## Alcorn and Prentiss: Seven schools appear on TVA's STEM award roster

The [Tennessee Valley Authority's 2026–27 recipient list](https://www.tva.com/learn/stem-awards), announced Monday, names **Alcorn Central High School, Corinth High School and Corinth Middle School** under Alcorn County Electric Power Association. It names **Booneville High School, New Site High School, Prentiss County Career and Technology Center and Wheeler Attendance Center** under Prentiss County Electric Power Association. [Alcorn County News](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/tva-stem-awards-corinth-schools/) and [Prentiss County News](https://prentissnews.com/prentiss-county-news/tva-lists-four-prentiss-county-schools-in-2026-27-stem-award-roster-83c7b44ac694/) also reported the local recipients.

[TVA says](https://www.tva.com/the-powerhouse/stories/tva-awards--1.5-million-to-support-stem-education-across-the-valley_edu) its regional program awarded $1.5 million to 334 schools. Individual awards can be up to $5,000; the published roster does **not** specify amounts or projects for these seven schools.

## Benton: MDOT lists proposed road work

The [Mississippi Department of Transportation's project schedule](https://mdot.ms.gov/applications/Schedule_of_Proposed_Projects/ScheduleOfProposedProjects.aspx), dated Nov. 23, lists four Benton County projects: an intelligent transportation systems project on I-22 and a scrub seal on Mississippi 7 in federal fiscal 2027; a thin pavement lift on Mississippi 4 in 2028; and a mill and overlay on I-22 between the Marshall and Union county lines in 2029. **These are planning entries, not announcements that work has started.** MDOT says its schedule can change.

No separate meaningful new Union or Tippah County development was verified for this edition.
