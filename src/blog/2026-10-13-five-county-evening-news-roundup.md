Two Alcorn County public-safety updates were reported after this morning's five-county roundup. The status of county burn bans can change, so readers should use the [Mississippi Forestry Commission's current listing](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/burning-info/burn-bans/) before making burning plans.

## Alcorn: county burn ban has expired

Alcorn County's outdoor burn ban ran through **Monday, Oct. 12** and is no longer shown for the county on the [Forestry Commission's Tuesday map](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/burning-info/burn-bans/). [Alcorn County News reported Tuesday afternoon](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/alcorn-county-burn-ban-ends/) that the order had expired without an extension. That changes the county-ban status covered in Monday's roundup; it does not mean outdoor fires are risk-free or that every type of burning is permitted under all local rules.

The same state map still marks **Tippah County** under a burn ban, as noted in this morning's roundup. Residents should check the commission's current list and any applicable local rules. The image above records the **Oct. 13** Alcorn update; it is not a live map.

## Alcorn: emergency management seeks rescue experience

[Alcorn County News reported Tuesday morning](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/alcorn-county-technical-rescue-volunteers/) that Alcorn County Emergency Management had asked people with technical rescue training or experience to volunteer. The agency's Monday request named rope, trench, structural-collapse, swiftwater or flood, and confined-space rescue among the useful specialties. The report says the agency did not specify a deadline or required certifications. Interested people can contact [Alcorn County Emergency Management through its official page](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61572761565399) for current details.

No separate meaningful new local development was verified this afternoon for **Union, Prentiss, or Benton** counties. Tippah's active ban is included above as current context; its status had already been reported this morning.
