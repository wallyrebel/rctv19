Blue Mountain Christian University won 78-66 over Central Baptist College in women's basketball Tuesday, Nov. 17, at Tyler Gym. The Toppers built an 18-point lead in the first quarter and improved to 6–1.

Central Baptist scored just four points in the opening quarter. By halftime, Blue Mountain led 43–24. The Mustangs scored 26 in the fourth quarter, their best stretch of the evening, but the Toppers had opened the period ahead 61–40.

Dominique Collins came off the bench to score 16 points on seven baskets, including two 3-pointers. Isabella Aversa added 15 points and three steals. Brooklyn Hodum made three shots from beyond the arc and finished with 13 points. Lena Taylor contributed 12 points, seven rebounds and four assists.

*An earlier version gave the day as Monday. Blue Mountain Christian’s [official box score](https://bmcusports.com/sports/womens-basketball/stats/2026-27/central-baptist-college/boxscore/5884) records the game on Tuesday, Nov. 17.*
