Ripley Main Street is participating in Pink Friday, a nationwide shop-small event held annually on the Friday before Black Friday. Today's deals include a $10 sweatshirt rack, significant clearance on totes, and $7 doorbusters, along with new arrivals added last night.

The store will remain open from 4:00-7:00 p.m. today with extended hours to accommodate shoppers.

Pink Friday, created in 2020 by The Boutique Hub, encourages customers to #SupportSmallFirst by shopping at local boutiques and independent retailers before major retailers' holiday promotions begin.
