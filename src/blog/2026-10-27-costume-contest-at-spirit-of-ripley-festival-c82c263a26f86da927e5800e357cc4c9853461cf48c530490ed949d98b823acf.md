# Costume Contest at Spirit of Ripley Festival

The Spirit of Ripley Festival is inviting community members to showcase their costumes at a free costume contest.

**Event Details:**
- **Registration:** 5:30–6:15 PM on the west side of the square
- **Contest:** 6:30 PM on the square
- **Eligibility:** All ages welcome
- **Cost:** Free to enter
- **Prizes:** Available for multiple age groups

The event is sponsored by Lindsey Davis & Associates CPA.
