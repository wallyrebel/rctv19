Ripley Main Street is recruiting pop-up vendors for its Jingle & Mingle Shop Small Saturday event on Saturday, November 28, in downtown Ripley.

Makers, artisans, boutiques and small businesses can reserve outdoor vendor spots at Brown & Covington, with overflow space available at The Cut-Off. Each vendor spot costs $20.

Interested vendors should send a private message to Ripley Main Street on Facebook with their name, business name and a brief description of what they sell. Space is limited.
