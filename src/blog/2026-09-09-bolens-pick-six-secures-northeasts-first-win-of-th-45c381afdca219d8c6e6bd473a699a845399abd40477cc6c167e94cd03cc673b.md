# Bolen's pick-six secures Northeast's first win of the season

Northeast Mississippi Community College earned its first victory of the season in Booneville, Mississippi, with a decisive defensive play. Jeb Bolen recorded a pick-six that proved crucial in securing the win for the team.

The interception return for a touchdown highlighted Northeast's defensive performance and marked a significant moment for Bolen in his football career.
