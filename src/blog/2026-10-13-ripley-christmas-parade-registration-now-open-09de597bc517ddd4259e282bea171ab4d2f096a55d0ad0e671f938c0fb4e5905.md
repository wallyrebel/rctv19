# Ripley Christmas Parade Registration Now Open

The Ripley Christmas Parade is inviting the community to participate in its upcoming event, scheduled for 8 weeks away. Organizers are encouraging participants to begin preparing floats now.

This year's parade theme is "Christmas Through the Decades," and organizers say they're excited to see the community's creativity in bringing this concept to life.

## Who Can Participate

The parade welcomes entries from:
- Businesses
- Churches
- Schools
- Organizations
- Community groups

## Key Details

**Cost:** There is no cost to participate in the parade.

**How to Register:** Interested participants can sign up online through the registration link provided by parade organizers.

Community members interested in joining are encouraged to register soon to ensure their float entry is included in the parade lineup.
