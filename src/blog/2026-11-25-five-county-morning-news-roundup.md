## Union: East Union girls remain unbeaten

[East Union's girls beat Belmont 63–50](https://unionsportsms.com/2026/11/24/varsity-feature-east-union-belmont-girls-basketball-final-2026-11-24/) on Tuesday at the Hickory Flat Thanksgiving Tournament, Union County Sports reported. It was the team's sixth win in six games and its highest point total so far this season. The team is scheduled to open district play at Myrtle on Monday, Nov. 30.

## Prentiss: Northeast wins both home games

Northeast Mississippi Community College's women [beat Arkansas State Mid-South 68–58](https://prentissnews.com/prentiss-county-news/northeast-sweeps-arkansas-state-mid-south-in-booneville-doubleheader-c457041e069f/) Tuesday in Booneville, and the men followed with an 84–80 comeback win, Prentiss County News reported, citing the college's athletics department. The men trailed 46–34 at halftime before rallying.

## Alcorn: Alcorn Central wins again

[Alcorn Central's boys defeated Itawamba Agricultural 51–44](https://alcornsportsms.com/2026/11/24/alcorn-central-beats-itawamba-51-44-for-second-seven-point-win-in-two-days/) Tuesday, Alcorn County Sports reported. The Golden Bears led 31–24 at halftime and finished the two-day holiday stretch with consecutive seven-point wins.

## Tippah: Ripley boys hand West Union its first loss

[Ripley's boys beat West Union 63–45](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/walnut-sweeps-hickory-flat-tournament/) Tuesday at the New Albany tournament, Tippah News reported from the posted final score. The result ended West Union's seven-game winning streak. Scores entered by schools and volunteers can be revised. Tuesday's Walnut and Falkner results were covered in [yesterday's evening roundup](/blog/north-mississippi-evening-roundup-november-24-2026/).

## Benton: County seeks disaster response volunteers

Benton County's Emergency Management Agency is forming a Community Emergency Response Team and taking volunteer sign-ups, [Tippah News reported Tuesday](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/benton-county-cert-volunteers/), following a [TVMO News Network report](https://tvmocommunitynews.substack.com/p/new-benton-county-community-emergency). Free training is planned for spring 2027; no class dates have been announced. Interested residents can sign up at the EMA office in the Ashland courthouse or call 662-224-4314. This announcement appeared Tuesday before the previous roundup and is included here as a newly verified Benton public-service item.
