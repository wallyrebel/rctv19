## Diversicare of Ripley Grilled Hot Dog Sale

Diversicare of Ripley is hosting a grilled hot dog sale on Friday, October 16, from 11 AM to 2 PM.

For $8, customers will receive a hot dog, chips, dessert, condiments, and a drink. All proceeds from the sale will go toward funding Christmas activities and gifts for Diversicare residents.

The community is invited to stop by, enjoy lunch, and support the cause.
