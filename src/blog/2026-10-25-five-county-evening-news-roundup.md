Sunday evening's five-county check found new school and public-safety notices in Alcorn County, a workforce education grant in Union County, and recent student career visits in Prentiss County. Monday's times below are **announced plans**, not events that have already happened.

## Alcorn: School district plans 12:30 p.m. Monday dismissal

The **Alcorn School District plans to dismiss students at 12:30 p.m. Monday, Oct. 26**, [WTVA reported Saturday](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/24/alcorn-school-district-dismiss-students-early-monday-ahead-trumps-visit/), citing the district. The district said it expects road closures and traffic delays tied to President Donald Trump's scheduled campaign rally at Crossroads Arena in Corinth. Families should check district messages for any later transportation instructions.

[Alcorn County News published a Corinth Police Department parking notice](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-police-issue-parking-and-security-notice-for-monday-trump-rally/) Saturday. Police told rally visitors to park only in authorized areas and warned that vehicles blocking roads or driveways or violating posted restrictions may be towed at the owner's expense. The notice also says weapons are prohibited at the arena. The rally is scheduled for Monday; these are advance traffic and entry instructions, not a report from the event.

## Union: District receives $45,000 for career exploration

The **Union County School District received a $45,000 grant** from the Toyota Wellspring Education Fund through CREATE!, [Union County News reported Thursday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/union-county-school-district-receives-45000-grant-for-workforce-development/), citing a district announcement. The district said the money will support workforce development and career exploration for kindergarten through eighth-grade students. The announcement did not give a timetable or a school-by-school spending plan.

## Prentiss: Baldwyn students visit workplaces

[Prentiss County News reported Friday](https://prentissnews.com/prentiss-county-news/baldwyn-students-shadow-media-health-care-insurance-and-aviation-jobs-75731786e08a/) that Baldwyn High School students took part in job-shadowing visits during the week. The school's and Northeast Mississippi Community College's posts described visits in media, health care, insurance and aviation. The report describes career exposure; it does not announce a new training program or job placements.

No separate meaningful new **Benton or Tippah County** development with enough verified detail was found for this edition. Recent Tippah festival sponsorship posts are already available as individual RCTV 19 articles, and older Benton event notices did not establish a new outcome.
