# Ripley Receives $100,000 Grant to Launch Downtown Maker Space

Ripley has been selected as one of six communities nationwide to receive funding through The Hartford Small Business Accelerator Grant Program, securing $100,000 to support economic development and entrepreneurship in the downtown area.

## Brown & Covington Takes Shape

The grant will fund the creation of Brown & Covington, an innovative downtown space designed to support local makers, entrepreneurs, and provide workforce training opportunities. During its inaugural year, the venue will feature and support six artisans from the Ripley community, creating a vibrant hub for creative professionals and small business owners.

## National Recognition for Local Vision

Being selected as one of only six communities nationwide for this competitive grant program represents significant recognition for Ripley's commitment to downtown revitalization and supporting local entrepreneurs. The award underscores the potential of the Brown & Covington project to serve as a catalyst for economic growth and community development.

## Community and Partner Support

Ripley Main Street expressed gratitude to several key partners in making this opportunity possible. The Mississippi Main Street Association nominated Ripley for the grant, while Main Street America and The Hartford provided the funding and support necessary to bring the project to life.

"We are honored to be one of six communities nationwide selected for funding," organizers stated, emphasizing the collaborative effort that led to the successful grant application.

## Looking Forward

As Brown & Covington continues to develop, Ripley Main Street has promised to share updates on the project's progress. The space is expected to become a cornerstone of downtown revitalization efforts, fostering entrepreneurship and supporting the creative economy in the community.

For more information about the Hartford Small Business Accelerator Grant Program and the six selected communities, visit Main Street America's official announcement.
