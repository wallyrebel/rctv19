Chula's restaurant is moving forward with opening a new location in Ashland. The business previously operated at The Cut-Off before deciding to expand into its own dedicated space.

The restaurant's growth from its time at The Cut-Off to establishing an independent location marks a significant milestone for the business. Community members are encouraging residents to follow along and support Chula's as it launches its new Ashland location.
