# Ripley Masonic Lodge Jeep Show This Saturday

Jeep enthusiasts are invited to bring their vehicles to the Ripley Masonic Lodge Jeep Show on Saturday, October 17, from 10 AM–2 PM on Line Street.

The family-friendly event will feature food, fellowship, and a Best of Show trophy for the winning vehicle. Entry fees are $25, or $20 for those who bring a canned good donation.

All proceeds from the event will benefit the Good Samaritan of Ripley and the Ripley Masonic Lodge.
