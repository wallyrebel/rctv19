Walnut beat Strayhorn 64–49 in boys varsity basketball Friday, November 20, according to MaxPreps.

Walnut led 23–4 after the first quarter and 32–23 at halftime. Walnut scored 15 points in the fourth quarter while Strayhorn scored 11.

Strayhorn's loss followed a 68–28 defeat to Byers on November 13. Walnut came into Friday's game having beaten Hickory Flat 55–43 on November 13 and lost to Nettleton 76–61 on November 17.
