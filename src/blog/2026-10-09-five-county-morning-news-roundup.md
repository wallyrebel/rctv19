Friday morning's edition covers developments verified after Thursday's evening roundup. Times are Central. Forecasts and event plans may change.

## All five counties: Updated Isaias outlook

The [National Weather Service in Memphis updated its Isaias graphic at 5:06 a.m. Friday](https://www.weather.gov/meg/). It expects the storm's remnants to bring periods of heavy rain and strong wind to the **broader Mid-South on Saturday**, with sustained winds of 20 to 25 mph, gusts up to 40 mph, and 1 to 3 inches of rain with isolated totals up to 4 inches. These are regional forecasts, not measured conditions or a guaranteed total for each county. The forecast remains uncertain.

The [Flood Watch issued Thursday remains in effect](https://www.weather.gov/wwamap/wwatxtget.php?cwa=meg&wwa=all) for **Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah** from 1 a.m. Saturday to 7 a.m. Sunday. A watch means flooding is possible. The [National Hurricane Center's 7 a.m. Friday advisory](https://prod-east-nhc.woc.noaa.gov/mobile/text/refresh/MIATCPAT4%2Bhtml/090857.shtml) put Isaias's maximum sustained winds at 110 mph over the Gulf. That is the hurricane's intensity at its center, **not** a wind forecast for north Mississippi. Check [current NWS Memphis alerts](https://www.weather.gov/meg/) before travel this weekend.

## Union and Tippah counties: Volleyball results set North Half matchups

New Albany swept Ripley **3-0** in Thursday's Class 4A playoff, with sets of 25-10, 25-22 and 25-18, according to the [MaxPreps match recap](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/f4j6_rDOI0uAEx3qPMAySQ/volleyball-recap-ripley-falls,-snapping-its-four-game-win-streak.htm). Ripley's postseason ended. [WTVA reports](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/09/tupelo-christian-ingomar-new-albany-east-union-among-local-teams-headed-north-half-volleyball-championships/) New Albany is scheduled to visit Mooreville for the Class 4A North Half title match Monday.

Walnut swept Myrtle **3-0** Thursday, [MaxPreps reports](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/WqR8f6S05UOzGEA_uTyVYA/volleyball-recap-walnuts-victory-on-thursday-extends-winning-streak-to-12.htm). [WTVA lists](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/09/tupelo-christian-ingomar-new-albany-east-union-among-local-teams-headed-north-half-volleyball-championships/) an all-local Class 2A North Half final Monday: **East Union hosts Walnut**. The report does not provide a set score for East Union's Thursday match, so this edition does not assign one.

## Alcorn County: Kossuth advances

Kossuth defeated fellow Alcorn County team Alcorn Central **3-0** Thursday, with sets of 25-12, 25-14 and 25-10, according to [MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/UFQS-II4n0e1-nqcaetRQA/volleyball-recap-kossuth-rolls-to-its-21st-straight-win.htm). [WTVA says](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/09/tupelo-christian-ingomar-new-albany-east-union-among-local-teams-headed-north-half-volleyball-championships/) Kossuth is scheduled to host Belmont in Monday's Class 3A North Half title match. Alcorn Central's playoff run ended with Thursday's loss.

## Prentiss County: Booneville falls; Northeast wins

Booneville's volleyball team lost a five-set playoff match to Belmont **3-2** Thursday. The [MaxPreps recap](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/Bdk3pxT0eU2hB6mey4FdEw/volleyball-recap-booneville-pushes-belmont-to-the-limit.htm) lists set scores of 18-25, 25-23, 19-25, 27-25 and 15-13 from Belmont's perspective; the deciding set was settled by two points. Booneville's postseason ended.

In college football, Northeast Mississippi Community College beat Mississippi Delta **35-17** Thursday in Booneville, [WTVA reported overnight](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/09/tupelo-christian-ingomar-new-albany-east-union-among-local-teams-headed-north-half-volleyball-championships/).
