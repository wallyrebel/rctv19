New reporting Tuesday described construction progress at a Corinth recovery campus, Monday's Ripley soccer results and an upcoming clothing ministry opening in Ripley.

## Alcorn: Freedom Center campus adds chapel and cottages

The chapel and multipurpose building at the Freedom Center's new campus off Central School Road is complete, and three residential cottages are under construction, [Alcorn County News reported Tuesday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-freedom-center-campus/), citing the Daily Corinthian. Living Free Ministries, which runs the residential recovery program, aims to raise $750,000 by the end of the year to finish the cottages and other campus work. The report says the ministry plans to turn its current building into transitional housing for graduates. The goal is a fundraising target, not money already raised.

## Tippah: Ripley soccer teams split season openers

Ripley's boys beat Caledonia **3–2** on Monday, Oct. 26, while the Ripley girls lost their opener to Caledonia **6–0**, [Tippah County Sports reported Tuesday](https://tippahsports.com/ripley/ripley-boys-edge-caledonia-3-2-in-soccer-opener-girls-fall-6-0/). Both teams are scheduled to visit Pontotoc on Nov. 3. These are results from Monday's games, following an earlier schedule preview.

## Tippah: Clothing ministry sets Nov. 5 grand opening

Clothed in Grace, a clothing ministry of First Baptist Church Ripley, plans an [official grand opening on Thursday, Nov. 5](https://tippahnews.com/ripley-ms-news/clothed-in-grace-ripley-grand-opening/) at 402 Siddall St. Tippah News, citing Ripley Main Street's announcement, lists a reception from 10:30 to 11:30 a.m. and shopping from 2 to 6 p.m. The ministry has already had a soft opening; Nov. 5 is its formal welcome.

No separate meaningful new **Union, Prentiss or Benton County** development with enough verified detail was found for this edition. The morning roundup's Corinth rally recap and New Albany art notice are not repeated.
