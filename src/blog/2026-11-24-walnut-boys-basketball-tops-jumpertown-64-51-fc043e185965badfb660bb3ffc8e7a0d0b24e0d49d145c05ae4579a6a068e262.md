Walnut beat Jumpertown 64–51 in boys varsity basketball Tuesday, November 24, according to the teams' final box score on MaxPreps.

Walnut trailed 17–15 after the first quarter but took a 35–28 halftime lead. The home team pulled away in the fourth quarter, scoring 15 points to Jumpertown's 11.

Jumpertown entered the game having beaten Ashland 70–47 on November 20 and lost to Hatley 57–51 on November 23. Walnut came in off wins against Strayhorn (64–49 on November 20) and Belmont (53–50 on November 23).
