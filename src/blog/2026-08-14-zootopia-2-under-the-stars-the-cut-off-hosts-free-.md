# Free Movie Night Comes to Downtown Ripley Tonight

The Cut-Off is rolling out the red carpet for a special outdoor movie event happening tonight as the sun sets over downtown Ripley. The venue will screen **Zootopia 2** under the stars, offering families and community members a chance to enjoy entertainment in the heart of the city.

## What to Expect

The movie experience kicks off at dusk, giving attendees the perfect opportunity to settle in with blankets and chairs for a comfortable viewing. Organizers are encouraging guests to bring their own seating and invite the whole family for what promises to be a memorable evening.

## Food and Entertainment

The event is more than just a movie—it's a full downtown experience. **Chula's Food Truck** will be on-site throughout the day, while **A6 Seafood and Funky Cones** will set up starting at 3:00 PM. This early setup gives attendees plenty of time to grab dinner and snacks before settling in for the film.

## Location and Details

- **Where:** 204 South Commerce Street, Ripley
- **When:** Tonight at dusk
- **What to bring:** Chairs, blankets, and family members
- **Cost:** Free admission

## Community Support

The evening is made possible through the generous sponsorship of **Rebel Services LLC**, demonstrating local business commitment to community entertainment and downtown revitalization.

Organizers are urging residents to arrive early to take full advantage of the food vendors and the vibrant downtown atmosphere before the main event begins.
