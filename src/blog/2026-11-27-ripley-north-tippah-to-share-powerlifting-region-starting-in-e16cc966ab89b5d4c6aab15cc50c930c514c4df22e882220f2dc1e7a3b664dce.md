Ripley and North Tippah will compete in the same powerlifting region beginning in the 2027-28 school year under the Mississippi High School Activities Association's 2027-29 alignment.

The schools will join Class 3A Region 1 alongside Alcorn Central, Belmont, Holly Springs, Independence, Kossuth, Nettleton and North Panola for both boys and girls competition.

Ripley is currently in Class 4A Region 1, a 10-school region that includes Amory, Corinth, Houston, Itawamba Agricultural, Mooreville, New Albany, North Pontotoc, Shannon and Tishomingo County. That alignment remains in effect through the 2026-27 school year.
