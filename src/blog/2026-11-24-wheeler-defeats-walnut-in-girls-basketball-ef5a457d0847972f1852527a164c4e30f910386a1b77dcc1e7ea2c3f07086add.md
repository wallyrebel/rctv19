Wheeler defeated Walnut 64–54 in girls varsity basketball Monday, November 23, according to MaxPreps.

Walnut entered the game with consecutive wins: a 69–37 victory over Nettleton on November 17 and a 64–14 win against Strayhorn on November 20.

Wheeler came into the matchup following a 63–33 win over Itawamba Agricultural on November 17, after a 43–40 loss to Ashland on November 16.
