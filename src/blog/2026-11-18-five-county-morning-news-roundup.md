## Union: Christmas parade entries close today

Registration for the New Albany and Union County Christmas Parade closes at 4 p.m. Wednesday, Nov. 18, [Union County News reported](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/registration-for-new-albany-christmas-parade-closes-wednesday/), citing New Albany Main Street. Organizers said space is limited and entries could close earlier if the parade reaches capacity. The parade is scheduled for Nov. 30 at 6:30 p.m. downtown.

## Alcorn: library trustees to meet Thursday

The Northeast Regional Library Board of Trustees is scheduled to meet Thursday, Nov. 19, in the Corinth Public Library auditorium, according to the [library's notice](https://www.nereg.lib.ms.us/) and [Alcorn County News](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/library-trustees-meet-in-corinth-nov-19-ahead-of-thanksgiving-closures/). The notice does not give a start time. The library also says all its branches will close Nov. 26 and 27 for Thanksgiving.

## Benton: Hickory Flat basketball player earns weekly honor

Hickory Flat junior Abby Rowland was voted the Northeast Mississippi Daily Journal's female Athlete of the Week for Nov. 9–14, [Tippah News reported Tuesday evening](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/hickory-flat-rowland-athlete-of-week/). The report, citing the Daily Journal, says Rowland averaged 15.7 points and 12 rebounds in three games for the Lady Rebels. Hickory Flat is in Benton County.

## Tippah: Blue Mountain Christian forward wins national award

Blue Mountain Christian senior Terrence Marco was named the NAIA men's basketball national Defensive Player of the Week on Tuesday. The [NAIA announcement](https://www.naia.org/news/featured/marco-named-prestosports-naia-mens-basketball-national-defensive-player-of-the-week/) says he blocked 14 shots across two games against Stillman and Faulkner. The national honor follows a conference award reported earlier this week.
