Ripley won the MHSAA Class 4A Boys Golf Championship with a team score of 642, 66 over par, according to the Mississippi High School Activities Association. The championship was held Oct. 26 at Dancing Rabbit Golf Club in Choctaw.

Spencer Kirk of Tishomingo County won the individual championship with a score of 147, 3 over par.
