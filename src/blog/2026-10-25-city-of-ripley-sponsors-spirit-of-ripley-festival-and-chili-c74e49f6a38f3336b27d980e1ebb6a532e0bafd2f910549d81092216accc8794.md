The City of Ripley is recognized as a sponsor of the Spirit of Ripley Festival & Chili Cookoff.

**Event Details:**
- **Date & Time:** Friday, October 30th at 5:30 PM
- **Location:** Historic Downtown Ripley

The festival brings the community together for fall festivities and competitive chili entries in the heart of downtown.
