## Tippah: Ripley Middle receives teacher-support grant

Ripley Middle School announced Thursday that Leslie Gaines received a 2026–27 Mississippi Professional Educators Administrator School Grant, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/ripley-ms-news/ripley-middle-mpe-school-grant/). The school said it plans to use the funding for teacher incentives and professional development. The announcement did not state the award amount. [MPE's program guidelines](https://cdn.ymaws.com/www.mpe.org/resource/resmgr/pdf/2026/2026.administrator_school_gr.pdf) allow administrator grants of up to $1,000; that program cap is not a confirmed amount for this award.

## Tippah: Falkner senior visits county courts

Falkner High School senior Kaliegh Morrison spent Thursday, Nov. 19, shadowing Ripley attorney Sean Akins in Tippah County's Chancery and Circuit courts, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/falkner-high-courtroom-job-shadow/). The school described the visit as part of its career exploration program. Morrison attended hearings and learned about legal work firsthand.

No distinct, meaningful new development was verified for Union, Prentiss, Alcorn or Benton counties for this edition.
