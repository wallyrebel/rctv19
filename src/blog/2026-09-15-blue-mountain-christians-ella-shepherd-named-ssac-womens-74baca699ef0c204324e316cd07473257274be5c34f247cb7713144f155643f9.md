Sophomore Ella Shepherd of Blue Mountain Christian has been named SSAC Women's Cross Country Runner of the Week following her performance at the Harding Invitational. Shepherd led the Blue Mountain Christian cross country team at the event, earning the weekly conference recognition for her effort.
