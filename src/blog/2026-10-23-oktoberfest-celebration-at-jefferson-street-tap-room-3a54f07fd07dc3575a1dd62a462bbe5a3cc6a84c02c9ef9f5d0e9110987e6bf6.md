## Oktoberfest at Jefferson Street Tap Room

Jefferson Street Tap Room will host an Oktoberfest celebration **Saturday, October 24 from 5:00 to 10:00 PM** on the square in downtown Ripley.

The event will feature traditional Oktoberfest fare including beer, brats, and pretzels, along with cornhole games for attendees. Live music will be provided by DJ Chase Lero and Alex Schofield of The Two Kays.

Weather conditions are expected to be ideal for the outdoor event, with sunny skies and a high of 78 degrees.

**Location:** 120 E Jefferson St, Ripley, MS
