# Whimsey On Wheels Returns to The Cut-Off

Food enthusiasts have reason to celebrate as Whimsey On Wheels makes its anticipated return to The Cut-Off for a special one-day pop-up event.

## Event Details

The beloved food truck will be stationed at The Cut-Off on **Tuesday, August 18th from 11 AM to 6 PM**, offering customers an opportunity to enjoy their full menu throughout the day.

**Location:** 204 South Commerce Street

## What to Expect

Visitors can stop by at their convenience to grab lunch or dinner from Whimsey On Wheels' delicious offerings. Whether you're looking for a midday meal or an evening bite, the food truck will be serving throughout the entire 7-hour window.

The venue has expressed enthusiasm about the return of this popular food vendor, making this a must-visit event for fans of quality food truck cuisine in the area.
