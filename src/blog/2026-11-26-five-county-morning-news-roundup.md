## Tippah: Recovery home planned in Blue Mountain

Planting Seeds Recovery Center says it is preparing a faith-based sober living home for women at 611A County Road 812 in Blue Mountain, [Tippah News reported Wednesday evening](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/blue-mountain-recovery-center-women/). The ministry has **not announced an opening date, capacity or application process**. Its November wish list includes trash bags through Nov. 30 and two large rolling garbage cans; the linked report gives contact and drop-off details.

## Prentiss: Booneville player earns volleyball honor

Booneville junior Hailee Lawless was named to the Mississippi Association of Coaches' 2027 North volleyball all-star roster, [Prentiss County News reported](https://prentissnews.com/prentiss-county-news/boonevilles-hailee-lawless-selected-for-mississippi-association-of-coaches-north-volleyball-roster-4db1ef00d0fe/). The [association lists](https://www.mscoaches.com/sports/volleyball) the all-star game for July 14 at Brandon High School. This recognition was newly verified for this edition.

## Alcorn and Prentiss: Court e-filing outage ahead

Mississippi Electronic Courts plans to take trial-court e-filing and online record access offline from **5 p.m. Dec. 23 until 8 a.m. Jan. 4**, [Alcorn County News reported](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/alcorn-county-court-e-filing-offline/). The statewide shutdown includes Alcorn and Prentiss courts. Clerks are to accept paper filings during business hours, and filing deadlines remain in force, according to the report. This is an advance notice newly verified for this roundup, not an outage happening today.

## Union and Benton

No separate meaningful new development was verified for Union or Benton counties for this edition.
