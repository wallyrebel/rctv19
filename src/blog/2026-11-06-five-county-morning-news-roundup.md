## Tippah: Walnut-area power outage planned tonight

Tippah Electric Power Association has scheduled an outage beginning **around 11 p.m. Friday, Nov. 6** while crews repair a switch at the Walnut substation, [Tippah News reports](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/tepa-scheduled-power-outage-walnut-friday-nov-6/). It could last up to four hours. The report identifies 72 East, 72 West, Chalybeate and Walnut South as affected areas; power is expected to remain on in Walnut's city center. Residents should check the utility's latest notices before relying on the schedule.

## Prentiss: Booneville-Baldwyn school merger proposed

The Booneville and Baldwyn school districts are pursuing a merger of their administrations. [Baldwyn School District said Thursday](https://baldwynschools.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Booneville-Baldwyn-Merger-Press-Statement.pdf) that a consolidation bill will be introduced in the 2027 legislative session. Under the proposal, both communities would keep their campuses and school identities. The district says school taxes are not expected to increase as a result of the merger. Legislative action is still needed; this is a proposal, not a completed consolidation.

## Alcorn: Corinth aldermen select mayor pro tem

At their first business meeting of the new term Tuesday, Corinth aldermen selected Alderman Michael McFall as mayor pro tem and renewed employee health coverage, [Alcorn County News reports](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-aldermen-mayor-pro-tem/), citing the *Daily Corinthian*. The board also approved additional costs for North Polk Street work and drainage work in the Coke area. The local report says the drainage increase followed the discovery of sewer lines missing from city maps.

## Union: New Albany business plans downtown location

Root and Branch is preparing to open at **137 W. Bankhead** in downtown New Albany and will join the New Albany Main Street Association as a partner, [Union County News reports](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/root-and-branch-to-open-in-downtown-new-albany/), citing the association. No opening date was announced.
