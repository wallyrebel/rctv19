The City of Ripley Christmas Parade will take place Tuesday, December 8, with organizers calling for widespread community participation.

The parade theme is "Christmas Through the Decades." Participants can decorate a trailer, golf cart, classic car, truck, or create any unique entry representing their chosen decade. Organizers note that elaborate floats are not required.

Entry is free, and registration is still open through the Ripley Main Street campaign page. Businesses, churches, schools, sports teams, families and community groups are encouraged to participate and tag organizations they'd like to see in the parade.
