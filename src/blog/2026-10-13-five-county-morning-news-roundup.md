Monday night's North Half volleyball finals produced four state-title berths for schools in this five-county area. [WTVA reported after the matches](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/13/ingomar-east-union-kossuth-new-albany-new-hope-are-bound-mhsaa-state-volleyball-championships/) that Ingomar, East Union, Kossuth and New Albany each won in three sets. These are results from **Monday, Oct. 12**; the state finals are still ahead. All times below are Central.

## Union and Tippah: East Union advances; Walnut's run ends

East Union swept Walnut **3–0** in the Class 2A North Half final, according to [WTVA's winners report](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/13/ingomar-east-union-kossuth-new-albany-new-hope-are-bound-mhsaa-state-volleyball-championships/) and [Tippah News' identification of Monday's opponents](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/walnut-volleyball-north-state-east-union/). East Union is scheduled to face Enterprise in Clinton **Thursday, Oct. 15, at noon**. Walnut, the defending state champion, does not advance. Neither linked report provides set-by-set scores for this match.

## Union: Ingomar and New Albany also secure berths

Ingomar swept Tupelo Christian Preparatory School **3–0** in Class 1A. [WTVA reported](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/13/ingomar-east-union-kossuth-new-albany-new-hope-are-bound-mhsaa-state-volleyball-championships/) that Ingomar won a close first set **29–27** and will play Resurrection Catholic **Friday, Oct. 16, at noon** in Clinton. The station described this as Ingomar's fifth straight state-title appearance.

New Albany swept Mooreville **3–0** in Class 4A and is scheduled to play Choctaw Central **Friday at 2 p.m.**, [WTVA reported](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/13/ingomar-east-union-kossuth-new-albany-new-hope-are-bound-mhsaa-state-volleyball-championships/). A [MaxPreps match recap](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/OZR5ZrlGKkS52G1V9mlzZQ/volleyball-recap-new-albany-picks-up-seventh-consecutive-win.htm), generated from entered match data, lists New Albany's set scores as **25–22, 25–13, 25–19**.

## Alcorn: Kossuth reaches Class 3A final

Kossuth swept Belmont **3–0** in the Class 3A North Half final, [WTVA reported](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/13/ingomar-east-union-kossuth-new-albany-new-hope-are-bound-mhsaa-state-volleyball-championships/). The Aggies are scheduled to meet Our Lady Academy in Clinton **Friday at 10 a.m.** The report does not provide individual set scores.

No separate, meaningful new **Prentiss** or **Benton** county development was verified for this morning's edition.
