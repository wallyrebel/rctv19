# Big Tae Eats Brings Smash Burgers to Ripley Main Street Tonight

Ripley Main Street is buzzing with excitement as Big Tae Eats prepares to serve up mouth-watering smash burgers this evening at a popular local venue.

## Event Details

The food vendor will be operating at **The Cut-Off**, located at **204 South Commerce Street**, beginning at **5 PM today**. The service will continue until all items are sold out, making this a first-come, first-served opportunity for burger enthusiasts.

## What to Expect

Big Tae Eats has earned a reputation for quality smash burgers, and tonight's pop-up is expected to draw a crowd. Attendees are advised to come with an appetite and arrive early to ensure they don't miss out on the limited inventory.

The event represents another exciting dining opportunity for the Ripley community, showcasing the area's growing food scene and the appeal of local food vendors.

**Mark your calendars and head to The Cut-Off this evening for fresh, delicious smash burgers!**
