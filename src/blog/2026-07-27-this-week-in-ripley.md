## Heat & Wellness Spa Launches Back-to-School Drive to Support Local Students and Teachers

*July 27, 2026*

<img src="/assets/optimized/cff339eafe6d406bc15e-960.webp" srcset="/assets/optimized/cff339eafe6d406bc15e-480.webp 480w, /assets/optimized/cff339eafe6d406bc15e-960.webp 960w" sizes="(max-width: 960px) 100vw, 900px" alt="Heat &amp; Wellness Spa Launches Back-to-School Drive to Support Local Students and Teachers" loading="lazy">

In a heartwarming effort to support the local community, Heat & Wellness Spa is organizing a Back-to-School Drive aimed at helping students and educators prepare for the upcoming school year.

### Event Details and Activities

The initiative will take place on August 1st at the spa's location on 608 City Ave. South in Ripley, Mississippi. The event combines fundraising with community support through multiple offerings:

**Fundraising Component:** Heat & Wellness Spa will sell snow cones, with proceeds directed toward purchasing essential school supplies for students in need.

**Community Giveaway:** The spa is providing free hot dogs and drinks to attendees while supplies last, creating an accessible community gathering space.

### How to Contribute

The spa is actively accepting donations of:
- Backpacks
- School supplies
- Shoes
- Children's clothing
- Socks
- Kid's tumblers
- Kleenex and other school essentials

Individuals who wish to donate items or know children in need of assistance are encouraged to contact Heat & Wellness Spa directly at 662-502-2907.

### Community Impact

The back-to-school initiative represents a significant community commitment, recognizing that school readiness extends beyond academics to include having proper supplies and clothing. By combining fundraising efforts with direct donations and community engagement, Heat & Wellness Spa is working to ensure that local students can begin the school year with confidence and the necessary tools for success.

Community members are encouraged to participate, whether through donations, attendance at the August 1st event, or by spreading awareness about this important local initiative.

## Rib Shack Marks One Year of Smoky Success in Ripley

*July 27, 2026*

<img src="/assets/optimized/f73bb9b1d7ae55de392d-960.webp" srcset="/assets/optimized/f73bb9b1d7ae55de392d-480.webp 480w, /assets/optimized/f73bb9b1d7ae55de392d-960.webp 960w" sizes="(max-width: 960px) 100vw, 900px" alt="Rib Shack Marks One Year of Smoky Success in Ripley" loading="lazy">

Rib Shack, the community's favorite barbecue destination, is marking an important milestone as it celebrates one full year of operations in Ripley.

Since opening its doors, the restaurant has become a beloved gathering spot for barbecue enthusiasts and families looking for quality smoked meats and traditional comfort food. The anniversary celebration reflects the strong support the establishment has received from local residents and the broader community.

The achievement represents more than just a year in business—it demonstrates the restaurant's commitment to serving quality food and maintaining the welcoming atmosphere that has drawn customers through its doors repeatedly.

As Rib Shack looks toward its future in Ripley, the one-year mark serves as a testament to the hard work of the team and the loyalty of its patrons who have made the restaurant's first year a success.
