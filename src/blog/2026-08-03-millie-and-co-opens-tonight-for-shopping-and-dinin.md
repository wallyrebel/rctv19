## Local Boutique and Eatery Team Up for Evening of Shopping and Dining

Millie & Co is throwing open its doors tonight for an evening of shopping and entertainment in the heart of downtown. The retail destination will be open starting at 5:00 PM, offering customers the perfect opportunity to browse while enjoying quality food from Big Tae's Eats.

### Event Details

The collaborative event takes place at **The Cut-Off**, located at **204 South Commerce Street**. Organizers are encouraging shoppers to arrive hungry and settle in for an extended visit, combining retail browsing with culinary delights.

### What to Expect

Attendees can look forward to:
- **Great food** from Big Tae's Eats
- **Fun shopping** at Millie & Co
- **Good times** in the downtown area

Whether you're looking to pick up something special or simply enjoy a casual evening out, tonight's event offers the best of both worlds. The pairing of a local eatery with a boutique shopping experience creates an inviting atmosphere for community members seeking an enjoyable night downtown.

Come hungry and stay awhile—Millie & Co and Big Tae's Eats are ready to welcome you starting at 5:00 PM tonight.
