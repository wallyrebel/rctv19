## Tippah: Three Tuesday basketball wins reported

In Tuesday's holiday basketball games, [Walnut's boys beat Jumpertown 64–51](https://tippahsports.com/tippah-county-sports/walnut-boys-beat-jumpertown-64-51/), [Walnut's girls beat Lafayette 61–26](https://tippahsports.com/tippah-county-sports/walnut-girls-beat-lafayette-61-26/), and [Falkner's girls beat Jumpertown 58–19](https://tippahsports.com/tippah-county-sports/falkner-girls-beat-jumpertown-58-19/), Tippah County Sports reported from final box scores on MaxPreps. Walnut's boys trailed 17–15 after one quarter but led 35–28 at halftime. Falkner's girls led 37–11 at halftime. Scores entered on MaxPreps can be revised.

## Tippah: Downtown Ripley shop crawl set for Saturday

In a [Monday evening announcement](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/ripley-shop-crawl-jingle-mingle/), Ripley Main Street set its **Jingle and Mingle** Shop Small Saturday crawl for Nov. 28. Shoppers can pick up a card at Brown & Covington, 301 S. Commerce St., on Friday or Saturday. The organizer's flyer says a purchase of $75 or more at a participating shop qualifies for the grand prize drawing; making a purchase at each participating shop and filling the card qualifies for a Main Street Moolah drawing. Check with [Ripley Main Street](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/ripley-shop-crawl-jingle-mingle/) for participating shops and any changes.

No separate meaningful new Union, Prentiss, Alcorn or Benton County development was verified for this edition.
