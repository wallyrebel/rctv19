## Tippah: Ripley and North Tippah placed in separate 3A regions

The [2027–29 MHSAA region assignments](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/mhsaa-regions-ripley-north-tippah/) put Ripley and North Tippah in Class 3A, but in different football and basketball regions beginning with the **2027–28 school year**. Ripley is in Region 1 with Alcorn Central, Belmont, Kossuth and Nettleton; North Tippah is in Region 2 with Holly Springs, Independence, North Panola and Rosa Fort. This season's teams remain under the current alignment.

Blue Mountain Christian University [opened its new Center for Grace and Healing Friday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/bmcu-center-grace-healing-opens/) with an 11:30 a.m. ribbon-cutting and tours starting at noon. The $15 million Blue Mountain building will house the School of Nursing along with science and mathematics programs, according to the local report citing the university. The report's picture is an illustrative simulation-lab image, not a photograph of the opening.

## Benton: Ashland and Hickory Flat share a future basketball region

The [new MHSAA alignment](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/mhsaa-regions-ripley-north-tippah/) places Ashland and Hickory Flat in Class 1A basketball Region 2 with Blue Mountain, Ingomar, Potts Camp and West Union for 2027–29. Ashland is separately assigned to football Region 1 with Biggersville, Potts Camp and Tupelo Christian. These are future assignments, not changes to the current season.

## Union: West Union placed in Class 1A Region 2

West Union is among the six schools in the [2027–29 Class 1A basketball Region 2 assignment](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/mhsaa-regions-ripley-north-tippah/), alongside Ashland, Blue Mountain, Hickory Flat, Ingomar and Potts Camp. No separate, meaningful Union County development after this morning's roundup was verified for this edition.

## Alcorn: New prosecutor; schools join Ripley's region

Corinth attorney Jeremy Hudson was sworn in as an assistant district attorney by Circuit Court Judge John R. White, [Alcorn County News reports](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-area-notices-dance-testing-aid-and-new-assistant-da-7bc13b783e0d/), citing the First Circuit District Attorney's Office. The office said he will work from Corinth and primarily serve Alcorn and Prentiss counties.

Alcorn Central and Kossuth are also assigned to Ripley's [2027–29 Class 3A Region 1](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/mhsaa-regions-ripley-north-tippah/) in football and basketball.

## Prentiss: Wheeler in new 1A basketball group

Wheeler is assigned to [2027–29 Class 1A basketball Region 1](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/mhsaa-regions-ripley-north-tippah/) with Pine Grove, Biggersville, Jumpertown, Thrasher and Tupelo Christian. The district attorney's office also said the newly sworn-in Jeremy Hudson will [primarily serve Prentiss and Alcorn counties](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-area-notices-dance-testing-aid-and-new-assistant-da-7bc13b783e0d/) from its Corinth office. We are not counting this morning's Booneville–Baldwyn merger proposal again as a new development.
