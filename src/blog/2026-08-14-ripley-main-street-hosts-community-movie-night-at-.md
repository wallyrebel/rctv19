# Community Gathers for Evening of Food and Film in Downtown Ripley

Ripley Main Street is inviting residents to enjoy a festive movie night this evening at The Cut-Off, located at 204 S. Commerce Street. The event promises a full evening of entertainment, dining, and outdoor entertainment for the entire community.

## What to Expect

Attendees are encouraged to arrive early to enjoy dinner and dessert from some of Ripley's favorite local establishments before the movie begins at dusk. The event will feature food options from three popular local vendors:

- **Chula's** - offering Mexican cuisine
- **A6 Seafood** - providing fresh seafood options
- **Funky Cones** - serving sweet treats and ice cream

## Come Prepared and Comfortable

Organizers are asking guests to bring their own chairs or blankets to ensure comfort during the outdoor screening. This creates an opportunity for families and friends to settle in and enjoy the film in a relaxed, community-oriented atmosphere.

## Event Details

**Location:** The Cut-Off, 204 S. Commerce Street
**Time:** Food service before dusk; movie begins at dusk
**Cost:** Not specified (likely free or minimal admission)

Ripley Main Street's movie night represents a continuation of community-building events designed to bring residents together and support local businesses. Whether you're looking for a casual evening out or a family-friendly activity, organizers are welcoming everyone to come hungry, stay for the movie, and enjoy a night of entertainment under the stars.
