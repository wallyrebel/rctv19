Saturday morning's five-county check includes one result from Friday night and local reports that were not in our Friday evening edition. The regional recovery council was announced Wednesday and reported Friday; its announcement did **not** occur overnight. Event times below are scheduled times, not reports that the events have already happened.

## Benton County: Ashland falls at Tupelo Christian Prep

Ashland lost **57–0** at Tupelo Christian Preparatory School on Friday night, according to [MaxPreps' postgame report](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/1in5Cft4zEOqE24sOlSImg/football-recap-tough-loss-for-ashland.htm). The [North Mississippi Sports Network's Ashland results page](https://www.watchnmsn.com/schools/ashland) also lists the 57–0 final. Ashland is scheduled to play at Walnut on Oct. 2.

## Prentiss and Alcorn counties: Regional recovery council launched

The CREATE Foundation and Tennessee Valley Authority have started a **Northeast Mississippi Disaster Recovery Council** to help local groups coordinate long-term recovery after disasters, [Alcorn County News reported Friday](https://alcornnewsms.com/mississippi-news/northeast-mississippi-disaster-recovery-council/). Leaders announced it Wednesday in Tupelo. CREATE and TVA each committed $100,000 to establish and support the effort. The report identifies CARE in Alcorn County and PROPEL in Prentiss County among local groups involved in recovery work after January's ice storm. The new council is intended to connect and assist local efforts across the region; the report does not say those two groups will each receive a specific share of the funding.

## Alcorn County: Hotel ribbon cutting set for Tuesday

The Alliance and Delta Lodging Group plan a public ribbon cutting for the new TownePlace Suites by Marriott in Corinth at **10 a.m. Tuesday, Sept. 29**, at 1901 South Parkway St., [Alcorn County News reported Friday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-towneplace-suites-ribbon-cutting/). The report says the hotel is already taking reservations. Tuesday's ceremony is still upcoming.

## Alcorn County: Youth production continues this weekend

The Corinth Dramatic Society's production of *Charlotte's Web* has performances scheduled at the Corinth Coliseum for **7 p.m. Saturday** and **2 p.m. Sunday**, according to [Alcorn County News' Friday report](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/charlottes-web-corinth-coliseum/). The report lists tickets at $10 for adults and $5 for students. Check the venue before traveling for any schedule or ticket changes.

## Union and Tippah counties

We found no separate meaningful, newly verified Union or Tippah County development for this morning's edition. Friday's roundup already covered the Saturday Crossroads Marching Classic and the New Albany Riverfest street closure; those remain upcoming events, not new announcements here.
