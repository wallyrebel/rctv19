The Holiday Open House scheduled for today, Sunday, November 1, will proceed as planned from 12:00 to 4:00 PM in Downtown Ripley and surrounding areas, with one adjustment due to weather.

Pop-up shops originally planned for outdoor locations will now be set up indoors at the Golden Bee. All other aspects of the event remain unchanged, with all 27 participating businesses open and ready to welcome shoppers.

Visitors are encouraged to shop the pop-ups at the Golden Bee and then visit the other participating businesses throughout downtown.
