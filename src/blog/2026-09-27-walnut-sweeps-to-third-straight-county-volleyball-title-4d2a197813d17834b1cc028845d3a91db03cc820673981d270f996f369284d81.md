Walnut volleyball team claimed their third straight Tippah County championship Saturday with a dominant performance, sweeping through the tournament without surrendering a single set.

The team defeated Ripley, Falkner, and host Pine Grove in their path to the title. Walnut's Rolison was named the tournament's Most Valuable Player for her performance throughout the competition.
