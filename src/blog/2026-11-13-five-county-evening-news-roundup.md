## Tippah: Blue Mountain Christian's season ends in four sets

Blue Mountain Christian University lost 3-1 to Loyola of Louisiana on Friday morning at the SSAC Volleyball Championship in Montgomery, Alabama, [BMCU Athletics reports](https://bmcusports.com/news/2026/11/13/womens-volleyball-volley-toppers-fall-to-loyola-in-day-two-of-the-ssac-championship.aspx). Blue Mountain won the second set 25-20, but Loyola took the first, third and fourth sets 25-19, 25-20 and 25-20. The university said the loss ended its 2026 season. Salice Speed had 12 kills and 13 digs; Abbi Williams had 18 digs. This is the result of the Friday match previewed in the morning roundup.

The [Town of Walnut's events page](https://www.walnut.ms/events) lists its Christmas parade for Dec. 4 at 7 p.m., with online registration or paper forms at Town Hall. It also lists the Color Me Cured 5K run and walk for Nov. 14; the event is untimed and benefits type 1 diabetes research. [Tippah News reported Friday](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/walnut-christmas-parade-2026/) that the town is encouraging parade sign-ups. These are upcoming events, not completed activities.

## Union: New Albany players receive volleyball honor

Sara Barton Ozbirn and Allie Keller were named to the 2026 MHSAA Volleyball All-State Tournament Team, [Union County News reported Thursday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/two-named-to-mhsaa-volleyball-all-state-tournament-team/), citing a team announcement. The two helped New Albany win the Class 4A state championship. This is a new individual honor following the previously reported team title.

## Prentiss: NEMCC quarterback earns national weekly award

Northeast Mississippi Community College freshman quarterback Xae Mayes was named NJCAA Offensive Player of the Week, [Alcorn County News reported Friday](https://alcornnewsms.com/mississippi-news/nemcc-football-mayes-njcaa-honor/), citing the college's Thursday announcement. The report says Mayes threw for 370 yards and three touchdowns and ran for 104 yards and another score in Northeast's 28-24 comeback win over Holmes. It also says his 16 touchdown passes set a school single-season record. NEMCC is based in Booneville.
