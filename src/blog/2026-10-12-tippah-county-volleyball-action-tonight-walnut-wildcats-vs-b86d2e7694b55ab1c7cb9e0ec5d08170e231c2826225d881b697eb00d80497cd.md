# Walnut at East Union: October 12 Volleyball Preview

Walnut's Lady Wildcats were scheduled to visit East Union High School in Blue Springs, Union County, for the North State volleyball final at 6 p.m. Central on Monday, October 12, 2026, according to Tippah County Sports.

This is an archived pre-match notice. The scheduled date has passed; this article does not report the match result.

**Correction, October 13, 2026:** The match was scheduled in Union County, not Tippah County.
