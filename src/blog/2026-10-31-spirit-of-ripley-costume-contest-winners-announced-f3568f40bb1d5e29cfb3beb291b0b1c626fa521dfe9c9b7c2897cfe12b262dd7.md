# Spirit of Ripley Costume Contest Winners Announced

Congratulations have been extended to all winners of the Spirit of Ripley costume contest. The event featured a variety of costumes, with organizers noting that every winner earned their placement.

Lindsey Davis & Associates CPA sponsored the costume contest. Photos from the event are expected to be shared soon.
