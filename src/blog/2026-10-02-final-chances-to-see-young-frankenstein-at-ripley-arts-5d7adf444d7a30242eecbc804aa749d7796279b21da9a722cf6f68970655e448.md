The Ripley Arts Council is reminding theater-goers that there are only four chances left to catch the comedy production of Young Frankenstein on stage.

Remaining performances are scheduled for:
- Thursday at 7:00 PM
- Friday at 7:00 PM
- Saturday at 7:00 PM
- Sunday at 2:00 PM

Tickets can be purchased online through the Stage on Main ticketing system.

The production is rated PG-13 and contains adult humor and material, so it is not recommended for young children. Organizers encourage patrons to gather friends and secure tickets soon, as seating is limited for the final shows.
