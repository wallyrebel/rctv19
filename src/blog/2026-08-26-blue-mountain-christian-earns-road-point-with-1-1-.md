# Blue Mountain Christian Earns Road Point with 1-1 Draw Against Freed-Hardeman

The Blue Mountain Christian men's soccer team secured a hard-fought draw on the road Tuesday evening, battling Freed-Hardeman University to a 1-1 stalemate in Henderson, Tennessee.

Both squads found their rhythm in the second half, with each team scoring once during the period. The evenly matched contest resulted in a split of the points, giving the Toppers a valuable road result against the Lions.

The draw marks a solid performance for Blue Mountain Christian in what proved to be a competitive matchup between the two programs. The road point will be an important addition to the team's overall record as they continue their season.
