# Spirit of Ripley Festival & Chili Cookoff This Friday

The Spirit of Ripley Festival & Chili Cookoff will take place in Historic Downtown Ripley on Friday, October 30th beginning at 5:30 PM.

## Event Schedule

- **5:30–6:15 PM** — Costume Contest registration (west side of the square)
- **6:00–7:00 PM** — Chili tasting & activities ($5 tasting tickets)
- **6:15 PM** — Ripley Middle School Cheer performance
- **6:30 PM** — Costume Contest on the square (all ages)
- **7:00 PM** — Pet Costume Contest at The Cut-Off ($5 per pet)
- **7:30 PM** — RHS Pep Rally on the square

## Additional Activities

The festival will also feature the chili cookoff, spirit booths, and raffle drawings every 30 minutes throughout the evening.
