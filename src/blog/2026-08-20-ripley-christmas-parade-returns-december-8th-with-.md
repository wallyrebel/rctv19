# Ripley Christmas Parade Set for December 8th with Nostalgic 'Christmas Through the Decades' Theme

The annual Ripley Christmas Parade is returning this year with a creative twist that will take spectators on a journey through time. The festive event is scheduled for **December 8th at 7:00 PM**, featuring this year's theme: **"Christmas Through the Decades."

## A Trip Down Memory Lane

Participants are invited to embrace the spirit of any era they choose, with the timeframe spanning from the 1920s all the way to the present day. This expansive approach offers parade-goers and float designers unlimited creative possibilities to reimagine holiday traditions from different periods in history.

## Call for Creative Entries

Organizers are encouraging residents and local organizations to start brainstorming their festive concepts now. Whether participants want to recreate the charm of a 1950s Christmas, the disco-era celebrations of the 1970s, or any other decade in between, they have the freedom to bring their vision to life.

"We can't wait to see what everyone comes up with!" organizers expressed in their announcement, signaling enthusiasm for the diverse and imaginative entries expected this year.

The Ripley Christmas Parade promises to be a memorable evening of holiday cheer, nostalgia, and community celebration. Those interested in participating should begin planning their decade-inspired floats and displays now.
