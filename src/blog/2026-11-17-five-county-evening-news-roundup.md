## Tippah: Blue Mountain students learn about forestry and fire safety

Mike Crabb of the Mississippi Forestry Commission spoke with Blue Mountain K12 School seniors about forestry careers and the agency's work, [Tippah News reported Tuesday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/blue-mountain-forestry-commission-visit/), citing the school. Crabb also visited kindergarten through second-grade students with Smokey Bear to discuss fire prevention and read a book. The school shared the visit Monday, Nov. 16; the report does not specify when the visit took place.
