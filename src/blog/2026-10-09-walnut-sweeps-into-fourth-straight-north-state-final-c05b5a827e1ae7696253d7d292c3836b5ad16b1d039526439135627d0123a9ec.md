Walnut secured a dominant three-set victory over Myrtle on Thursday, advancing to the North State final for the fourth consecutive year as the team continues its state-title defense.

The sweep keeps Walnut's championship aspirations on track as the program looks to maintain its status among the state's elite volleyball programs.
