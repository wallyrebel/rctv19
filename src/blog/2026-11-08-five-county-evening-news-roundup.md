A [Tippah News basketball roundup published Sunday at 7:38 a.m.](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-county-basketball-roundup-nov-8/) supplies the principal new five-county update since the morning edition. The games were played earlier in the week; these are newly reported results, not games played Sunday.

## Tippah: Walnut wins both Saturday games

Walnut's girls beat Strayhorn **77–12**, and its boys beat Tishomingo County **64–60** in games listed for Saturday, Nov. 7. The scores appear in the [Tippah News roundup](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-county-basketball-roundup-nov-8/) and the [North Mississippi Sports Network scoreboard](https://www.watchnmsn.com/scores). Tippah News also reports Blue Mountain's boys edged Calhoun City 55–52 in a Saturday game. The source calls Nov. 7 a Friday in one passage, but the 2026 calendar places Nov. 7 on Saturday.

## Benton: Hickory Flat takes two from Houlka

Hickory Flat swept Houlka in games listed for Nov. 6: **70–17 in the girls game** and **49–43 in the boys game**, according to [Tippah News](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-county-basketball-roundup-nov-8/). The boys result also appears on the [North Mississippi Sports Network scoreboard](https://www.watchnmsn.com/scores). Tippah News reports Ashland's boys lost 71–48 to Nettleton and its girls lost 75–16 to South Pontotoc on Nov. 7; both scores are also listed by North Mississippi Sports Network.

## Union, Prentiss and Alcorn

No distinct, meaningful new county development was verified for these counties since the morning roundup. Earlier holiday announcements and football results have already been covered.
