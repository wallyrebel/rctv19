Blue Mountain Christian's men's basketball team fell short in a close contest, losing 76-74 to Dalton State on Tuesday at Bandy Gym in Dalton, Georgia.

Jonathan Baker tied the game for the Toppers with 34 seconds left. On Dalton State’s final possession, MJ Smith rebounded a miss by Kyoya Sasaki and scored with two seconds on the clock. It was Smith’s second putback in the final minute.

KJ Anderson hit four first-half 3-pointers for Blue Mountain. His last one came with 58 seconds left in the half, before Seck Zongo scored to give the Toppers a 42–39 lead at the break. The lead changed hands 19 times, and the teams were tied eight times.

Smith finished with eight points and seven rebounds. Five of his rebounds came on the offensive end, including the one that settled the game.
