Hickory Flat defeated Falkner 57–38 in girls varsity basketball Monday, November 23, according to MaxPreps.

Falkner entered the game on a two-game winning streak, having beaten Ashland 56–29 on November 17 and Mantachie 68–48 on November 20.

Hickory Flat came into the matchup with mixed recent results, having lost to South Pontotoc 65–23 on November 16 before defeating Tupelo Christian Prep 55–13 on November 19.
