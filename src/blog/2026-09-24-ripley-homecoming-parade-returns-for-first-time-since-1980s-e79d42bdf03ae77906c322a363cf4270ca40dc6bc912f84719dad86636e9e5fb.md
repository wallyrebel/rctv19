# Ripley Homecoming Parade Returns for First Time Since 1980s

Ripley is celebrating the return of its Homecoming Parade tonight for the first time since the 1980s. The parade will begin at 6:30 p.m. near First Baptist Church and conclude near First Methodist Church.

Community members are encouraged to bring lawn chairs and wear black and gold to line the parade route and cheer on the Tigers. Following the parade, a community pep rally will take place at the Ripley High School football field at 7:30 p.m.
