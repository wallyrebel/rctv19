This Thursday evening, Sept. 24, roundup covers newly reported opportunities in Alcorn and Tippah counties. We did not find a meaningful, verified development to add for Union, Prentiss or Benton counties since the morning edition.

## Alcorn County: Health and safety fair seeks vendors

Alcorn County Emergency Management and the county's Community Emergency Response Team are inviting agencies, businesses and organizations to participate in their first Health & Safety Fair, [Alcorn County News reported Thursday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/vendors-wanted-for-first-annual-alcorn-county-health-safety-fair-on-oct-17/). The event is scheduled for **Saturday, Oct. 17, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m.** at Crossroads Arena. Organizers plan health, safety and emergency preparedness activities. Prospective vendors can contact George Argeropoulos at 816-985-4981 or geoargero@gmail.com, according to the report.

## Alcorn County: Free concert planned at Corinth battlefield center

Shiloh National Military Park and Visit Corinth plan a free concert on **Saturday, Oct. 3, at 5 p.m.** on the lawn of the Corinth Civil War Interpretive Center, 501 W. Linden St. Steve and Lisa Ball will perform Civil War era music and stories, followed by bluegrass band Pride of the North Bank, according to the [National Park Service's Sept. 21 announcement](https://www.nps.gov/shil/learn/news/live-music-to-commemorate-164th-anniversary-of-battle-of-corinth.htm), which [Alcorn County News highlighted Thursday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/battle-of-corinth-anniversary-concert/). Visitors are encouraged to bring lawn chairs. This is a calendar item from an earlier announcement, not a newly announced event.

## Tippah County: School district seeks cafeteria substitutes

South Tippah School District is seeking cafeteria substitutes, [Tippah News reported Thursday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/south-tippah-cafeteria-substitutes/), based on a district notice posted Sept. 22. The notice lists 662-837-8779 and gravesa@stsd.ms for inquiries. The district also provides a [non-certified employment application](https://southtippahschools.com/join-our-team/) through its website. The notice does not state pay, hours or an application deadline; applicants should ask the district for those details.
