Falkner beat Jumpertown 58–19 in girls varsity basketball Tuesday, November 24, according to MaxPreps.

Falkner led 20–7 after the first quarter and extended its advantage to 37–11 by halftime. The fourth quarter saw Falkner score 7 points and Jumpertown score 6.

Jumpertown entered the game having lost to Ashland 42–32 on November 20 and to Byers 50–25 on November 23. Falkner had defeated Mantachie 68–48 on November 20 before losing to Hickory Flat 57–38 on November 23.
