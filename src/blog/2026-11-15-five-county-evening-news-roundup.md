## Alcorn and Prentiss: Shoebox collection begins Monday

Operation Christmas Child's National Collection Week runs Nov. 16–23, [Samaritan's Purse says](https://samaritanspurse.org/operation-christmas-child/drop-off-locations/). [Alcorn County News reported Sunday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-shoebox-drop-off-sites/) that Farmington Baptist Church, 84 County Road 106A near Corinth, will accept shoeboxes Monday from 9 a.m. to noon and 1 to 6 p.m. Gaston Baptist Church, 1908 Gaston Road in Booneville, is listed for Monday from 8 to 10 a.m. Each church has different hours on later days; check the organization's [location finder](https://samaritanspurse.org/operation-christmas-child/drop-off-locations/) before traveling.

## Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah: Deer gun season opens Saturday

The first gun season segment, which permits hunting with dogs, is scheduled for Nov. 21–Dec. 1 in all five counties under the [Mississippi Department of Wildlife, Fisheries, and Parks' 2026–27 calendar](https://www.mdwfp.com/wildlife-hunting/hunting-seasons-and-bag-limits). The current archery and early youth gun segments end Nov. 20. The agency places Alcorn, Benton and Tippah in the North Central Deer Management Unit; Union and Prentiss are in the Hills Unit. Bag limits and legal buck rules differ by unit, and public lands can have additional rules. Hunters should review the [full state regulations](https://www.mdwfp.com/wildlife-hunting/hunting-seasons-and-bag-limits/) before going out. The Alcorn opening was also [reported Sunday by Alcorn County News](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/deer-gun-season-opens-alcorn-county/).

No other distinct, meaningful new county development was verified for this edition.
