Friday night's first-round high school football playoff results provide this Saturday morning's new developments in Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties. These are **Nov. 6 final scores**, not games scheduled for today. The MaxPreps recaps cited below are generated from scores entered on that platform; we use them only for the posted finals.

## Union: New Albany wins its opener

New Albany defeated Rosa Fort **52–14** Friday, according to the [MaxPreps final](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/E3U2ztacYkGj9jgHYQEunQ/football-recap-new-albany-piles-up-the-points-against-rosa-fort.htm). The same scoreboard lists Louisville as New Albany's next opponent; check the school or tournament schedule for any change before traveling.

## Prentiss: Booneville advances

Booneville beat Yazoo County **13–0** in its first-round game, [MaxPreps' Booneville schedule](https://www.maxpreps.com/ms/booneville/booneville-blue-devils/football/schedule/) shows. The schedule lists Noxubee County next Friday as the next opponent.

## Alcorn and Tippah: Corinth beats Ripley

Corinth defeated Ripley **42–0** in the Class 4A first round, according to the [Associated Press score list](https://www.ctpost.com/sports/article/friday-s-scores-22517694.php). That result is the new Alcorn County development and one of the Tippah County results in this edition.

Tippah's Walnut lost to North Side **48–6**, also in the [AP results](https://www.ctpost.com/sports/article/friday-s-scores-22517694.php). French Camp Academy beat Falkner **55–14**, according to [High School On SI's Mississippi finals](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/mississippi-high-school-football-final-060105341.html).

## Benton: Ashland's playoff run ends

Ashland lost to West Tallahatchie **48–6**, according to the [MaxPreps final](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/0B09W_EXJk-vnHcLqlV-5g/football-recap-west-tallahatchie-piles-up-the-points-against-ashland--how-to-watch.htm). The platform lists no further Ashland game on its current schedule.
