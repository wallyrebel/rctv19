## Union: Museum schedules three children's programs

The Union County Heritage Museum in New Albany has scheduled **Preschool Music Day for Dec. 4**, free Christmas card making for Dec. 10 and a Christmas stocking hand-sewing class for ages 10 and older for Dec. 17, [Union County News reported](https://unionnewsms.com/local-news/union-county-museum-schedules-december-programs-for-children/). The preschool session is for ages 2 to 5 and requires a parent to stay. All three programs are planned at 114 Cleveland St.; the museum's information number is 662-538-0014.

## Union, Prentiss and Alcorn: OrthoSouth adds area clinics

OrthoSouth says the former OrthoXpress clinics in **New Albany, Booneville and Corinth** joined its practice Nov. 16, along with five other clinics in Mississippi and Tennessee. Existing staff teams are continuing, and current patients need take no action to continue seeing their providers, according to the [company announcement](https://orthosouth.org/news/orthosouth-welcomes-eight-new-orthopedic-clinics-to-its-mid-south-family/). [Alcorn County News](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/orthosouth-corinth-booneville-clinics/) reported the local change Friday and lists clinic contact details. This is a newly reported local service update; the practice change itself took effect earlier this month.

## Tippah: Ripley lists five public storm shelter sites

The City of Ripley's posted map lists the American Legion, City Park Gym, TCHS Wellness Center, Good Samaritan Center and West Ripley Church of Christ as public storm shelter locations, [Tippah News reported Friday](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/ripley-storm-shelters-sunday/). The report follows a forecast for Sunday showers and thunderstorms. **The map does not give opening hours:** check city alerts or contact the city before relying on a site. See the [city's map linked in the source report](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/ripley-storm-shelters-sunday/) for directions and the [current National Weather Service forecast](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=34.7296&lon=-88.9444) for changing conditions.
