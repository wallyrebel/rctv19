# Blue Mountain Christian Volleyball Completes Vette City Classic with Mixed Results

The Blue Mountain Christian volleyball team wrapped up its participation in the Vette City Classic tournament in Bowling Green, Kentucky, with a mixed performance that saw the team secure one victory while suffering two defeats over the two-day event.

## Tournament Performance

Competing against a field of regional opponents, the Volley Toppers demonstrated resilience throughout the tournament, though ultimately fell short of a winning record. The team's 1-2 finish reflects the competitive nature of the Vette City Classic and the challenges posed by the tournament's roster of participating teams.

## Looking Ahead

While the results were not what the program hoped for, the tournament provided valuable experience and competitive matchups for Blue Mountain Christian as the volleyball team continues its season. The experience gained from facing quality opponents in Bowling Green will likely prove beneficial as the team moves forward in its schedule.

The Blue Mountain Christian volleyball program will look to build on the lessons learned from the Vette City Classic as it prepares for upcoming matches.
