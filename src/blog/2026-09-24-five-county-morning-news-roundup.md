This Thursday morning, Sept. 24, 2026, roundup covers developments reported after Wednesday's edition in Union, Prentiss and Alcorn counties, plus a schedule reminder for tonight's Ripley Homecoming events. We found no additional meaningful, verified Benton County update.

## Union County: Mayor discusses growth and Riverfest

New Albany Mayor Jeff Olson discussed the city's development plans and the upcoming Tallahatchie Riverfest during an appearance on *Mornings with Richard Cross*, [Union County News reported Thursday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/new-albany-mayor-jeff-olson-discusses-growth-and-riverfest/). The report does not specify a new project or a Riverfest schedule change, so residents should check organizers' current information before making plans.

## Prentiss County: Baldwyn student explores automation work

Baldwyn High School junior Gavin Gamble spent Tuesday shadowing staff at Challenge Automation in Tupelo, [Prentiss County News reported Wednesday evening](https://prentissnews.com/prentiss-county-news/baldwyn-high-student-shadows-at-challenge-automation-in-tupelo/), citing a Baldwyn School District post. The business is owned by Baldwyn High alumnus Eric Nanney. Gamble said the visit showed him both the equipment and how the company operates.

## Alcorn County: Police visit Corinth Elementary

Corinth Police Department officers visited Corinth Elementary for Career Day and spoke with students about police work and community service, [Alcorn County News reported Wednesday evening](https://alcornnewsms.com/corinth-news/corinth-police-career-day-elementary/), citing the department's post. The department shared photos of officers with students during the visit.

## Tippah County: Homecoming parade and pep rally tonight

Ripley High School's Homecoming parade is scheduled to start near First Baptist Church at **6:30 p.m. Thursday**, ending near First Methodist Church. A community pep rally is scheduled for **7:30 p.m.** at the school's football field, [Tippah News reports](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-news-2026-09-23-ripley-homecoming-parade/), citing Ripley Main Street. These events were [announced earlier](https://www.facebook.com/875713894854316/posts/1098411145917922); this is a reminder for residents planning to attend, not a new event announcement. The Tigers are scheduled to host Booneville on Friday.
