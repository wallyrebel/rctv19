Saturday afternoon's forecast brings a practical update for Tippah and Benton counties. Two other newly posted community reports concern Ripley and Ingomar. The Friday football finals appeared in this morning's roundup and are not repeated here.

## Tippah and Benton: Frost possible overnight

[Tippah News reported at 12:41 p.m. Saturday](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/tippah-county-frost-forecast/) that the National Weather Service forecast calls for patchy frost in **Ripley after 3 a.m. Sunday**, with a low near 35 degrees. The same report gives **Ashland** a low near 35 with patchy frost and fog after 2 a.m. Forecasts can change; check the [National Weather Service point forecast](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=34.73&lon=-88.95) before an early trip. Clear frost from vehicle windows and protect tender outdoor plants.

## Tippah: Ripley church starts bed-building effort

Men from First Baptist Church Ripley joined a Beds for Kids build night Thursday, Nov. 5, which the church described as the start of a new ministry, [Tippah News reported Saturday morning](https://tippahnews.com/ripley-ms-news/fbc-ripley-beds-for-kids/). The church identified Michael Massengill and Chris Taylor as leaders. The report says the church did not specify how many beds were built or where they will go.

## Union: Ingomar students hear about health careers

Stefenie Hodges of Baptist Hospital spoke to a health class at Ingomar Attendance Center about careers in health care, according to a [Saturday Union County News report](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/baptist-hospital-representative-discusses-health-careers-with-students/) citing the school's post. The report gives no date for the visit, so this is a newly posted school update rather than a claim that the visit occurred today.

## Prentiss and Alcorn

No distinct, meaningful new development was verified for either county since the morning roundup. Friday's Booneville and Corinth playoff results were already covered there.
