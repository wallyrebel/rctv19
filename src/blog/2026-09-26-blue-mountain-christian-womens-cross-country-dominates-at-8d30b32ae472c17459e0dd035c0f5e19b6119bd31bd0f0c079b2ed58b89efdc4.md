Blue Mountain Christian's women's cross country team delivered a dominant performance at the Topper Trails College Classic, sweeping the top five individual spots and claiming the team championship. The victory was particularly meaningful as it came on Senior Day, allowing the program to honor its graduating runners during the competition.
