# Tippah County Basketball Doubleheaders Scheduled for November 7

Multiple basketball games are taking place across Tippah County today. Walnut is hosting a girls-and-boys basketball doubleheader, while Blue Mountain takes on Calhoun City in two games. Tipoff times range from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
