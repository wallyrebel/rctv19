Sunday's verified updates for Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties include two community service and event notices, a school career trip and a state golf schedule.

## Alcorn: Trail of Treats returns Tuesday

Corinth's Trail of Treats is scheduled for **Tuesday, Oct. 20, from 6 to 8 p.m.** at Crossroads Regional Park, [Alcorn County News reported Sunday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-trail-of-treats-crossroads-park/), citing the *Daily Corinthian*. More than 20 vendors are expected to hand out treats for children 12 and under. The report says an early session for visitors with special needs runs from **5:30 to 6 p.m.** Children younger than 8 must be accompanied by an adult.

## Prentiss: Booneville ministry offers Monday meal

Honey in the Rock offers a **free public meal each Monday from 5 to 7 p.m.** at its Church Street location in Booneville, according to [Alcorn County News' Sunday profile](https://alcornnewsms.com/public-service/booneville-ministry-free-weekly-meal/) based on the *Booneville Banner-Independent*. The nonprofit also provides emergency food assistance, a clothing room and classes in practical skills. The ministry is seeking churches, groups and individuals to help provide meals; the report lists **662-554-7596** for those interested. This is a newly reported profile of an ongoing service, rather than a newly opened program.

## Tippah: Walnut students tour Toyota plant

Walnut Attendance Center students visited the Toyota Mississippi plant in Blue Springs during the past week, [Tippah News reported Sunday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/walnut-students-toyota-mississippi-tour/), citing the school's post. The school described the visit as a chance to see robotics and manufacturing work and connect classroom STEM subjects with careers. The plant is in Union County, but the participating students are from Tippah County.

## Tippah: Falkner golfers begin state trip Monday

Falkner's girls golf team is scheduled for the **Oct. 19–20 state championship in Brookhaven**, [Tippah County Sports reported Sunday](https://tippahsports.com/blue-mountain/falkner-golfers-head-to-state-as-tippah-runners-prepare-for-regionals/), citing the school's announcement. The team qualified with a division runner-up finish. No tee time was posted in the report, and this is a schedule item, not a tournament result.

No distinct, meaningful new **Union** or **Benton County** development with enough verified detail was found for this edition. Benton schools' Discovery Day and school grades, and Union County's recent volleyball titles, were covered in earlier roundups.
