## Tippah and Benton: Counties no longer on state burn-ban list

The Mississippi Forestry Commission's latest county burn-ban list does not show Tippah or Benton under a ban, [Tippah News reported Tuesday afternoon](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/tippah-county-burn-ban-lifted/). The outlet says both counties were on the state map Nov. 3 but absent from its Nov. 4 update and the map posted Monday evening. Tippah's earlier order had been scheduled through Nov. 21; the outlet has not seen a written county order explaining when or why it ended. Residents should [check the commission's current list](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/burning-info/burn-bans/) before burning, because the status can change.

## Alcorn and Prentiss: Drought conditions improve on Nov. 3 map

[Alcorn County News reported Tuesday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/alcorn-county-drought-eases-november/) that the U.S. Drought Monitor's **Nov. 3 map, released Nov. 5**, no longer places any of Alcorn County in extreme drought. The report says 45.73% of Alcorn remains in severe drought, with the rest in moderate drought. Prentiss County moved from severe to moderate drought throughout the county. Neither county is drought-free. This is an update on last week's conditions, with the next map due Thursday; it is not a measurement taken today. See the [Drought Monitor's county data](https://droughtmonitor.unl.edu/DmData/DataTables.aspx?county,28003=).

## Union: New Albany holds holiday open house

Families gathered downtown for a holiday open house and Christmas tree lighting, [Union County News reported Tuesday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/new-albany-celebrates-holiday-open-house-and-tree-lighting/), citing organizers. The New Albany Choir performed and Santa posed for photos. The brief report does not specify an attendance count.
