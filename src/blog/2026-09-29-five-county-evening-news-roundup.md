Tuesday's local reports bring a utility recovery funding announcement and a university leadership change in Tippah County. The other updates below concern a school schedule change and a college internship event. All times are Central.

## Union County: Homecoming schedule moved up

A high school's football game and homecoming activities have been **moved to Thursday, Oct. 1**, because of the forecast for Friday thunderstorms, [Union County News reported Tuesday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/high-school-football-game-and-homecoming-events-moved-to-thursday-due-to-weather/), citing school officials. The report does not identify the school or give event times, and says further details will come through school channels. Anyone planning to attend should check the school's own schedule before traveling. This is a schedule update, not a report that the game has taken place.

## Prentiss County: Northeast reports internship interview event

Northeast Mississippi Community College said it hosted a **Tiger Internship Interview Day** bringing students and participating local industries together, [Prentiss County News reported Tuesday](https://prentissnews.com/local-news/northeast-mississippi-community-colleges-tiger-internship-program-gains-praise/). The report says the program helps employers meet students and consider future hires; it does not specify when the event took place or report placement totals. The college lists Carrie Cobb at cjcobb@nemcc.edu as a contact for program information.

## Tippah County: FEMA award announced for electric cooperative

FEMA Region 4 announced **$3.6 million for Tippah Electric Power Association** for work tied to the January 2026 winter storm, [Tippah News reported Tuesday](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/fema-awards-3-6-million-to-tippah-electric-power-association-for-winter-storm-recovery/). The report says the award covers a fiber distribution system, cut-and-toss operations, and repairs to damaged poles and transformers. The award is part of a broader $8.3 million Mississippi package. The announcement describes funding for recovery work; it does not say every project has been completed or that the full award has already been spent.

## Tippah County: Blue Mountain Christian president plans retirement

Blue Mountain Christian University President **Barbara C. McMillin plans to retire June 30, 2027**, at the end of the 2026–27 academic year, [Tippah News reported Tuesday](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/blue-mountain-christian-university-president-dr-barbara-mcmillin-to-retire-in-june-2027/). The report says the university released the announcement Monday and that its trustees will provide presidential-search details later. McMillin became the university's eighth president in 2012. Her retirement is a future plan, not an immediate change in leadership.
