Blue Mountain Christian forward Terrence Marco earned NAIA Men's Basketball Defensive Player of the Week honors for performances from Nov. 9-15, according to the national office.

Marco averaged 7.0 blocks, 8.5 rebounds and 11.5 points per game during the week as Blue Mountain Christian went 1-1.
