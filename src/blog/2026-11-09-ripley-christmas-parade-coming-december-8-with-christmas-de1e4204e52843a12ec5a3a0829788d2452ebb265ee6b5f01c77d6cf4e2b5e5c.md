# Ripley Christmas Parade Set for December 8

The City of Ripley announces the upcoming Ripley Christmas Parade on Tuesday, December 8, beginning at 7:00 PM in downtown Ripley.

This year's theme is "Christmas Through the Decades." Participants are invited to select their favorite decade—the 1950s, 1960s, 1970s, 1980s, or 1990s—and decorate their float accordingly.

There is no entry fee to participate. Interested participants can sign up online or by scanning the QR code provided on the event flyer. The city is encouraging community involvement to make this "the biggest parade yet."
