Brown & Covington, a new business in Ripley, has chosen a name that honors two figures from the town's past. According to Ripley Main Street on Facebook, the business is named after Mrs. C.B. Brown and Tommy Covington.

The business says it aims to ensure "the stories of those who came before us are never forgotten" as it begins operations. The owners are inviting community members to share memories of Mrs. Brown or Tommy Covington in the coming days.
