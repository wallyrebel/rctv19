# Veterans Day Program Planned at Ripley Sportsplex

A Veterans Day program will be held on Wednesday, November 11 at 11:00 AM at the Ripley Sportsplex to honor those who have served in the military.

The event will feature a patriotic program with musical performances by the Ripley Elementary Gifted Class, the Ripley High School Band, and the South Tippah Choir. A picnic lunch will be available for $5, offering hamburgers and hot dogs.

For questions about the event, contact American Legion Post 81 at 662-321-0221.
