The newly posted Class 1A state cross-country standings add results for Benton, Union and Tippah counties beyond the Class 2A and 3A finishes in this morning's roundup. In Alcorn County, a Corinth nonprofit has announced its holiday Toy Store registration schedule.

## Benton: Hickory Flat boys finish second in Class 1A

Hickory Flat Attendance Center's boys finished **second in the Class 1A state cross-country championship with 72 points**, behind French Camp Academy's 36, according to the [Mississippi High School Activities Association's official results](https://www.misshsaa.com/2026/10/29/french-camp-wins-2026-class-1a-boys-cross-country-championship/). Sophomore Ryder Paul placed third individually in 18:20.10. The [girls standings](https://www.misshsaa.com/2026/10/29/tupelo-christian-wins-2026-class-1a-girls-cross-country-championship/) list Hickory Flat fifth with 136 points; sophomore Oliva Beth Roberson was sixth individually in 22:28.30. The races were held Thursday, Oct. 29, in Clinton.

## Union: West Union girls place third

West Union Attendance Center's girls placed **third in Class 1A with 88 points**, the [MHSAA standings show](https://www.misshsaa.com/2026/10/29/tupelo-christian-wins-2026-class-1a-girls-cross-country-championship/). In the [boys race](https://www.misshsaa.com/2026/10/29/french-camp-wins-2026-class-1a-boys-cross-country-championship/), West Union placed fifth with 125 points, and Ingomar was sixth with 146. Ingomar freshman Austin Hancock finished second individually in 17:57.50.

## Tippah: Blue Mountain and Jumpertown complete state meet

In the [Class 1A boys team results](https://www.misshsaa.com/2026/10/29/french-camp-wins-2026-class-1a-boys-cross-country-championship/), Blue Mountain placed seventh with 171 points and Jumpertown 10th with 242. These are separate from Walnut's Class 2A runner-up finish reported in the morning roundup.

## Alcorn: Toy Store registration opens Monday in Corinth

The Lighthouse Foundation plans to take **in-person Toy Store registrations Nov. 2–20**, on Mondays, Tuesdays and Thursdays from 9 a.m. to noon at 1103 S. Johns St. in Corinth, [Alcorn County News reports](https://alcornnewsms.com/corinth-news/lighthouse-toy-store-registration-corinth/), citing the Daily Corinthian. The program is for Alcorn County families with children from birth through age 18. Applicants should bring a photo ID, proof of county residency and a birth certificate for each child. The foundation's registration phone number is **662-286-0091**.
