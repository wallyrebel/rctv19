Potts Camp's boys basketball team overwhelmed Falkner 72–9 on Tuesday, November 24, in a lopsided matchup. The game was decided early, with Potts Camp establishing a 35–1 lead in the first quarter and extending it to 51–1 by halftime. Potts Camp added 14 points in the fourth quarter while Falkner was held scoreless in the final period.

Falkner entered the game on a losing streak, having fallen to Mantachie 59–42 on November 20 and Hickory Flat 64–18 on November 23. Potts Camp came in with consecutive wins over Bruce (74–48 on November 21) and East Union (79–53 on November 23).
