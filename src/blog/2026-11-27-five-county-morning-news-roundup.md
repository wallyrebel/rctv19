## Union: Meriwether Lewis marker dedication set for Dec. 2

The Union County Heritage Museum plans to unveil a Mississippi Department of Archives and History historic marker commemorating Meriwether Lewis' final journey at **10 a.m. Wednesday, Dec. 2**, at the Tallahatchie River Bridge in New Albany, [Union County News reported](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/new-albany-to-unveil-marker-honoring-meriwether-lewis-final-journey/), citing the museum's announcement. The dedication is scheduled; the marker has not yet been unveiled.

## Prentiss and Alcorn: Four county schools to share tennis region

Jumpertown, New Site, Thrasher and Wheeler are assigned to **Class I Region 1 for the 2027–29 tennis cycle**, alongside Alcorn County's Biggersville, [Prentiss County News reported](https://prentissnews.com/prentiss-county-news/mhsaa-tennis-realignment-will-put-all-four-prentiss-county-schools-in-one-region-cycle-41dec95fc359/). Its report, published Thursday, compares the new alignment with the current regions and cites the Mississippi High School Activities Association's Nov. 4 release. Booneville is assigned to Class 4A Region 1 in the next cycle.

## Tippah: Sunday forecast calls for rain and storms

The National Weather Service's Thursday evening point forecast for Ripley called for a **90% chance of showers and thunderstorms Sunday, Nov. 29**, with winds that could gust to 30 mph, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/tippah-county-weekend-forecast-storms/). Friday and Saturday were forecast to be mostly sunny and sunny, respectively. Forecasts can change; check the [current NWS forecast](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=34.7296&lon=-88.9444) before travel.
