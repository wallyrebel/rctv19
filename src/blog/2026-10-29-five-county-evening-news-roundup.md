This evening's new local reports include an extended Corinth school fundraiser deadline and a Union County classroom visit. A newly published regional football preview also confirms Benton County's Ashland game for tonight. The school district announced its extension Tuesday, Oct. 27; the Alcorn County News report appeared Thursday.

## Alcorn: Corinth school tag pre-orders extended to Nov. 19

The [Corinth School District Foundation says](https://www.corinth.k12.ms.us/details-page/~board/district-news/post/warrior-tag-deadline-extended) it has extended the pre-order deadline for its proposed Warrior specialty license plate to **Nov. 19**. The foundation needs **300 pre-orders** before the Mississippi Department of Revenue can begin producing the plates. Each application carries a **$33 fee**, of which **$24 goes to the foundation**, the district says. [Alcorn County News reported the extension Thursday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-warrior-tag-deadline-extended/). The district's [tag page](https://www.corinth.k12.ms.us/tag) has application instructions. The deadline change was announced Tuesday, not made this evening.

## Union: Sheriff's staff speak with students

Chief Deputy Baron Baker, investigators Adam Fitts and Calub Merritt, and school resource officer Tommy Sloan spoke to a college and career readiness class about law enforcement careers, [Union County News reported Thursday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/union-county-deputies-speak-to-students-about-law-enforcement/), citing an original school post. The four also spoke to a health class about narcotics and their effects. The report did not specify the date of the classroom visit.

## Benton: Ashland scheduled to host Byers tonight

Ashland is scheduled to host Byers in a region football game at **7 p.m. Thursday**, according to [Tippah News' Thursday preview](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-county-football-season-finale/). This is an advance schedule notice, not a game result. Check with the school for any late change.
