# Movie Night at The Cut-Off

The Cut-Off is hosting a fall movie night featuring the classic film Hocus Pocus. The event takes place on **October 17th at 7:00 PM** at The Cut-Off, located at 204 South Commerce Street in downtown Ripley.

The outdoor screening is free and open to the public. Attendees are invited to bring blankets and chairs, grab popcorn, and enjoy the movie with family and friends. Costumes are welcome for the fall-themed event.

West Glass Company is sponsoring the movie night. The event aims to bring entertainment and community gathering to downtown Ripley.
