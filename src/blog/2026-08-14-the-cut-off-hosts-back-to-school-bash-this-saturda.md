# The Cut-Off Celebrates Back-to-School Season with Community Event This Saturday

While students have already returned to classrooms, The Cut-Off is extending the back-to-school celebration with a festive community event this Saturday.

## Event Details

The Back-to-School Bash will take place **Saturday from 12:00 PM to 4:00 PM** at The Cut-Off, located at **204 S. Commerce Street in Ripley**.

## What to Expect

Families attending the event will enjoy a variety of entertainment and activities, including:

- Bouncy houses for children
- Face painting stations
- Interactive games
- Multiple vendors
- Food trucks offering refreshments and meals
- **Free school supplies for students**

## Community Focus

The event represents a community effort to support students and families during the school year. Organizers are encouraging residents to bring their children downtown for an afternoon of entertainment and to take advantage of the free school supply distribution.

## Get Involved

The downtown Ripley location makes this an accessible event for the community, combining entertainment with practical support for students as they settle into the academic year. Parents and guardians are invited to make it a family outing and enjoy the downtown atmosphere while participating in the festivities.
