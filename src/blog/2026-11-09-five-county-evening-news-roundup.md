## Tippah: Falkner sixth graders visit NEMCC

Falkner sixth graders visited Northeast Mississippi Community College in Booneville on **Friday, Nov. 6**, as part of the college's mini NEnow program, according to a [Tippah News report published Monday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/falkner-sixth-graders-nemcc-visit/) citing Falkner High School. The visit included a welcome from the Tiger Band, cheerleaders and Pom Squad, a campus tour and lunch. The trip happened Friday; it was reported today.

## Alcorn: Corinth forecast calls for Wednesday rain chance

A [National Weather Service Memphis graphic updated early Monday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-freeze-friday-veterans-day-showers/) shows a **60% maximum probability of precipitation for Corinth on Wednesday, Nov. 11**, as a cold front moves through the Mid-South Wednesday afternoon into Thursday morning. The graphic says showers and very isolated thunderstorms are possible, with light rainfall expected. [Alcorn County News](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-freeze-friday-veterans-day-showers/) reports the NWS point forecast put Corinth near **28 degrees Friday night**. These are forecasts and may change; check the [latest NWS Memphis updates](https://www.weather.gov/meg/) before making plans.
