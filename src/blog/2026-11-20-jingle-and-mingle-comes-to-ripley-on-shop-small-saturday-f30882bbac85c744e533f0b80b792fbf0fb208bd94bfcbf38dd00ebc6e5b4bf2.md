Ripley Main Street is hosting Jingle and Mingle on Shop Small Saturday, November 28.

Shoppers can pick up a Jingle and Mingle card at Brown and Covington, 301 South Commerce Street, and shop throughout the day. Those who spend $75 or more at a participating shop will be entered into the Grand Prize Giveaway. Customers can also fill up their cards for a chance to win Main Street Moolah.
