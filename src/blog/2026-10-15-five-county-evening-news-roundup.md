A planned recovery service opening, electric infrastructure work and a university alumni event lead Thursday evening's update for Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties. Dates for the upcoming events are plans as of the linked reports.

## Prentiss: Frankstown recovery center plans Oct. 20 opening

God's House of Hope plans to open a recovery center in the Frankstown community on **Tuesday, Oct. 20**, [Alcorn County News reported Thursday](https://alcornnewsms.com/public-service/prentiss-county-recovery-center-frankstown/), citing the *Daily Corinthian*. The report says the Shook Family Foundation's new location will offer recovery support and life-skills help for people facing addiction and other instability. A soft opening is planned from **9 a.m. to 8 p.m.**, with a **10:30 a.m. ribbon cutting**. The center has not opened yet; the linked report has the day's additional plans.

## Alcorn: ACE Power updates Corinth substation targets

ACE Power now hopes to complete its **industrial substation by the end of 2026** and is targeting **mid-to-late 2027** for its downtown substation, according to [Thursday's Alcorn County News report](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/ace-power-substation-upgrades-corinth/) based on *Daily Corinthian* reporting. The projects are intended to improve electric reliability around Corinth. The cooperative's [2024 annual report](https://ace-power.com/2024-annual-report-to-the-membership/) also described the industrial and downtown substations and anticipated commissioning years of 2026 and 2027. Both dates remain targets, not completed work.

## Tippah: BMCU alumni dinner set for Oct. 27

Blue Mountain Christian University has scheduled its Ministerial Alumni Dinner for **Tuesday, Oct. 27, at 5 p.m.** at Broadmoor Baptist Church in Madison, according to the [university's ticket listing](https://secure.qgiv.com/for/alumni/event/madinner2026/) and [alumni events page](https://www.bmc.edu/alumni-events). Tickets are listed at **$16–$25**. [Tippah News reported Thursday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/bmcu-ministerial-alumni-dinner/) that the dinner marks 70 years of ministerial education at the Blue Mountain school and that advance ticket sales close Oct. 21. The gathering is in Madison; its local connection is the university and its alumni.

No separate meaningful new development with enough specific, verifiable detail was found this evening for **Union or Benton** counties. East Union's Thursday state volleyball final was scheduled, but a final score was not confirmed by the checked sources at publication time, so no result is reported here.
