Nettleton defeated Blue Mountain 68–49 in boys varsity basketball Monday, November 23, according to the teams' final box score on MaxPreps.

The loss was Blue Mountain's second in three games. The team beat Houlka 53–47 on November 17 before losing to Marshall Academy 53–41 on November 20.

Nettleton improved to 3–0 in its recent stretch, having defeated Walnut 76–61 on November 17 and Jumpertown 77–65 on November 19 before the Blue Mountain game.
