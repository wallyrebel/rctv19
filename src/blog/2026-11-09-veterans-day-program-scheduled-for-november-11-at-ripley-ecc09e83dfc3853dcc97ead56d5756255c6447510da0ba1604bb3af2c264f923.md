# Veterans Day Program Scheduled for November 11 at Ripley Sportsplex

The community is invited to a Veterans Day program on Wednesday, November 11 at 11:00 AM at the Ripley Sportsplex. The event will feature a patriotic program with music and food.

## Entertainment and Activities

Musical performances will be provided by the Ripley Elementary Gifted Class, the Ripley High School Band, and the South Tippah Choir.

A picnic lunch will be available for $5, featuring hamburgers and hot dogs.

## Contact Information

For questions about the event, contact American Legion Post 81 at 662-321-0221.

The organizers extend their thanks to all who have served.
