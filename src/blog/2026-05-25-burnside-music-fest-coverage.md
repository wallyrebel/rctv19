## North Mississippi Primary Health Care Sponsors Burnside Music Fest, Supporting Live Blues in Historic Ripley

*May 25, 2026*

North Mississippi Primary Health Care has stepped up as a sponsor of the Burnside Music Fest, demonstrating a strong commitment to supporting local culture and community events in the Ripley area.

### Community Partnership in Action

The sponsorship represents more than just financial support—it reflects a broader dedication to preserving and celebrating the rich musical heritage of the North Mississippi Hill Country. Through partnerships like this, organizers say they can continue bringing vibrant live music experiences and cultural tourism to the heart of historic downtown Ripley.

### About the Festival

Burnside Music Fest is scheduled for Saturday, June 6, in downtown Ripley. The event celebrates the region's legendary blues tradition while energizing the community and attracting visitors to the area.

### Supporting Local Culture

Organizers expressed their gratitude for the healthcare provider's involvement, noting that community partnerships are essential for sustaining local events and preserving cultural traditions. By backing the festival, North Mississippi Primary Health Care is helping ensure that the distinctive sounds and stories of Hill Country blues continue to resonate with both longtime fans and new audiences.

Festival organizers encourage residents and visitors to mark their calendars for the June 6 celebration of music, community, and local pride.

## Signed Burnside Festival Guitar Hits Auction Block to Support Historic Ripley Revitalization

*May 28, 2026*

<img src="/assets/optimized/2ff3ac8660a784ebcd1e-960.webp" srcset="/assets/optimized/2ff3ac8660a784ebcd1e-480.webp 480w, /assets/optimized/2ff3ac8660a784ebcd1e-960.webp 960w" sizes="(max-width: 960px) 100vw, 900px" alt="Signed Burnside Festival Guitar Hits Auction Block to Support Historic Ripley Revitalization" loading="lazy">

### Burnside Festival Guitar Auction Launches to Support Community Revitalization

A unique opportunity to own a piece of Hill Country Blues history is now available as a signed guitar from the 2025 Burnside Music Festival goes under the hammer in an online auction.

The instrument, autographed by the artists who performed at the festival, is being auctioned to benefit Historic Downtown Ripley revitalization projects. The initiative combines support for live music with community development efforts in Ripley, Mississippi.

#### Auction Details

**Starting Bid:** $100
**Minimum Raise:** $10
**Auction Deadline:** Saturday, June 6 at 10:00 PM

Prospective bidders can place their offers directly in the comments section of the auction post. The highest bid at the time the auction closes will secure the guitar.

#### Supporting Local Music and Community

All proceeds from the sale will be directed toward downtown revitalization initiatives, making this auction a meaningful way for music enthusiasts and community supporters to contribute to Ripley's development while acquiring a memorable collectible.

The Burnside Music Festival organizers emphasize that this auction represents an opportunity to support live music, Hill Country Blues traditions, and the local community simultaneously.

Interested bidders are encouraged to place their bids before the Saturday deadline to participate in this charitable fundraising effort.
