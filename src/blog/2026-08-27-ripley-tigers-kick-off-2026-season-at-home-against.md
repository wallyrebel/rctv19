# Ripley Tigers Ready for Season Opener Against Mooreville

The Ripley Tigers will launch their 2026 football campaign Friday night with a home matchup against Mooreville at Tiger Stadium, setting the stage for what promises to be an intriguing defensive battle.

This contest marks a rematch of last year's game between the two programs, which was characterized by strong defensive performances from both sides. The Tigers will be looking to establish momentum early in the season as they enter Year 2 under head coach.

## Season Opener at Home

Kicking off the season at Tiger Stadium provides Ripley with the advantage of playing in familiar surroundings with home crowd support. The home-field advantage could prove crucial as the Tigers work to build chemistry and establish their identity early in the campaign.

## Defensive Focus Expected

Based on last year's matchup, fans can expect another defensive struggle between these two programs. Both teams appear to prioritize defensive execution, which should make for a competitive, hard-fought contest.

## Year 2 Under New Leadership

The opener comes as the Tigers continue their development under their head coach, who is now entering his second year at the helm. The preseason will have provided opportunities to implement his system and develop continuity among the roster.

The Ripley Tigers and Mooreville will square off Friday night with kickoff at Tiger Stadium.
