# Movie Night Magic Comes to Ripley This Friday

Movie enthusiasts and families in Ripley have a special treat lined up for this Friday, August 14, as The Cut-Off prepares to host an outdoor movie screening under the stars.

## Event Details

The venue will showcase **Zootopia 2** at dusk, offering the perfect opportunity for a casual evening of entertainment. Located at 204 South Commerce Street in Ripley, The Cut-Off is transforming into an open-air cinema where attendees can settle in with their own chairs and picnic blankets for maximum comfort.

## Food and Refreshments

Organizers are encouraging guests to arrive early and make an evening of it, with multiple dining options available on-site. Three food vendors will be operating throughout the event:

- **Chula's Food Truck** - offering Mexican cuisine
- **A6 Seafood** - featuring fresh seafood selections
- **Funky Cones** - providing ice cream and frozen treats

Popcorn will also be available for the classic movie-watching experience.

## Community Support

The movie night has been made possible through the generous sponsorship of **Rebel Services LLC**, demonstrating local business commitment to community entertainment and family activities.

## What to Bring

Attendees are asked to bring their own seating arrangements, such as lawn chairs or picnic blankets, to ensure comfort during the screening. The event runs from dusk, making it an ideal summer evening activity for families and friends looking to enjoy outdoor entertainment together.
