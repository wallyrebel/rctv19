## Union: New Albany moves holiday garbage pickup

New Albany will collect its **Thursday and Friday garbage routes on Wednesday, Nov. 25**, ahead of the Thanksgiving closures on Nov. 26–27. The change appears on the [city's 2026 holiday schedule](https://www.visitnewalbany.com/streets-sanitation) and was [reported Saturday by Union County News](https://unionnewsms.com/local-news/new-albany-moves-thanksgiving-garbage-routes-to-nov-25/). The city lists 662-534-1010 for questions about pickup.

## Prentiss: Northeast women win at home

Northeast Mississippi Community College's women's basketball team beat Coastal Alabama-North **71–53 Saturday in Booneville**, [Alcorn County News reported](https://alcornnewsms.com/mississippi-news/nemcc-womens-basketball-coastal-alabama/), citing the college's box score. Northeast improved to 6–1 and has won six straight. The Lady Tigers' next listed home game is Tuesday, Nov. 24, against Arkansas State Mid-South; check the college's schedule for any change.

## Alcorn and Prentiss: Fire marshal urges care with heaters

As households prepare for colder nights, the [Mississippi State Fire Marshal's Office](https://www.mid.ms.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Pressrel111726-1.pdf) says to keep bedding, curtains and other combustible items **at least three feet from heating equipment**, inspect space-heater cords, and turn heaters off when leaving home or going to sleep. The agency issued its statewide advice Nov. 12 after a fatal space-heater-related fire in Yazoo County; [Alcorn County News highlighted the advice for the Corinth area Saturday](https://alcornnewsms.com/corinth-news/space-heater-safety-corinth-freeze/). The reported death was **not** in the five-county coverage area.
