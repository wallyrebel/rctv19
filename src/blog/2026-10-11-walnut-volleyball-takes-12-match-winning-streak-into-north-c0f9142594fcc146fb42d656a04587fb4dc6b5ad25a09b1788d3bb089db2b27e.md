Walnut's volleyball team is heading into a North State matchup against East Union with momentum on their side, carrying a 12-match winning streak into the contest.

The Lady Wildcats will face East Union in a rematch of an earlier September meeting in which East Union emerged victorious. The upcoming game carries added significance as Walnut pursues a state championship, with East Union standing as an obstacle on that path.
