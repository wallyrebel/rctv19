# Chula's & Scoops: Free Gelato Promotion in November

Two local favorites are teaming up for a sweet November deal. Every Thursday from 2–4 PM this month, customers who make a purchase at Chula's (located at The Cut-Off) can claim a free scoop of gelato at Brown & Covington, which is located just across the street.

To redeem the offer, customers simply need to bring their receipt from Chula's to Brown & Covington. The promotion runs through November on Thursdays only.
