## Union: Parade entrants get height and staging guidance

For the **Nov. 30 New Albany–Union County Christmas parade**, the [New Albany Main Street Association's participant instructions](https://www.newalbanymainstreet.com/2026parade) set a 13-foot maximum entry height, including decorations. The Tanglefoot Trail bicycle bridge over Main Street has 14 feet of clearance. Organizers also give school bands separate arrival instructions and restrict large vehicles from the regular staging area. The parade starts at 6:30 p.m. Participants should consult the organizer's instructions before building or moving an entry. [RCTV 19 has a detailed participant guide](/blog/new-albany-christmas-parade-float-height-staging/).

## Alcorn: Marketplace plans Thanksgiving weekend opening

The 38834 Vendor Marketplace plans a grand opening at **3003 Highway 72 West in Corinth**, [Alcorn County News reported Monday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-vendor-marketplace-grand-opening/). Its reported hours are 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Friday, Nov. 27, and 10 a.m. to 3 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 28. The business says the market will feature local vendors and makers; check with it for any changes before visiting.

## Alcorn: Community theater lists December performances

The Corinth Dramatic Society has [listed tickets](https://simpletix.com/e/a-very-mississippi-christmas-tickets-299940) for *A Very Mississippi Christmas* at 303 South Fulton Drive on Dec. 3–5. The ticket page identifies Amy Marsh as writer and director and lists a $16.88 ticket price. [Alcorn County News](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-christmas-play-december/) reports 7 p.m. performances Thursday and Friday and an 11 a.m. Saturday matinee. This is an upcoming event, not a review of a performance.

## Tippah: Library branches close Thursday and Friday

The [Northeast Regional Library system's holiday notice](https://www.nereg.lib.ms.us/) says **all branches close Nov. 26 and 27**. [Ripley Library](https://www.nereg.lib.ms.us/ripley-public-library/) lists regular Monday–Thursday hours of 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. Readers planning a visit this week should check their branch's current hours. [RCTV 19's branch guide](/blog/tippah-county-library-hours-thanksgiving-week-2026/) includes Blue Mountain, Chalybeate and Walnut options.

No separate meaningful new Prentiss or Benton County development was verified for this edition.
