Ingomar defeated Pine Grove 75–44 in girls varsity basketball Friday, November 20, according to MaxPreps.

Pine Grove had won its previous two games: 52–31 over Ashland on November 10 and 55–12 over Blue Mountain on November 12.

Ingomar came into the matchup with consecutive wins over Thrasher (64–8 on November 17) and Myrtle (60–36 on November 19).
