Scooter's Coffee is opening its doors in Ripley on Friday, September 18. The celebration includes a ribbon cutting ceremony at 11 a.m., followed by the grand opening from 6 a.m. to 8 p.m. at 917 City Ave. South.

The new location is offering special promotions to mark the occasion, including free medium drinks for all customers and free shirts for those using the drive-thru. The community is invited to welcome the new business to Ripley.
