Blue Mountain Christian University's men's and women's basketball teams will open December with four consecutive Southern States Athletic Conference home doubleheaders.

Point University visits Thursday, Dec. 3, with women's games at 5:30 p.m. and men's at 7:30 p.m. Life University comes Saturday, Dec. 5, for games at noon and 2 p.m., respectively. The Toppers host the University of Mobile on Thursday, Dec. 10, at 5:30 and 7:30 p.m., and William Carey on Saturday, Dec. 12, at noon and 2 p.m. Women play first each date.

These will be the first home conference contests of the season for both teams. The men finished November 3-3 overall with a 3-0 home record and 0-2 conference mark. The women won 91-52 at Wesleyan on Nov. 21 and split their first two SSAC games.

Both teams also host Tennessee Southern on Dec. 17, with women playing at 5 p.m. and men at 7 p.m.
