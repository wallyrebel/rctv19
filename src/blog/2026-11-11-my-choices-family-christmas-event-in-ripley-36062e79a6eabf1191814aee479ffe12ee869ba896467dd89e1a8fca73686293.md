# My Choices Family Christmas Event

My Choices will host a Family Christmas celebration on Tuesday, November 17 from 2 to 7 PM at 109 Bails Road in Ripley.

The free event will include:
- Free gifts for moms and children, including diapers and wipes
- Pictures with Santa
- Kids activities
- Cookies and hot cocoa
- Parenting education information

For more information, text or call 662-401-3299.
