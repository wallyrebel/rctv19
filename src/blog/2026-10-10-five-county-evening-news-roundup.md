This Saturday evening roundup covers newly published local reporting since the morning edition. Times are Central. Friday's games and the drought map's data date are identified below; neither happened this afternoon.

## Union and Tippah: Friday football scores confirmed

New Albany defeated Ripley **32-15** on Friday, Oct. 9, and Biggersville beat Walnut **16-6** in Walnut, according to [Tippah County Sports' Saturday report](https://tippahsports.com/ripley/walnuts-tying-try-falls-short-ripley-loses-at-new-albany/). The morning roundup reported New Albany's win without a score; the 32-15 final is now verified. Walnut's result was not in that edition.

Walnut cut Biggersville's lead to 8-6 on Sawyer Cardwell's touchdown with 2:40 left, but its two-point try failed, the report says. Ripley scored all 15 of its points in the fourth quarter. These are results from Friday night, not new Saturday games.

## Alcorn and Prentiss: Drought persists as rain arrives

A [Saturday Alcorn County News report](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/severe-drought-tishomingo-alcorn-isaias/) examined the [U.S. Drought Monitor's Mississippi map dated Oct. 6](https://droughtmonitor.unl.edu/Data/pdf/20261006/20261006_MS_text.pdf), which was released Thursday. It found all of Alcorn County and about 68% of Prentiss County in **severe drought (D2)**. These figures describe conditions through Tuesday, before Saturday's rain; they do not measure any effect from the storm.

The report says Alcorn County's burn ban is scheduled to expire **Monday, Oct. 12**. It found no announced decision on an extension as of Saturday. Residents should check the [Mississippi Forestry Commission's current burn-ban information](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/burning-info/burn-bans/) before outdoor burning.

## Tippah: University profiles theatre professor

Blue Mountain Christian University highlighted speech and drama assistant professor Christi Mitchell in a feature shared Saturday, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/bmcu-theatre-professor-christi-mitchell/). Mitchell joined the university in 2017. The university's profile describes her faith-centered teaching and student productions; it does not announce a new performance or program.

## Benton: No separate verified update

We found no meaningful new Benton County development since the morning roundup. The earlier five-county weather coverage remains available in that edition; this report does not infer local damage or outages from the forecast.
