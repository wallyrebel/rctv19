## Alcorn and Prentiss: Fall hospital safety grades released

The Leapfrog Group's fall 2026 Hospital Safety Grade gives Magnolia Regional Health Center in Corinth a **C**, down from its spring **B**, while Baptist Memorial Hospital-Booneville received an **A**, up from a **B**, [Alcorn County News reported](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/hospital-safety-grades-booneville-magnolia/). The grades, released Nov. 18, evaluate patient safety measures. Readers can review the hospitals' detailed scorecards through the report's link to Leapfrog; a letter grade is one comparison tool, not a judgment about an individual patient's care.

## Prentiss and Tippah: Ripley man arrested after county pursuit

Prentiss County deputies arrested Eric Scott Topash, 39, of Ripley after a Thursday vehicle pursuit and foot search that began near a safety checkpoint, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/ripley-man-arrested-after-prentiss-county-vehicle-pursuit-and-foot-chase/). He was charged with failure to stop a motor vehicle when an officer signals and faces multiple misdemeanor charges. A justice court judge set bond at $25,000 on the felony charge. The case is to be presented to a grand jury; the charges are allegations, not a conviction.

## Tippah: Ripley Middle names Teacher of the Year

Ripley Middle School named seventh-grade math teacher Charlie Meeks its Teacher of the Year in a Friday announcement, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/ripley-ms-news/ripley-middle-teacher-of-the-year-meeks/). The school highlighted his dedication to students and positive effect on the school community.

## Union: Heritage Museum welcomes archive visitors

The Union County Heritage Museum says its archive is open for on-site visits, [Union County News reported](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/union-county-heritage-museum-opens-archive-to-public/). Its holdings include local yearbooks, bound copies of the *New Albany Gazette*, photographs and letters. Materials cannot be checked out, and the museum is seeking Union County yearbook donations. The report gives 662-538-0014 as the museum's contact number.
