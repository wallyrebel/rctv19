Walnut's girls varsity basketball team beat Strayhorn 64–14 on Friday, November 20, according to MaxPreps.

Walnut improved to 3–0 in recent games, having previously defeated Nettleton 69–37 on November 17 and Simmons 55–39 on November 14.

Strayhorn has lost three consecutive games: 77–12 to Walnut on November 7, 43–26 to Byers on November 13, and Friday's 64–14 loss.
