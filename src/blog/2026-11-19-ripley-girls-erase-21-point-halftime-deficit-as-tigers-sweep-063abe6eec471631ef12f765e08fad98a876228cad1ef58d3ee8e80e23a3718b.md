The Ripley girls trailed 40–19 at halftime but outscored Mooreville 21–18 in the second half and overtime to win 67–62 Tuesday. Mooreville led 23–8 after one quarter and extended its advantage to 40–19 by halftime. Ripley cut the deficit to 48–37 after three quarters, then scored 21 points in the fourth quarter to force overtime at 58–58. The Lady Tigers scored nine of 13 points in overtime to secure the victory, according to MaxPreps.

The Ripley boys defeated Mooreville 61–59 to complete the sweep. The two-point win was their closest game in recent play; they previously beat Kossuth 74–45 at home and Wheeler 67–60 on the road. The Ripley girls also won at Wheeler 42–37 for two straight road victories.

Both teams return home Thursday, Nov. 19, to face Center Hill, with the girls' game at 6 p.m. and the boys' at 7 p.m. The girls will play Mooreville again Monday, Nov. 23, at 3:15 p.m.
