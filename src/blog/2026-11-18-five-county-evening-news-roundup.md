## Tippah: Pine Grove students recognized for perfect scores

The South Tippah School Board honored Pine Grove students at its Tuesday, Nov. 17, meeting for perfect scores on the 2025–26 English language arts or mathematics MAAP state tests, [the district announced](https://southtippahschools.com/pine-grove-students-with-perfect-scores-on-ela-math-state-tests/). [Tippah News](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/pine-grove-perfect-maap-scores/) reported the recognition Wednesday. The district's notice does not name the students or specify their individual tests.

## Alcorn: Corinth posts spring Cambridge exam dates

Corinth School District has shared its spring 2027 Cambridge exam calendar in its Nov. 16–29 newsletter, [Alcorn County News reported Wednesday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinths-spring-cambridge-exams-begin-april-26-and-run-through-june-8/). The schedule begins April 26 with Thinking Skills and English Language papers and continues through June 8. Families should consult the [full calendar linked in the report](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinths-spring-cambridge-exams-begin-april-26-and-run-through-june-8/) for their student's subject, paper and time.

## Tippah: Ripley musical opens Thursday

Stage on Main's *A Very Mississippi Christmas* opens Thursday, Nov. 19, in Ripley, with 7 p.m. performances also scheduled for Friday and Saturday, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/stage-on-main-very-mississippi-christmas/). The theater sells tickets online and accepts cash or check at the box office, according to that report.

No distinct, meaningful new Union, Prentiss or Benton County development was verified for this edition.
