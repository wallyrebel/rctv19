Ripley Main Street invites shoppers to participate in the Jingle and Mingle Shop Crawl this Saturday, November 28.

Participants can pick up a Jingle & Mingle card at Brown & Covington, 301 S. Commerce Street, on Friday or Saturday. Shoppers who spend $75 or more at participating shops will be entered to win the Grand Prize Giveaway, which includes items from all participating retailers. Those who make purchases at every participating shop and fill their card can enter to win Main Street Moolah.
