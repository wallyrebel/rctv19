## Union, Benton and Tippah: Judicial runoffs ahead Dec. 1

Voters in Benton and Tippah counties will decide two judicial races in the Dec. 1 general runoff election, [Tippah News reported Thursday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-benton-judicial-runoffs-dec-1/). The 18th Chancery Court contest is between Mike Watts and Jenessa Carter Hicks. The Third Circuit Court contest is between Bart M. Adams and Shirley C. Byers. Union County is also in the Third Circuit district, so its voters have that race on the runoff ballot. These are runoffs from the Nov. 3 general election, not newly called elections.

The [Mississippi Secretary of State said Thursday](https://www.sos.ms.gov/news/saturday-person-absentee-voting-begins-upcoming-deadlines-2026-general-runoff-election) that circuit clerks' offices will be open for in-person absentee voting Saturday, Nov. 21, from 8 a.m. to noon. The final in-person absentee voting period is Nov. 28, also from 8 a.m. to noon. Voters can use the state's My Election Day portal to check the ballot for their address.

## Alcorn: Two Corinth officers complete academy training

Corinth police officers Ethan Tucker and Nate Gay completed basic training with Mississippi Law Enforcement Officers' Training Academy Class 281, [Alcorn County News reported Thursday](https://alcornnewsms.com/corinth-news/corinth-police-officers-graduate-academy/). The department announced the achievement Wednesday, Nov. 18, the day of the class's graduation ceremony.
