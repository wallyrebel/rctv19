# Tippah County Football Games Scheduled for Tonight, October 29

Three football games are scheduled for this evening across Tippah County. All games kick off at 7 p.m.

The Falkner Eagles will host Potts Camp at home, while both Ripley and Walnut are traveling for their matchups tonight. Local sports fans are encouraged to support their Tippah County teams as they take the field.
