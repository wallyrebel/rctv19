The Cut-Off is looking for a food truck operator to establish a consistent presence at its downtown location.

Chula's food truck is moving on to their next venture, opening up the opportunity for a new vendor to become a regular partner at The Cut-Off. The venue is seeking someone interested in being part of the downtown community, not just for special events but as an ongoing fixture.

The Cut-Off plans to host various community activities throughout the year, including movie nights, markets, live music, and other events. Applications for the food truck partnership will open soon.

Food truck owners or those interested in the opportunity are encouraged to apply. For more information or to be notified when applications become available, contact Ripley Main Street at ripleymainstreet@gmail.com or call 662-512-0226.
