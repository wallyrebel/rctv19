Designer Jonathan Kayne is making a one-day visit to Mississippi for in-store appointments. Customers can book appointments through www.merlenormanandmore.com to meet with the designer.

During the visit, attendees will have the opportunity to view the 2027 collection and place preorders to secure pieces before they become available to the general public. Kayne will also be showcasing exclusive one-of-a-kind gowns that have never been seen before.
