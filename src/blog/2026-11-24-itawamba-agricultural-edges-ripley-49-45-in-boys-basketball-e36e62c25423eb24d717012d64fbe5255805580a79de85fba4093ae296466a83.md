Itawamba Agricultural beat Ripley 49–45 in boys varsity basketball Monday, November 23, according to MaxPreps.

Itawamba Agricultural led 9–8 after the first quarter and extended its advantage to 26–15 by halftime. In the fourth quarter, Itawamba Agricultural scored 10 points while Ripley added 7.

Ripley entered the game with consecutive wins over Mooreville (61–59 on November 17) and Center Hill (54–51 on November 19). Itawamba Agricultural had lost its previous two games to Wheeler (69–40 on November 17) and New Hope (75–42 on November 19).
