# Calvo's Penalty Save, Alici's Goal Lead Blue Mountain Christian Past Mobile

Blue Mountain Christian men's soccer secured a 1-0 victory over the University of Mobile in SSAC conference play. The Toppers' win was built on two key moments: a penalty save by goalkeeper Calvo and a second-half goal from Alici that proved to be the difference in the match.
