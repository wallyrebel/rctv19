Hickory Flat beat Falkner 64–18 in boys varsity basketball Monday, November 23, according to MaxPreps.

Hickory Flat led 17–7 after the first quarter and extended its advantage to 34–11 by halftime. In the fourth quarter, Hickory Flat scored 9 points while Falkner added 3.

Falkner entered the game on a losing streak, having fallen to Tupelo Titans 52–40 on November 19 and to Mantachie 59–42 on November 20. Hickory Flat had won its previous two games, beating Tupelo Titans 46–31 on November 17 and Tupelo Christian Prep 53–42 on November 19.
