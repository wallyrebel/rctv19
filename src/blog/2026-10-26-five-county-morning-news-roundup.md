## Union, Alcorn and Tippah: Boys golf state play begins Monday

**New Albany's boys golf team is scheduled to compete Oct. 26–27** at Dancing Rabbit Golf Club in Choctaw. [Union County Sports reported Sunday](https://unionsportsms.com/2026/10/25/union-county-sports-2026-10-25/) that New Albany advanced from its regional tournament alongside **Corinth and Ripley**. The [Mississippi High School Activities Association lists Dancing Rabbit as the Class 4A boys championship site and Oct. 26 as the start date](https://www.misshsaa.com/2026/10/19/2026-golf-championship-host-sites/). Tee times and results were not available in the cited report. Readers planning to attend should confirm the latest schedule with the schools or MHSAA.
