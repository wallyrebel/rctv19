This Wednesday evening, Sept. 23, 2026, update covers verified developments in Benton, Prentiss and Alcorn counties. We found no meaningful new item for Union or Tippah counties since the previous roundup.

## Benton County: Crews respond to grass and woods fire near Ashland

Ashland firefighters and state forestry crews responded to a grass and woods fire on Highway 5 south of Ashland at about 12:30 a.m. Wednesday, [Action News 5 reports](https://www.actionnews5.com/2026/09/23/crews-battle-overnight-fire-ashland-while-burn-bans-remain-active-across-mississippi/), citing the Ashland Fire Department. Heavy smoke was reported along the highway. The report says there were no injuries or structural damage. Fire officials reminded residents that the burn ban remains in effect despite cooler weather.

## Prentiss County: Campus officer reads to preschoolers

Northeast Mississippi Community College campus police officer Jeremy Pace spent a morning reading to children in the college's Early Childhood Education Technology program, [Prentiss County News reports](https://prentissnews.com/local-news/northeast-mississippi-community-college-officer-reads-to-preschoolers-in-early-childhood-program/), citing the college. The program gives students classroom experience as they work toward a two-year degree.

## Alcorn County: School district partner receives state award

Strategic Solutions for Families received the 2026 Mississippi Association of Partners in Education Governor's Award for its work with the Alcorn School District, [Alcorn County News reports](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/alcorn-school-district-mape-award/), crediting the Daily Corinthian. The organization provides character and life-skills programs for kindergarten through 12th-grade students across the district.

## Alcorn County: Cool night before warmer days

The National Weather Service Memphis forecast calls for a partly to mostly cloudy and cool Wednesday night, with lows in the upper 50s to lower 60s across the Mid-South. Its outlook shows Corinth warming above normal later this week, with mainly rain-free weather through the weekend, [Alcorn County News reports](https://alcornnewsms.com/corinth-news/nws-memphis-corinth-stays-cool-before-warming-by-weekend-a02059ec693c/). Forecasts can change; check the [National Weather Service](https://www.weather.gov/meg/) for current conditions.
