Monday evening's edition focuses on newly reported local developments and an updated regional forecast. The Friday city action is dated as such; the Thursday school events and weekend weather are upcoming. Times are Central.

## Alcorn: Corinth schools set Thursday parade and early dismissal

The [Corinth School District's Oct. 12–18 newsletter](https://www.corinth.k12.ms.us/fs/comms-manager/view/949b50b6-91f8-4cc8-b35b-5a22fa7dfea0) lists **2 p.m. early dismissal at all Corinth schools Thursday, Oct. 15**, followed by the **homecoming parade at 5:30 p.m.** Report cards are also scheduled to go home Thursday. The district lists a Friday, Oct. 16, **7 p.m. home football game against Tishomingo County**, with the homecoming court presented at halftime. [Alcorn County News' report](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-homecoming-week-2026/) says the district newsletter did not give a parade route; families should check the district for route and parking details before attending.

## Alcorn and Prentiss: Corinth attorney due to join district attorney's office

Jeremy Hudson of Corinth is expected to become an assistant district attorney for Mississippi's First Circuit Court District on **Nov. 1**, [Alcorn County News reports](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/jeremy-hudson-of-corinth-to-join-district-attorneys-office-as-assistant-da/). The report says Hudson previously practiced law with Wood and Carlton and has led a Corinth property-management company since 2019. [Alcorn County's district attorney page](https://www.alcorncounty.org/district-attorney/) lists both Alcorn and Prentiss among the counties the office serves. The appointment is prospective; the report does not say he has already begun the job.

## Alcorn: City adopts emergency kratom sales ban

Corinth aldermen adopted an emergency ordinance at a **Friday, Oct. 9, special meeting** that immediately bans kratom sales inside city limits, [Alcorn County News reported Monday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-kratom-ban-emergency-ordinance/), citing the Daily Corinthian. The report says Police Chief Landon Tucker requested the action. Anyone needing the ordinance's exact product definitions or enforcement terms should contact Corinth City Hall; the ordinance text was not available in the linked report.

## Across the area: forecasters expect a Friday cool-down

The [National Weather Service in Memphis said at 1:14 p.m. Monday](https://forecast.weather.gov/product.php?site=MEG&issuedby=MEG&product=AFD) that much of the Mid-South should remain in the upper 80s to low 90s through at least Thursday. Its current regional outlook calls for a cold front Friday, with many places in the 60s and 70s over the weekend and limited rain from the front. The forecast discussion also warns that dry fuels and winds behind the front could increase fire danger. These are regional forecasts, not observed conditions or a county-specific burn-ban change; check the latest forecast and [Mississippi Forestry Commission burn-ban list](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/) before making plans.

No separate, meaningful new **Union**, **Benton** or **Tippah** county development was verified for this edition. Tonight's North State volleyball matches had not produced a verified final score at publication time, so their previously reported schedules are not repeated as results.
