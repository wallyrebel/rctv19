The Compton Center is hosting a Holiday Open House on Sunday, November 1, from 12:00 to 4:00 p.m. at 109 School Street in Ripley.

The event will feature unique gift items, cheesecake, and giveaways. The Compton Center is one of several participating locations in the broader Holiday Open House celebration happening throughout Downtown Ripley and surrounding areas on the same day.
