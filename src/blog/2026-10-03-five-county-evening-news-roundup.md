Saturday evening's roundup covers new reports and results not included in our morning edition, including a substantive update in the Corinth shooting case. The arrest occurred Friday; the college football and volleyball results below are from Saturday. Sources were checked Saturday evening, Oct. 3. Times are Central.

## Alcorn County: Arrest in shooting investigation; anniversary concert canceled

Corinth police arrested **Bacarra Lamar Hill, 39, of Corinth**, on Friday in connection with the shooting investigation near Scott Street, [Alcorn County News reported Saturday](https://alcornnewsms.com/corinth-news/corinth-shooting-arrest-kidnapping-charge/). [WTVA's report](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/02/man-accused-kidnapping-shooting-victim/) confirms that he faces kidnapping and attempted murder charges and was captured during a traffic stop involving officers, detectives and the Corinth-Alcorn County SWAT team. Police allege he took the victim at gunpoint. The victim was flown to Regional One in Memphis. The charges are allegations; Hill is presumed innocent unless proven guilty. Alcorn County News said the victim's name and current condition had not been released.

The **Battle of Corinth anniversary concert scheduled for 5 p.m. Saturday at the Corinth Civil War Interpretive Center was canceled** because of the weather forecast, [Alcorn County News reported](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/battle-of-corinth-concert-canceled/), citing Shiloh National Military Park. The park had not scheduled a replacement date. This updates the concert plans carried in our Sept. 24 roundup.

## Prentiss County: Northeast loses by one; women's basketball schedule released

Northeast Mississippi Community College fell **28–27 at East Mississippi** on Saturday in Scooba, according to the [college's official box score](https://www.nemccathletics.com/sports/fball/2026-27/boxscores/20261003_wf5e.xml). The Booneville-based Tigers led 14–0 after the first quarter and trailed 28–21 at halftime. A third-quarter touchdown brought Northeast within a point, but its extra-point attempt failed. Neither team scored in the fourth quarter. Northeast is now 1–5 overall and 0–3 in conference play.

In a separate [Oct. 2 announcement](https://www.nemccathletics.com/sports/wbkb/2026-27/releases/20261002puh13p), newly highlighted by [Alcorn County News on Saturday](https://alcornnewsms.com/mississippi-news/nemcc-womens-basketball-schedule-2026-27/), the college released its women's basketball schedule with **14 home games** at Bonner Arnold Coliseum. The Lady Tigers open Oct. 30–31 at the Kragel Classic and play their first home game **Nov. 2 against Snead State**. Conference play begins Jan. 11 at Holmes. Fans should consult Northeast athletics for the complete schedule and game details.

## Tippah County: Ripley advances in volleyball playoffs

Ripley rallied to beat North Pontotoc at home Saturday in the first round of the Class 4A volleyball playoffs, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-sports/ripley-volleyball-beats-north-pontotoc/), citing Tippah County Sports. The Lady Tigers advance to face **Itawamba Agricultural High School on Tuesday, Oct. 6**. Match time and site were not confirmed in the report. Set scores were also not provided.

## Benton County: Hickory Flat advances; Ashland's season ends

In the Class 1A volleyball playoffs, Hickory Flat swept West Tallahatchie **3–0** Saturday, while Ashland lost **3–0 at Houlka**, according to [Tippah News' regional results](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-sports/ripley-volleyball-beats-north-pontotoc/). [MaxPreps also lists Ashland's 3–0 loss as final](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/ujjZCEoZFUG8uXUL1Ycmsw/volleyball-recap-tough-loss-for-ashland.htm). Hickory Flat advances; Ashland's playoff run is over.
