## Statewide result affecting all five counties

The Associated Press [called Mississippi's U.S. Senate race for Democrat Scott Colom on Wednesday](https://apnews.com/article/scott-colom-mississippi-senate-midterms-56c18f292a734443992f9466c3b0f346), after local officials spent several days processing absentee and affidavit ballots. Republican incumbent Cindy Hyde-Smith conceded later that day, the AP reports. Colom will be Mississippi's first Black U.S. senator since Reconstruction. The result affects Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties along with the rest of the state; this is a statewide result, not a separate county vote tally.
