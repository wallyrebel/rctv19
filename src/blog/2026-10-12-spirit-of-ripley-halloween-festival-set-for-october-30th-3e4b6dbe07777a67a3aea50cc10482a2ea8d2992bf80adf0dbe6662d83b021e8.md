# Spirit of Ripley Halloween Festival Set for October 30th

The Spirit of Ripley Halloween event is scheduled for Friday, October 30th at 5:30 PM in Historic Downtown Ripley. The celebration will feature Halloween festivities and a chili cookoff.

Organizers are currently accepting registrations for both Spirit booths and chili cookoff entries. Community members interested in participating should check [Ripley Main Street's event announcement on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/875713894854316/posts/1118351600590543) for registration instructions or contact the organizer through that post. The event organizers encourage downtown involvement and look forward to welcoming attendees to celebrate the Halloween season together.
