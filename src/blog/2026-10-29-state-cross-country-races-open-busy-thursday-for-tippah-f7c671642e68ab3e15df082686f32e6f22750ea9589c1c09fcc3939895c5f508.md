# State cross-country races open busy Thursday for Tippah teams

Three Tippah County schools are preparing for state cross-country competition Thursday, Oct. 29. Blue Mountain, Pine Grove, and Walnut will all be competing as part of what shapes up to be a busy day for local runners.
