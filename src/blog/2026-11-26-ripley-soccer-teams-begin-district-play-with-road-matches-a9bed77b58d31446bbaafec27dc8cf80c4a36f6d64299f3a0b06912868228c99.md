Ripley's girls and boys soccer teams begin district play with road doubleheaders against Byhalia on Monday, Nov. 30, and South Pontotoc on Friday, Dec. 4, according to Tippah County Sports. Girls play at 5 p.m. Central, followed by boys at 7 p.m.

The Ripley boys are 2-2 overall. They lost 7-0 at New Albany on Nov. 19, then defeated Saltillo 4-0 at home Nov. 21. The Tigers also beat Caledonia 3-2 and lost 3-2 at Pontotoc, scoring nine goals in four matches.

The girls open their fourth consecutive road match at Byhalia after playing at Caledonia, Pontotoc and New Albany. The reported results show the Tigers have not scored a goal in those matches.

Ripley's next scheduled home matches are against North Pontotoc on Dec. 11, with the girls playing at 5 p.m. and the boys at 7 p.m. Both teams host South Pontotoc in a district rematch Jan. 8.
