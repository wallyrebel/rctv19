Walnut responded to an earlier shutout with a convincing road win Friday night, defeating previously unbeaten Holly Springs 22-14. The victory was particularly notable as it reached a milestone for head coach John Meeks, who continues to build on his successful tenure with the program. The win demonstrates Walnut's resilience and ability to compete against top-tier opponents in their conference.
