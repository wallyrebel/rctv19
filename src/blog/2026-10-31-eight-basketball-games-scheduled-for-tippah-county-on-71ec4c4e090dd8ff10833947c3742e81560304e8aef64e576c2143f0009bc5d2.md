# Eight Basketball Games Scheduled for Tippah County on October 31

Eight basketball matchups are on the schedule today across Tippah County. Teams from Falkner, Ripley, Walnut, and Pine Grove will take the court for the games.

Walnut and Pine Grove are hosting girls basketball games at home to bring the action to local fans.
