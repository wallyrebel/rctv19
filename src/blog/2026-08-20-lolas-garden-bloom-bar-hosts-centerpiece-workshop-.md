# Lola's Garden Bloom Bar Centerpiece Workshop Coming This Saturday

Floral enthusiasts are invited to join Lola's Garden Bloom Bar for an exclusive centerpiece workshop this Saturday morning at 10:00 AM at The Cut-Off.

## Workshop Details

The hands-on floral design class offers participants the opportunity to create their own stunning centerpiece under expert guidance. All materials needed for the project are included in the workshop fee.

## Registration and Pricing

The workshop is priced at $35 per person. Due to high demand, organizers are encouraging interested participants to register soon, as available spots are filling up rapidly.

Those interested in attending can sign up through the official registration form, which is available online.

## About the Event

Lola's Garden Bloom Bar specializes in floral design and garden-related workshops, offering the community opportunities to learn and create with fresh flowers. This Saturday's centerpiece workshop is perfect for both beginners and those with some floral design experience looking to expand their skills.

Prospective attendees are advised to complete their registration promptly to secure their spot in this popular workshop.
