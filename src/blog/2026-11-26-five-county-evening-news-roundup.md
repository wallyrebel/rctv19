## Alcorn: Corinth to consider food truck ordinance

The Corinth Board of Mayor and Aldermen is scheduled to consider adopting a food truck ordinance at **5 p.m. Tuesday, Dec. 1**, at City Hall, 300 Childs St., according to the [city's posted agenda](https://www.corinthms.gov/post/december-1st-2026-agenda) and [Alcorn County News](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-food-truck-ordinance-vote/). The agenda lists the vote but does not provide the proposed ordinance text, so permit rules and other details are not yet confirmed. The board is also scheduled to discuss a sign ordinance and property cleanup cases.

## Tippah: Falkner High plans blood drive

Falkner High School and Vitalant plan a blood drive **Wednesday, Dec. 2, from 8:30 a.m. to 1 p.m.**, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/falkner-high-school-blood-drive/), citing the school's announcement. The school directs prospective donors to a Vitalant appointment link in its Facebook post. The report notes that an attached flyer mistakenly says *Tuesday, Dec. 2*; the school's written notice says Wednesday, which matches the calendar. Donors who want to confirm the schedule can contact the school before traveling.

## Tippah: Walnut parade entry deadline approaches

The Town of Walnut says entries for its Christmas parade are due by **4 p.m. Tuesday, Dec. 1**, [Alcorn County News reported](https://alcornnewsms.com/mississippi-news/walnut-christmas-parade-entries-due/). The parade is scheduled for 7 p.m. Friday, Dec. 4. Organizers direct entrants to the [town's events page](https://www.walnut.ms/events) or Town Hall for registration. The report says each entry is asked to bring a new toy for a local child.

## Tippah: Ripley soccer opens district play on the road

Ripley's girls' and boys' varsity soccer teams are scheduled to play at Byhalia on **Monday, Nov. 30**, then at South Pontotoc on **Friday, Dec. 4**, [Tippah County Sports reported](https://tippahsports.com/tippah-county-sports/ripley-soccer-teams-open-district-play-with-road-doubleheaders/). The report lists the girls' matches for 5 p.m. and boys' matches for 7 p.m. These are upcoming games, not results.

## Union, Prentiss and Benton
