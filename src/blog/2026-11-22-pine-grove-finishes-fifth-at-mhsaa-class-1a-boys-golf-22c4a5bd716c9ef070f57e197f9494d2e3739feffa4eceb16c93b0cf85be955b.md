Pine Grove finished fifth at the MHSAA Class 1A Boys Golf Championship, shooting 342 in the first round and 354 in the second for a combined 696 at Dogwoods Golf Course in Grenada.

Trevor Shinall and Noah Howell tied for 16th place with scores of 169. Vince Bryant placed 29th at 178, Kendall Jumper finished 32nd at 180, Riggs Childs tied for 36th at 188, and Talen Wilkerson placed 45th at 212.

Tupelo Christian and Vardaman tied for the championship with 644 strokes, with Tupelo Christian listed first in the official standings. Gage Wisinger of Bogue Chitto won the individual title with an even-par 144.
