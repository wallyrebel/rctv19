A new destination is coming to downtown Ripley at Brown & Covington. The venue will feature locally made handmade goods, creative activities, gelato, and community gathering space.

The project aims to showcase local artisans and provide a shopping and entertainment destination for residents. More details about the opening date and featured artisans will be shared as the venue prepares to launch.
