Corinth's mayoral election result and newly posted state volleyball final times lead this Wednesday morning's update for Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties. The final match schedule adds practical details to the teams' previously reported North Half wins; those wins are not new results today.

## Alcorn: WTVA reports Dance reelected Corinth mayor

Incumbent **Ralph Dance won reelection** in Corinth's Tuesday, Oct. 13, mayoral vote, [WTVA reported Tuesday night](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/14/corinth-mayor-wins-reelection/). The station identified Nestly Gates, Tommy Irwin and Taylor Coombs Moak as the other candidates. The election outcome follows the voting information previously covered in RCTV 19's Oct. 11 roundup.

## Union and Alcorn: state volleyball final times posted

The [Mississippi High School Activities Association's championship schedule](https://www.misshsaa.com/2026/10/13/2026-mhsaa-volleyball-championships/) now lists **East Union vs. Enterprise Clarke at noon Thursday, Oct. 15**, in the Class 2A final. On **Friday, Oct. 16**, it lists **Kossuth vs. Our Lady Academy at 10 a.m.** in Class 3A, **Ingomar vs. Resurrection at noon** in Class 1A, and **New Albany vs. Choctaw Central at 2 p.m.** in Class 4A. All four matches are scheduled for A.E. Wood Coliseum at Mississippi College in Clinton. The association's page has ticket and livestream links; readers should check it for any schedule changes before traveling.

No separate meaningful new local development was verified this morning for **Prentiss, Benton or Tippah** counties. Tippah's previously reported burn ban and Walnut volleyball result are not being recast as new items.
