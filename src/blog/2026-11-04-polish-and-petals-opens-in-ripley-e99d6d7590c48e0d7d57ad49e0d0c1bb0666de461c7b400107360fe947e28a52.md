# Polish & Petals Opens in Ripley

Polish & Petals has officially opened its doors in Ripley. The new business is located at 1703 City Avenue North Suite C, Ripley, MS 38663.

The business is now accepting both walk-in customers and appointments. According to the announcement, the owners expressed gratitude to everyone who supported and encouraged them throughout the process of opening the business.
