## Tippah: Ripley plans Veterans Day program Wednesday

[Tippah News reported Monday evening](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/veterans-day-program-set-for-nov-11-at-ripley-sportsplex/) that Ripley Main Street announced a Veterans Day program for **Wednesday, Nov. 11, at 11 a.m.** at the Ripley Sportsplex. The notice says the Ripley Elementary Gifted Class, Ripley High School Band and South Tippah Choir will provide music. A picnic lunch of hamburgers and hot dogs will be available for $5. For questions, the notice directs residents to American Legion Post 81 at 662-321-0221. This is an upcoming event, not a report that it has taken place.

## Union: Thirteen New Albany teachers receive grants

Thirteen New Albany School District teachers received grants through the Dean Provence Endowment for Excellence in Education, [Union County News reported Monday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/new-albany-teachers-receive-education-grants/), citing a district social media post. The district said the awards will support classroom projects and educational opportunities; the report did not give individual grant amounts.

## Prentiss: Investigators report county shop burglary charges

Prentiss County investigators charged a Booneville man with commercial burglary and grand larceny after a break-in at a county shop near Booneville, [WTVA reported Monday](https://www.wtva.com/2026/11/09/sheriff-man-arrested-theft-county-property/), citing the sheriff's department. The station reported that a county-owned pickup taken from the shop was later found abandoned in Helena, Arkansas, and that the man was arrested in Panola County. These are allegations; he is presumed innocent unless proven guilty.
