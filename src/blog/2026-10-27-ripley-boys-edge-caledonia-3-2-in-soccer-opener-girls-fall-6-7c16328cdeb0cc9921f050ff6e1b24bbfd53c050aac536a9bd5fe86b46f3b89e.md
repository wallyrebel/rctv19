Ripley's boys soccer team began their season on a winning note Monday, defeating Caledonia 3–2 in their opening match. The Tigers' one-goal victory marked a successful start to the campaign.

In girls soccer, Ripley's team faced a more difficult opening, falling to Caledonia 6–0 in their season debut.
