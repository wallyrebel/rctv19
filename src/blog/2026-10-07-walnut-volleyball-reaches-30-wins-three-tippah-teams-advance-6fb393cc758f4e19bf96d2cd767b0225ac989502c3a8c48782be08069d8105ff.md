Walnut High School volleyball marked a significant evening on Tuesday, Oct. 6, celebrating two senior milestones while the team secured its 30th victory of the season. The achievement also resulted in the team advancing further in competition.

The victory was part of a strong showing by Tippah County volleyball programs, as three teams from the area advanced during the same period.
