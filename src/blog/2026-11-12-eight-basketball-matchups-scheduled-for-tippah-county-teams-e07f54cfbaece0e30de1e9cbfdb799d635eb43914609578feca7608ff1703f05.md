# Eight Basketball Matchups Scheduled for Tippah County Teams Today

Tippah County basketball teams are set for an active Thursday with eight matchups on the schedule. Ripley, Walnut, and Blue Mountain will host home games.

Girls' games are scheduled to tip off at 6 p.m., with boys' games to follow.
