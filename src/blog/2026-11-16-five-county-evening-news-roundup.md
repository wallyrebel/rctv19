## Alcorn: Renovated surgical entrance opens in Corinth

Magnolia Regional Health Center's renovated Surgical Services entrance is now open, [Alcorn County News reported Monday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/magnolia-regional-surgery-renovation-opens/). The hospital held a ribbon cutting Nov. 10; the change in patient arrival instructions was reported in today's local account. Patients arriving for scheduled surgery, labor induction or cesarean section are directed to Entrance E off Alcorn Drive. The report says the lobby is open weekdays from 5 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.; patients arriving outside those hours for those scheduled services should use the Emergency Department entrance. Patients should confirm their own arrival instructions with their care team. The [hospital described the renovation plans in May](https://www.mrhc.org/blog/news/advancing-surgical-care-at-magnolia-regional-health-center/), including larger surgical suites and a redesigned drop-off and waiting area.

## Tippah: Ripley announces Pink Friday hours

Ripley Main Street announced a Pink Friday shopping event for Nov. 20 from 4 to 7 p.m. at downtown and other participating shops around town, [Tippah News reported Monday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/ripley-pink-friday-holiday-shopping/). Individual stores set their own specials and hours, so shoppers should check with a store before visiting. The organization also listed Black Friday on Nov. 27 and Shop Small Saturday on Nov. 28; specific hours for those dates had not been posted in the report.

No distinct, meaningful new Union, Prentiss or Benton County development was verified for this edition.
