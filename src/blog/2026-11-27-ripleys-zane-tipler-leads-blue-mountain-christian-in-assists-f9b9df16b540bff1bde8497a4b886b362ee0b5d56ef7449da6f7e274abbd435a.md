Ripley native Zane Tipler leads Blue Mountain Christian University's men's basketball team with 17 assists through its first six games, according to Tippah County Sports.

The junior guard has started four games and averaged 21 minutes, totaling 25 points, 15 rebounds and five steals. Tipler holds a three-assist lead over teammate Seck Zongo and four assists ahead of Jeremiah Hayes.

In Blue Mountain's 91-84 victory over Bethel on Nov. 24, Tipler recorded two assists, two points, two rebounds and a steal in 16 minutes. The Toppers led 48-43 at halftime to improve to 3-3.

Blue Mountain is scheduled to host Point on Dec. 3 at 7:30 p.m. and Life on Dec. 5 at 2 p.m.
