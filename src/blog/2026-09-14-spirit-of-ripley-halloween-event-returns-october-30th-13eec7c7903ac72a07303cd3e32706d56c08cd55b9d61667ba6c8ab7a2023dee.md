# Spirit of Ripley Halloween Event Returns October 30th

The community is invited to join Spirit of Ripley on **October 30th at 5:30 PM in Historic Downtown Ripley**.

## Chili Cookoff
Participants are encouraged to enter the Chili Cookoff by bringing their best recipe to compete for bragging rights. All skill levels are welcome to participate.

## Spirit Booths
Businesses, churches, organizations, clubs, teams, and community groups are invited to set up booths throughout downtown to provide Halloween entertainment and activities for attendees.

## How to Participate
Interested participants can sign up for either the Chili Cookoff or a Spirit Booth through the registration link provided. Organizers are seeking numerous entries for both categories to make the 2026 event the largest yet.

For more information and to register, visit the official signup page.
