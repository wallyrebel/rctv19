# Chula's & Scoops November Promotion

Two local favorites are partnering for a November promotion. Every Thursday from 2–4 PM, customers who make a purchase at Chula's (located at The Cut-Off) can receive a free scoop of gelato at Brown & Covington, which is situated directly across the street.

To claim the free gelato, customers simply need to bring their receipt from Chula's to Brown & Covington during the promotion window.
