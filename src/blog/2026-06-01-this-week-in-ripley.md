## Heat & Wellness Recovery Spa Joins Ripley's Main Street Moolah Program

*June 2, 2026*

<img src="/assets/optimized/63070a9b3a679c895b54-960.webp" srcset="/assets/optimized/63070a9b3a679c895b54-480.webp 480w, /assets/optimized/63070a9b3a679c895b54-960.webp 960w" sizes="(max-width: 960px) 100vw, 900px" alt="Heat &amp; Wellness Recovery Spa Joins Ripley's Main Street Moolah Program" loading="lazy">

Ripley Main Street announced the addition of Heat & Wellness Recovery Spa to its growing roster of participating businesses accepting Main Street Moolah, the community's local currency initiative.

The expansion brings wellness and recovery services into the Main Street Moolah ecosystem, allowing residents to direct their local currency toward relaxation and health-focused treatments while simultaneously supporting a local business.

### Supporting Community Commerce

Main Street Moolah operates as a community investment tool designed to keep dollars circulating within Ripley's local economy. By accepting the currency, Heat & Wellness Recovery Spa joins other local merchants in strengthening the town's commercial district and fostering economic resilience.

"We're excited to welcome Heat & Wellness Recovery Spa to the growing list of businesses that accept Ripley Main Street Moolah," organizers stated. "Now you can use your Main Street Moolah toward relaxation, recovery, and wellness while supporting a local business."

### How to Participate

Residents interested in purchasing Main Street Moolah can order through the program's official platform. The initiative encourages community members to shop locally and invest directly in Ripley's economic development.

Program organizers emphasized gratitude for community participation, stating: "Thank you for shopping local and investing in our community!"

For more information about Main Street Moolah or to place an order, residents can visit the program's official ordering page.

## Get All Shook Up: Elvis Bingo Night Coming to The Cut-Off

*June 3, 2026*

<img src="/assets/optimized/96efbf0ce1c773ccbdcb-960.webp" srcset="/assets/optimized/96efbf0ce1c773ccbdcb-480.webp 480w, /assets/optimized/96efbf0ce1c773ccbdcb-960.webp 960w" sizes="(max-width: 960px) 100vw, 900px" alt="Get All Shook Up: Elvis Bingo Night Coming to The Cut-Off" loading="lazy">

Fans of the King of Rock and Roll are invited to lace up their blue suede shoes and head to The Cut-Off for an evening of Elvis-themed entertainment and games.

### Event Details

The Cut-Off, located at 204 S. Commerce Street in downtown Ripley, will host the special bingo event on **Thursday, June 12 at 7:00 PM**. The evening promises to be filled with classic Elvis music, themed fun, and plenty of opportunities to win prizes.

### What to Expect

Attendees can look forward to:

- **Multiple prize giveaways** throughout the night
- **Bingo games** designed for players of all ages
- **Classic Elvis music** and entertainment
- **Themed atmosphere** celebrating the legendary performer

### Who Should Attend

The event is **free to play** and welcomes everyone—families, friends, and Elvis enthusiasts of all ages are encouraged to attend. Organizers are inviting guests to "bring your friends, bring your family, and come see if you can leave all shook up... with a prize in hand!"

For more information, follow Ripley Main Street on social media or visit The Cut-Off directly.
