Thursday morning's edition covers developments verified after the Oct. 7 evening roundup. Event plans and weather forecasts can change. Times are Central.

## All five counties: Isaias strengthens; local weekend outlook updated

The [National Hurricane Center's 4 a.m. Thursday advisory](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/archive/2026/al09/al092026.public.007.shtml) classified Isaias as a **hurricane with maximum sustained winds of 80 mph** over the Gulf. That is the storm's measured intensity there, **not** a wind forecast for Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton or Tippah counties.

In a [graphic updated at 4:15 a.m. Thursday](https://www.weather.gov/images/meg/graphicast/4.png), **NWS Memphis** expects Isaias's weakening remnants to reach the Tennessee River Valley Saturday. Its Mid-South outlook calls for periods of heavy rain, **1 to 3 inches of rainfall with locally higher amounts near the Tennessee River**, and gusts up to **35 mph**. These are regional forecasts, not county rainfall totals or observed impacts. Check the [latest NWS Memphis outlook](https://www.weather.gov/meg/events/latest_storm/) as the track and timing change.

## Tippah County: Supervisors discuss animal shelter partnership

[Tippah News reported Wednesday evening](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-county-supervisors-lazy-h-shelter/) that supervisors discussed a possible arrangement with Ripley's Lazy H dog shelter at their Monday meeting. They considered questions such as capacity and how long animals might stay, but **took no formal action**. The board also left the county burn ban in place through Oct. 18 and expects to revisit it at its Oct. 15 meeting. The forecast rain does not itself end the ban.

## Union County: New Albany pageant applications open

[Union County News reported Thursday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/applications-open-for-2027-new-albany-miss-hospitality-pageant/) that applications are open for the **2027 New Albany Miss Hospitality and Miss Teen New Albany** pageants. The report links separate online application forms; [New Albany Main Street](https://www.newalbanymainstreet.com/newalbanymisshospitality) describes the titles as local tourism ambassador roles. The report does not state an application deadline.

## Alcorn County: Free seed harvest planned for Oct. 13

[Alcorn County News reported Wednesday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-free-wildflower-seeds/) that Keep Corinth-Alcorn Beautiful plans a free wildflower seed harvest **Tuesday, Oct. 13, from 5 to 7 p.m. or until dark** at the Margaret F. Shackleford Memorial Garden on Fulton Drive behind Kroger in Corinth. Organizers plan to provide bags and instructions. This is a scheduled event, not a report that the harvest has taken place.

## Prentiss and Benton counties

No additional meaningful, verified county-specific update was found for **Prentiss or Benton** since the previous roundup. Both are included in the regional weather outlook above.
