# Ripley Christmas Parade Registration Now Open

The Ripley Main Street Association has opened registration for its upcoming Christmas Parade with the theme "Christmas Through the Decades."

Participants are encouraged to select their favorite decade—whether the 1950s, 1970s, 1980s, or 1990s—and bring that era to life in their parade entries. The association is seeking floats, classic and antique cars and trucks, marching bands, pageant winners, churches, businesses, clubs, and other organizations to participate.

Classic cars are highlighted as particularly fitting for this year's decade-themed celebration. Registration is now available online through the Ripley Main Street Association's sign-up portal.
