Foote, a Blue Mountain native and former Blue Mountain High School player, has come off the bench in every game while playing 136 minutes. She has 15 offensive rebounds and 32 defensive rebounds, along with 39 points scored.

In her most recent notable performance against Faulkner on Nov. 14, Foote scored 15 points with six rebounds in 31 minutes, making three of seven 3-point attempts. She also had five defensive rebounds against Central Baptist and six rebounds (including two offensive) at Wesleyan on Nov. 21.

Blue Mountain Christian hosts Point on Thursday, Dec. 3, at 5:30 p.m. Central.
