Brown & Covington, a new shop on Ripley Main Street, celebrated its opening day today with a Holiday Open House from 12:00 to 4:00 p.m.

Located at 301 S. Commerce Street, the shop offers locally-made goods and gelato. Visitors were invited to explore the new storefront and support local makers during the opening celebration.
