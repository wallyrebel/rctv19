Monday evening's roundup for Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties focuses on a newly announced manufacturing plan, newly reported school attendance figures and a change in Tippah's state burn-ban listing. The router agreement is preliminary; no new jobs or production start date have been confirmed.

## Alcorn: Corinth named in router assembly plan

[TP-Link announced Monday](https://www.tp-link.com/us/press/news/22580/) that it signed a **letter of intent** with Key Tronic Corp. for domestic assembly of networking products at Key Tronic's Corinth operation. TP-Link says Wi-Fi 8 home networking products could be assembled there **as early as 2027, pending U.S. regulatory approval**. A letter of intent does not establish that production has begun or that the companies have signed a final manufacturing contract. The announcement gave no Corinth hiring figure for this proposed work. [Alcorn County News](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/key-tronic-tp-link-router-assembly-corinth/) also reported the local connection Monday.

## Alcorn: School absence rates decline

The share of Corinth School District students who were chronically absent in 2025–26 fell to **28.1% from 37.8%** the prior year, [Alcorn County News reported Monday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-schools-chronic-absenteeism-drops/), citing the state education department's report and the *Daily Corinthian*. The report also puts Alcorn School District at **20.3%, down from 26.3%**. Chronic absenteeism means missing at least 10% of the school year for any reason. These are school-year figures newly reported locally, not a change measured on Monday.

## Tippah: State burn-ban listing changes; fire danger persists

[Tippah News reported Monday morning](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/tippah-county-burn-ban-off-state-list/) that Tippah County no longer appeared on the Mississippi Forestry Commission's active burn-ban map and list after a Sunday night update. The [Forestry Commission says](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/burning-info/burn-bans/) county bans expire at midnight on their stated expiration date. Its page did not provide an explanation for Tippah's removal, and the absence of a county from the state list does not confirm whether a separate local restriction applies. Residents should check with county officials before burning.

Dry conditions remain a concern. In its [Monday 11:59 a.m. forecast discussion](https://forecast.weather.gov/product.php?issuedby=MEG&product=AFD&site=MEG), the National Weather Service in Memphis said elevated fire danger would continue each afternoon through much of the week and that a fire danger statement was in effect until **7 p.m. Monday**. The weather service urged people to obey any local burn bans and dispose of cigarettes and matches safely.

No separate meaningful new **Union, Prentiss or Benton County** development with enough verified detail was found for this edition. Monday morning's Union County cross-country result and earlier coverage of the Booneville meal and Benton school news are not repeated here.
