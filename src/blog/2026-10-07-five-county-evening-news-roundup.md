Wednesday evening's edition covers updates verified since the morning roundup. The volleyball matches took place Tuesday, Oct. 6; their detailed Tippah County report appeared Wednesday. Forecasts may change. Times are Central.

## Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties: Weekend storm outlook

In a graphic **updated at 1:31 p.m. Wednesday**, [NWS Memphis](https://www.weather.gov/meg/events/latest_storm/) said the remnants of Tropical Storm Isaias are expected to move into the Mid-South Saturday. The office forecasts periods of heavy rain Saturday into Sunday, with **1.5 to 3 inches widely expected, locally higher totals possible, and gusts up to 35 mph mainly in north Mississippi and west Tennessee**. These are regional forecasts, not measured rainfall or wind in any of the five counties. The storm's path and local effects may change; residents should check the [latest NWS Memphis outlook](https://www.weather.gov/meg/events/latest_storm/) before weekend travel or events.

The [National Hurricane Center's 1 p.m. advisory](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/archive/2026/al09/al092026.public_a.004.shtml) placed Isaias in the Gulf with maximum sustained winds near **50 mph**. That figure describes the storm at the time of the advisory, **not** a forecast wind speed for north Mississippi.

## Tippah County: Three teams advance in volleyball playoffs

[Tippah County Sports reported Wednesday](https://tippahsports.com/ripley/walnut-reaches-30-wins-as-three-tippah-volleyball-teams-advance/) that three county teams won their Tuesday second-round matches. [Walnut swept East Webster 3–0](https://www.maxpreps.com/ms/volleyball/match/east-webster-maben-vs-walnut/10-6-2026/?c=d917f845-040d-42e4-a258-4671e69e1a9a), giving the Wildcats a **30–4** record. [Ripley beat Itawamba Agricultural 3–1](https://www.maxpreps.com/ms/volleyball/match/itawamba-agricultural-fulton-vs-ripley/10-6-2026/?c=2580c19b-691a-4acb-9e8a-3512628d677a), and [Pine Grove swept West Union 3–0](https://www.maxpreps.com/ms/volleyball/match/pine-grove-ripley-vs-west-union-myrtle/10-6-2026/?c=6ba6ee2c-1bab-4cb8-9d18-158d460a79a7).

All three are due back Thursday, Oct. 8, according to Tippah County Sports: Ripley hosts New Albany at **6 p.m.**, Pine Grove visits Ingomar, and Walnut faces Myrtle. Check with the schools for any schedule change as the weekend weather outlook develops. The morning roundup had already reported New Albany's advancement and Ingomar's scheduled match; these Tippah results add the scores that were unavailable then.
