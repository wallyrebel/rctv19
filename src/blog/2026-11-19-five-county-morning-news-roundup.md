## Tippah: 22 Blue Mountain Christian athletes earn academic honors

Twenty-two Blue Mountain Christian University athletes were named 2026 NAIA Fall Sports Scholar-Athletes, [the university's athletic department announced Wednesday afternoon](https://bmcusports.com/news/2026/11/18/bmc-22-toppers-named-2026-naia-fall-scholar-athletes.aspx). The honorees compete in men's and women's cross country, men's and women's soccer, and volleyball. The recognition requires at least sophomore academic standing, a cumulative GPA of 3.5 or higher, and a full year at the institution. The university's release names all 22 athletes.

## Tippah: Ripley seniors visit Mississippi State

Ripley High School seniors recently toured Mississippi State University to learn about admissions, scholarships, housing and campus life, [Tippah News reported Wednesday evening](https://tippahnews.com/ripley-ms-news/ripley-high-seniors-mississippi-state/), citing a school post shared that day. The school did not specify the date of the trip in the report. Tippah News also reported a separate physical therapy job-shadowing experience for a Ripley student.
