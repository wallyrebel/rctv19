Tuesday evening's roundup covers county-specific reports published after the morning edition. The road work and museum performances are planned; neither has happened yet. Sources were checked Tuesday afternoon, Oct. 6. Times are Central.

## Benton County: Jay Kimery appointed EMA director

The Benton County Board of Supervisors has appointed **Jay Kimery** as the county's new emergency management director, [TVMO News Network reported Tuesday](https://tvmocommunitynews.substack.com/p/jay-kimery-appointed-new-ema-director). TVMO says Kimery volunteers with the Ashland and Snow Lake fire departments and will work with the retiring director during a transition over the coming weeks. [Tippah News also reported the appointment](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/benton-county-ema-director-kimery/). Neither report provides an exact date for Kimery to take over fully.

## Alcorn County: Supervisors approve Farmington Road paving contract

Alcorn County supervisors approved a **$406,406.73** State Aid paving contract Monday for a little more than one mile of Farmington Road in the county's Second District, [Alcorn County News reported Tuesday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/farmington-road-paving-contract-alcorn/), citing the Daily Corinthian. The contract went to Cook & Son Construction of Smithville. The report does not give a start date or traffic plan, so approval should not be mistaken for work already underway.

## Alcorn County: Corinth Angel Tree applications due Friday

Families seeking Christmas assistance through the Salvation Army's Corinth Angel Tree program must return applications by **3 p.m. Friday, Oct. 9**, according to [Alcorn County News' Tuesday report](https://alcornnewsms.com/corinth-news/corinth-angel-tree-applications-due/) citing the Daily Corinthian. Packets are available at the Salvation Army office at **2200 Lackey Drive in Corinth** and must be returned there. Families should ask the office what documentation their application requires; the report does not give a definitive local checklist.

## Union County: Heritage Museum announces November dinner theater

The Union County Heritage Museum plans a **“Rainey Night” Mystery Dinner Theater for Nov. 6 and 7**, [Union County News reported Tuesday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/union-county-heritage-museum-hosts-rainey-night-mystery-dinner-theater-in-november/). Its report says Blue Mountain Christian University Theater Players will perform a five-act mystery alongside a four-course meal. Organizers are limiting each performance to 50 tickets, according to the report; readers can use its linked ticket forms for details and availability.

## Prentiss and Tippah counties

No additional meaningful, verified county-specific development was found for Prentiss County. A Tippah volleyball schedule was posted separately today, but no game result was available at this afternoon's source check, so this edition does not repeat the schedule or imply a result.
