A Benton County photography exhibit, Blue Mountain Christian golf results and a Corinth housing approval lead this Wednesday evening's update for Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties. The Corinth board action itself took place last week and was reported Wednesday.

## Benton: Charlie Cox photographs on view in Ashland

The **Charlie Cox Collection** is on display at the Benton County Historical Courthouse and Museum in Ashland **through Nov. 2**, [Tippah News reported Wednesday morning](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/charlie-cox-collection-benton-county-museum/), citing the Southern Sentinel. Check with the museum for current visiting hours before going. The [University of Mississippi Libraries' collection description](https://egrove.olemiss.edu/ccox/) says Cox's glass-plate photographs document early-1900s life in Ashland and surrounding Benton County. The university holds the original negatives and makes digitized images available online.

## Tippah: Blue Mountain Christian golf teams place first, second and third

At the CBC Fall Invite in Maumelle, Arkansas, on Monday and Tuesday, **Blue Mountain Christian University's women's A team won the team title and its B team finished second**, according to [the university's Wednesday report](https://bmcusports.com/news/2026/10/14/womens-golf-bmcu-womens-golf-claims-top-two-spots-at-cbc-fall-invite.aspx). The A team shot 638 over two rounds; the B team shot 666. Ansley Pratt won the individual title with a 154. In the men's event, [BMCU Athletics reported](https://bmcusports.com/news/2026/10/14/mens-golf-bmcu-mens-golf-earns-third-place-finish-at-cbc-fall-invite.aspx) that the Toppers finished **third of nine teams**, with Damil Ataullah third individually. The university's results add detail to [Tippah News' county sports roundup](https://tippahnews.com/sports/tippah-county-sports-roundup-oct-14-2026-bmcu-women-win-golf-title-men-finish-third/).

## Alcorn: Corinth approves zoning for 10 Washington Street homes

The Corinth Board of Mayor and Aldermen granted zoning approvals for **10 planned single-family homes** near 1217 Washington Street at a meeting last week, [Alcorn County News reported Wednesday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-washington-street-homes/), citing the Daily Corinthian. The report says five homes would face Washington Street and five would face Tishomingo Street. Zoning approval does not mean construction has begun; no construction date or home prices were reported.

No separate meaningful new development with enough specific, verifiable detail was found this evening for **Union or Prentiss** counties.
