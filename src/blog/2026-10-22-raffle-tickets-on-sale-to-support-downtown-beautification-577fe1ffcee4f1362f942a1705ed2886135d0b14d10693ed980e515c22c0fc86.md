# Raffle Tickets on Sale to Support Downtown Beautification

Raffle tickets are now available for purchase to support downtown beautification projects in Ripley. Tickets are priced at $5 each or 5 for $20, with sales ending October 30 at 5:00 PM.

Prizes include up to $500 in cash, a Yeti cooler with accessories from Alfa, two steaks from Freemans & Co., a $75 oil change from Ford Place, a Turtle Box from West Glass, services from Big Jim's Car Wash, and gift cards from local shops and restaurants.

Winners will be announced every 30 minutes during the Spirit of Ripley Festival, and winners do not need to be present to claim their prizes.

The raffle is supported by donations from local businesses including Chickasaw Equipment, Freemans & Co., Ford Place, Big Jim's Car Wash, West Glass, and Alfa. Cash prize sponsors include Farm Bureau, CB&S Bank, and West Glass.
