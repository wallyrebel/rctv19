## Prentiss: State archaeologists visit Booneville cannonball site

Staff from the Mississippi Department of Archives and History visited the Booneville property where more than 200 Civil War-era cannonballs were found this summer, [WTVA reports](https://www.wtva.com/2026/11/11/work-continues-site-where-civil-war-era-cannonballs-found/). State Rep. Tracy Arnold said the visit took place Tuesday, Nov. 10. MDAH's Cindy Davis said the next step is to assign the location an archaeological site number. She hopes to work with landowner Harold Knight on a possible landmark designation and contact preservation organizations. The site's historical role remains under study; no landmark designation has been announced.

## Union: Ingomar students recognize veterans

Ingomar Attendance Center held a Veterans Day parade and program for veterans, students and the public, [Union County News reports](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/ingomar-students-honor-veterans-with-parade-and-program/), citing the school's post. Fourth graders presented the annual program. The account was published Thursday about this week's observance; it is not a notice of a future event.

## Tippah: Walnut player named to All-Area volleyball team

Walnut's Molly-Kate Rolison was named to the *Northeast Mississippi Daily Journal* All-Area volleyball first team, [Tippah News reports](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/walnut-volleyball-rolison-all-area/), citing *The Southern Sentinel*. The report says Rolison was also Region 1-2A MVP and recorded 453 kills this season. The Daily Journal announced its team Nov. 7; this is recognition of the completed season, not a new game result.
