Tuesday morning's check found a new Northeast Mississippi Community College golf result and two local reports not covered in Monday evening's roundup. The parade and election details below are upcoming plans and voting information, not reports that those events have concluded.

## Union County: New Albany homecoming parade set for Thursday

New Albany High School's homecoming parade is scheduled for **6 p.m. Thursday, Oct. 1**, [Union County News reported Monday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/new-albany-high-school-hosts-homecoming-parade-and-pep-rally-thursday/), citing school officials. The announced route begins at the Union County Library, follows Main and Bankhead streets, and ends at the Union County Courthouse. A community pep rally is planned immediately afterward, with food trucks available. Residents should check the school's latest notices for any schedule change.

## Prentiss County: Northeast golf fifth after first round

Northeast Mississippi Community College stood **fifth after the first round** of the NJCAA South District Preview, [Prentiss County News reported Tuesday](https://prentissnews.com/local-news/northeast-mississippi-community-college-places-fifth-after-first-round-of-njcaa-south-district-preview/), citing the college. Andie Kate Ramey, Ally Westmoreland and Lynden Williams were each inside the individual top 20 after day one. These are interim standings; the tournament is continuing.

## Alcorn County: Corinth mayoral absentee voting continues

Eligible voters can cast absentee ballots for Corinth's **Oct. 13 municipal election** at City Hall, 300 Childs Street, during regular business hours, [Alcorn County News reported Monday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-mayoral-race-absentee-voting/). The report, citing the Daily Corinthian, said **107 absentee ballots had been cast by early Friday afternoon, Sept. 25**. That count is a Friday snapshot, not a Tuesday total. The city election is separate from the Nov. 3 general election.

## Benton and Tippah counties

No meaningful new county-specific development was verified for this edition. Earlier burn-ban reports, the voter-registration deadline and weekend sports results have already been covered; residents should check current agency notices for any change to restrictions.
