Saturday evening's check found newly reported Friday scores, a Saturday report on a Ripley industrial property, and a public-safety update. The drought map was **released Thursday using Tuesday's data**; the Benton and Tippah burn bans were issued earlier in September and remain in effect. These are status updates, not new Saturday orders.

## Union, Prentiss and Tippah counties: Friday football results

Falkner beat **East Union 42–34** on Friday night in Tippah County, according to [Tippah County Sports' game report](https://tippahsports.com/ripley/adams-scores-four-times-as-falkner-edges-east-union-ripley-falls-to-booneville/). Ethan Adams scored four touchdowns for Falkner. The report says Cade Alston forced a fourth-quarter fumble and Jonis Flynn scored the deciding touchdown. The result gives Union County's East Union a loss and Tippah County's Falkner a homecoming win.

In another game linking two of the five counties, Prentiss County's **Booneville beat Ripley 31–21**. [Tippah County Sports](https://tippahsports.com/ripley/adams-scores-four-times-as-falkner-edges-east-union-ripley-falls-to-booneville/) reports that Ripley's Kyran McDonald rushed for 110 yards and passed for 109, while Booneville's Tony Ogden scored three rushing touchdowns. These are Friday results reported Saturday, not Saturday games.

## Alcorn County: Severe drought expands

The [U.S. Drought Monitor map released Sept. 24](https://droughtmonitor.unl.edu/) places much of Alcorn County in severe drought. [Alcorn County News' Saturday report](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/alcorn-county-severe-drought-burn-bans/) gives the county figure as **85.7% in severe drought**, up from none the prior week. It also reports all of Prentiss County in at least moderate drought, with 12.8% in severe drought. Those figures describe the map's **Sept. 22** conditions; they are not a live reading of conditions this evening.

## Benton and Tippah counties: Burn bans still active

[Tippah News reported Saturday](https://tippahnews.com/public-service/tippah-benton-burn-ban-drought/) that the previously issued county burn bans remain in place: Benton County's is scheduled through **Oct. 8**, and Tippah County's through **Oct. 18**. The [Mississippi Forestry Commission explains](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/burning-info/burn-bans/) that a burn ban prohibits outdoor fires that produce embers, including campfires and debris burning. Check the commission's current ban page before burning; orders can change.

## Tippah County: County-owned industrial building marketed

The Tippah County Development Foundation is marketing a **20,000-square-foot** county-owned building at **405 County Road 565 in Ripley**, [Tippah News reported Saturday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/ripley-industrial-building-available/). The report says it has office and manufacturing space, three dock-high loading doors and a layout that could serve two tenants. This is an available-property announcement; the report does not identify a new tenant or job commitment.
