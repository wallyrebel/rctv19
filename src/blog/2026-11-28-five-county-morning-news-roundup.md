## Tippah: December trade day dates set in Ripley

Ripley First Monday Trade Day has listed its final market weekend of 2026 for **Saturday, Dec. 5, from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.** and **Sunday, Dec. 6, from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m.**, [Tippah News reported Friday evening](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/ripley-first-monday-december/). The market grounds are at 10590 Highway 15 South. The report says organizers posted events for both days and are promoting holiday shopping with local vendors.

## Tippah: Paynes Chapel plans Christmas comedy

Paynes Chapel Community Church in the Dumas area plans to present *Christmas at the Inn: Zero Stars at Bethlehem Booking.com* at **5 p.m. Sunday, Dec. 13**, at 4521 County Road 611, [Tippah News reported Friday evening](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/paynes-chapel-christmas-play-ripley/). The church's public event notice describes it as a comedy Christmas play. The report says the listing does not give ticket or admission details; contact the church for questions.
