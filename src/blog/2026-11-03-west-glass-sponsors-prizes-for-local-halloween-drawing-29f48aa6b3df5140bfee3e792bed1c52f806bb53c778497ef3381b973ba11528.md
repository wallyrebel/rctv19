West Glass and owner Scott Stubbs are being thanked for sponsoring prizes for this year's Halloween Drawing Contest.

The contest received many entries from local students, showcasing creativity from the community's youth. All contest winners will be able to collect their artwork and prizes at their respective schools by Wednesday.

Organizers expressed gratitude to Stubbs and West Glass for their support in making the contest a special event for the participating students.
