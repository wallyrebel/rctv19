Sunday's five-county check found two timely holiday updates and a Ripley church event. These are announcements and schedules, not accounts of events already held.

## Union: New Albany shops hold Holiday Open House today

New Albany's Holiday Open House is scheduled for **1 to 5 p.m. Sunday, Nov. 8**, according to [Union County News](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/new-albany-holiday-open-house-offers-2000-in-prizes/) and [Visit New Albany's events calendar](https://www.visitnewalbany.com/city-events). Union County News reports that 23 participating businesses are offering purchase-based entries for a drawing of $2,000 total in Main Street Money. The report says organizers plan to announce 18 winners at 5:30 p.m. on New Albany Main Street's Facebook page. Check participating shops for entry details before making a trip.

## Alcorn: Farmington opens Christmas parade entries

The [City of Farmington's registration page](https://www.farmington.ms.gov/node/46/christmas-parade-registration) lists its annual Christmas parade for **Dec. 7** and has a form for floats, walking groups, bands, vehicles and other entries. The city page does not give a start time or registration deadline. [Alcorn County News](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/farmington-christmas-parade-2026/) also reports that the city's calendar pairs the parade with a Light the Park celebration; watch for a fuller schedule from the city.

## Tippah: First Baptist Church Ripley begins Revival Days

First Baptist Church Ripley's Revival Days begin Sunday, Nov. 8, with a **9 a.m. service** and continue with **6:30 p.m. evening services through Wednesday**, according to [Tippah News' Saturday report](https://tippahnews.com/ripley-ms-news/fbc-ripley-revival-rick-coram/), which cites the church's announcement. The event is at 202 E. Pine St. in Ripley. Evangelist Rick Coram is scheduled to speak, and the report says childcare will be available during evening services.

## Prentiss and Benton

No distinct, meaningful new county development was verified for either county since Saturday afternoon's roundup. Earlier football results and the Benton frost forecast have already been covered.
