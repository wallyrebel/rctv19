Walnut beat Belmont 53–50 in boys varsity basketball Monday, November 23, according to MaxPreps.

Walnut led 24–13 after the first quarter and extended its advantage to 30–21 by halftime. Belmont mounted a fourth-quarter comeback, scoring 19 points to Walnut's 11, but fell short of the comeback.

Belmont had defeated Tishomingo County 64–60 on November 20 before the loss to Walnut. Walnut entered Monday's game with a mixed record, having lost to Nettleton 76–61 on November 17 before beating Strayhorn 64–49 on November 20.
