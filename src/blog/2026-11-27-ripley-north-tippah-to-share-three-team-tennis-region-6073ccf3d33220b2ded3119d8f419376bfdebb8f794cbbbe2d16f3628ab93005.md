Ripley and North Tippah will join Rosa Fort in Class 3A Region 2 beginning with the 2027-29 cycle, according to the Mississippi High School Activities Association's tennis alignment released Nov. 4.

The new three-team region cuts Ripley's number of region opponents in half from its current four. Ripley will remain in Class 4A Region 1 for the coming spring with Corinth, New Albany, North Pontotoc and Tishomingo County, but none of those schools will be in its region for the next cycle. New Albany and North Pontotoc will move to Class 4A Region 2, while Corinth and Tishomingo County will remain in Region 1.

Rosa Fort currently competes in Class 4A Region 3. The new alignment lists North Tippah under that school name.

Ripley's path to the team playoffs in spring 2027 will still run through its current Region 1 opponents. Under the 2026-27 Class 4A bracket, the Region 1 champion will play Region 4's fourth-place qualifier, while the Region 1 fourth-place team will play Region 4's champion.
