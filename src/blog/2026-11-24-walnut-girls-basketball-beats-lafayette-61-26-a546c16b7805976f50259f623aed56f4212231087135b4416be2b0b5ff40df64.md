Walnut won 61–26 over Lafayette in girls varsity basketball on Tuesday, November 24.

Walnut's recent results include a 64–14 victory over Strayhorn on November 20 and a 64–54 loss to Wheeler on November 23.

Lafayette's previous games included a 59–27 loss to Southaven on November 12 and a 62–42 loss to South Pontotoc on November 19.
