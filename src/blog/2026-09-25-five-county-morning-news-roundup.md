This Friday morning, Sept. 25, roundup covers newly reported Union and Prentiss County developments, plus a Corinth health care announcement highlighted since our previous edition. We found no additional meaningful, verified update for Benton or Tippah counties.

## Union County: Riverfest closes part of Main Street Saturday

Main Street in downtown New Albany, from East Bankhead to Carter Avenue, is scheduled to close **Saturday, Sept. 26, from 8 a.m. to 11 p.m.** for Tallahatchie Riverfest, [Union County News reported Thursday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/main-street-to-close-in-downtown-new-albany-during-tallahatchie-riverfest/), citing city officials. Drivers should plan another route. The report also says vehicles left in posted closure areas along Bankhead and Main streets may be towed at the owner's expense; check street signs for vehicle removal times.

## Union County: East Union and Myrtle receive cyber education grants

East Union and Myrtle's Cyber Foundation programs each received **$5,000** from New Albany Light, Gas and Water in partnership with the Tennessee Valley Authority, [Union County News reported Thursday](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/east-union-myrtles-cyber-programs-receive-10000-grant-from-local-utility-and-tva/). The schools plan to use the funding to support technology education, including coding and 3D modeling resources.

## Prentiss County: Northeast recognizes Hall of Fame inductees

Northeast Mississippi Community College in Booneville honored six people at a Thursday night Hall of Fame ceremony, [Prentiss County News reported](https://prentissnews.com/local-news/northeast-mississippi-community-college-honors-six-legendary-alumni-in-hall-of-fame-induction/). The honorees include Kent Geno, Mario Addison and Qyntel Woods. [Northeast's earlier announcement](https://nemcc.meritpages.com/news/nemcc-announces-2026-alumni-hall-of-fame-inductee-kenneth-paul--kent--geno--jr---p-e-/61845) identifies Geno, a Booneville native and president of Cook Coggin Engineers, as its 2026 Alumni Hall of Fame inductee.

## Alcorn County: Corinth pediatric clinic welcomes provider

Magnolia Pediatric Clinic in Corinth has welcomed pediatric nurse practitioner Holly Byrd, a Corinth native, [Magnolia Regional Health Center announced Sept. 21](https://www.mrhc.org/blog/news/magnolia-pediatric-clinic-welcomes-holly-byrd-msn-rn-cpnp-pc/). [Alcorn County News highlighted the announcement Thursday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/magnolia-pediatric-clinic-holly-byrd/). The hospital says Byrd has more than four years of experience as a pediatric primary care nurse practitioner. This is a recent hospital announcement receiving local coverage, not a newly announced addition this morning.
