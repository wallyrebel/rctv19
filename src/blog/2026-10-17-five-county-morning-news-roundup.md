Friday's state volleyball finals and a newly reported voting schedule lead this Saturday morning update for Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties. The match results are from Friday, Oct. 16; neither game took place Saturday morning.

## Union: New Albany takes Class 4A volleyball title

New Albany defeated Choctaw Central **3–1** in Friday's Class 4A final, according to the [Mississippi High School Activities Association's official result](https://www.misshsaa.com/2026/10/16/new-albany-wins-2026-class-4a-volleyball-championship/). After dropping the opening set 22–25, New Albany won 25–7, 25–13 and 25–10 to claim the championship. The association published its detailed result after Friday's match.

## Alcorn: Kossuth finishes runner-up in Class 3A

Kossuth fell **3–1** to Our Lady Academy in Friday's Class 3A final, [WLOX reported](https://www.wlox.com/2026/10/16/our-lady-academy-downs-kossuth-3-1-claim-5th-straight-volleyball-state-title/). Kossuth took the second set 25–20 after losing the first 21–25; Our Lady Academy won the final two 25–18 and 25–8. The [MHSAA championship page](https://www.misshsaa.com/2026/10/16/our-lady-academy-wins-2026-class-3a-volleyball-championship/) also names Our Lady Academy the Class 3A winner. This is the final result for the match previewed earlier in local coverage.

## Prentiss: two Saturday absentee voting sessions listed

The Prentiss County Circuit Clerk's Office plans in-person absentee voting from **9 a.m. to noon on Saturday, Oct. 24, and Saturday, Oct. 31**, [Alcorn County News reported Friday evening](https://alcornnewsms.com/mississippi-news/prentiss-county-absentee-voting-hours/), citing the *Daily Corinthian*. The general election is Nov. 3. The local report flags an apparent date error in the underlying account for the final in-person absentee day, so voters should confirm deadlines and eligibility with the clerk's office before visiting.

No separate meaningful new **Benton** or **Tippah County** development with enough verifiable detail was found in this morning's source check.
