# Spirit of Ripley Raffle Offers Amazing Prizes Through October 30

The Spirit of Ripley Raffle is now selling tickets for a chance to win numerous prizes donated by local businesses. Raffle offerings include an Ashley Furniture recliner, a Turtlebox speaker from West Glass, a gas patio heater from ACE Hardware, an Arctic Cooler from Chickasaw Equipment, and cash prizes up to $500 sponsored by Farm Bureau, CB&S Bank, and West Glass.

Additional prizes include a YETI cooler with accessories from Alfa, two steaks from Freemans & Co., a $75 oil change from Ford Place, a car wash from Big Jim's Car Wash, pottery from Brown & Covington, Main Street Moolah, and gift cards from local shops and restaurants.

Tickets are priced at $5 each or 5 for $20. Sales end Friday, October 30 at 5 PM. Winners will be announced every 30 minutes during the Spirit of Ripley event. All proceeds from ticket sales support downtown beautification projects. Tickets can be purchased online or by scanning a QR code.
