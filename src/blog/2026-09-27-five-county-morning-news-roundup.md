Sunday morning's check found a Saturday tournament result, a college honor reported Saturday and a late report from Corinth. Dates below refer to when events happened, not to new Sunday events.

## Tippah County: Walnut wins county volleyball tournament

Walnut won Saturday's **2026 Tippah County volleyball tournament** at Pine Grove School, defending its county title, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/walnut-tippah-county-volleyball-tournament/). Pine Grove, Ripley and Falkner also took part in the four-team round robin. The report did not have final scores for the other matches or an all-tournament team as of Saturday evening, so those results are not included here.

## Prentiss County: NEMCC alumna receives Mississippi State honor

Kunshinge Sorrell-Howard, a Northeast Mississippi Community College alumna and Success Coaching team member, was inducted into Mississippi State's **2026 M-Club Sports Hall of Fame**, [Prentiss County News reported Saturday](https://prentissnews.com/local-news/nemcc-alumna-kunshinge-sorrell-howard-inducted-into-mississippi-state-hall-of-fame/). The report says the honor was announced during a gala ahead of Mississippi State's football game against Missouri. Her connection to the Booneville college makes this a Prentiss County-area item; the induction took place at Mississippi State.

## Alcorn County: Marching contest held in Corinth

The **Crossroads Marching Classic** took place Saturday in Corinth, with **11 high school bands competing**, according to a [late Saturday Gray Media video report carried by KTTC](https://www.kttc.com/video/2026/09/27/crossroads-marching-classic-takes-place-corinth/). Earlier local coverage listed participating bands, but the late report does not give placements or awards. We will wait for verified results before naming winners.
