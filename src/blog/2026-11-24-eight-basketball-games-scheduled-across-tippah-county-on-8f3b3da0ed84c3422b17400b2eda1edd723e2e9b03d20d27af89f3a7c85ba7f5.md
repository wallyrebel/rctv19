# Eight basketball games scheduled across Tippah County on November 24

**Boys Basketball:**
- Walnut Wildcats host Jumpertown Cardinals at 8:00 AM in Walnut
- Ripley Tigers visit West Union Eagles at 3:15 PM in Myrtle
- Falkner Eagles travel to Potts Camp Cardinals at 8:45 PM in Potts Camp

**Girls Basketball:**
- Falkner Eagles host Byers Lions and Jumpertown Cardinals at 9:15 AM in Falkner
- Ripley Tigers host Ashland Blue Devils at 10:15 AM in Ripley
- Walnut Wildcats travel to Lafayette Commodores at 11:30 AM in Oxford
- Falkner Eagles travel to Potts Camp Cardinals at 8:30 PM in Potts Camp

Full schedules are available at TippahSports.com.
