# The Shark 102.3 Sponsors Spirit of Ripley Festival & Chili Cookoff

The Shark 102.3 is supporting the upcoming Spirit of Ripley Festival & Chili Cookoff, scheduled for Friday, October 30th at 5:30 PM in Historic Downtown Ripley.

The community event will feature a chili cookoff competition and festive activities in the downtown area.
