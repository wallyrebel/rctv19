# Ripley Christmas Parade Accepting Float Sign-Ups

The City of Ripley is now accepting sign-ups for its annual Christmas Parade, scheduled for **December 8th at 7:00 PM** in downtown Ripley.

## Theme: Christmas Through the Decades

This year's parade theme invites participants to celebrate their favorite decade, including:
- The fabulous 1950s
- Groovy 1960s
- Disco 1970s
- Totally awesome 1980s
- 1990s and beyond

## Who Can Participate

The parade welcomes entries from:
- Floats
- Businesses
- Churches
- Schools
- Clubs and organizations
- Pageant winners
- Classic cars
- And more

## How to Sign Up

Interested participants can register online by scanning the QR code on the official flyer or visiting the sign-up link at https://app.seemylegacy.com/community/2637/campaign/7402.

Organizers encourage participants to dig through old photos for inspiration as they plan their decade-themed entries.
