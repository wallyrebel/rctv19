## Pet Costume Contest Tonight in Downtown Ripley

Pet owners are invited to bring their costumed companions to a costume contest at The Cut-Off, located at 204 South Commerce Street, starting at 7:00 PM tonight.

Registration is $5 per pet, and all animals must be kept on a leash. Prizes will be awarded in three categories: spookiest, silliest, and most creative pet costumes.

For more information, search #SpiritOfRipley or #PetCostumeContest on social media.
