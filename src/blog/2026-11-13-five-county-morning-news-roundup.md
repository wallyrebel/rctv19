## Tippah: Blue Mountain Christian wins SSAC tournament opener

Blue Mountain Christian University swept Andrew College 25-14, 25-16, 25-16 in Thursday's Southern States Athletic Conference volleyball tournament opener in Montgomery, Alabama, [BMCU Athletics reports](https://bmcusports.com/news/2026/11/12/womens-volleyball-volley-toppers-sweep-andrew-college-to-open-play-at-the-ssac-championship.aspx). Salice Speed led the Volley Toppers with 13 kills, and Abbi Williams had 15 digs. [Tippah News published its local report](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/bmcu-volleyball-ssac-championship-sweep/) after the previous roundup. BMCU was scheduled to face Loyola at 9 a.m. Friday in its final pool-play match; that result was not yet available for this morning edition.
