This Saturday morning roundup covers developments since Friday's evening edition. Times are Central. Weather figures below are forecasts, not observed local damage or rainfall.

## All five counties: Wind Advisory and Flood Watch

The [National Weather Service Memphis advisory](https://www.weather.gov/wwamap/wwatxtget.php?cwa=meg&wwa=all) includes **Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah** counties in a Wind Advisory through **8 p.m. Saturday, Oct. 10**. The bulletin forecasts northeast winds of 15 to 25 mph with gusts up to 50 mph. It warns that unsecured objects could blow around, tree limbs could fall and some outages could result. These are possible impacts, not confirmed incidents.

The same NWS listing keeps all five counties under a **Flood Watch through 7 a.m. Sunday**. Its late-Friday update forecast 1 to 3 inches across the broader watch area, with the heaviest rain most likely in parts of northeast Mississippi and western Tennessee. [NWS Memphis's Saturday morning graphic](https://www.weather.gov/meg/events/latest_storm/) allows for locally higher totals up to 3.5 inches, mainly in northeast Mississippi. Check [current NWS alerts](https://www.weather.gov/meg/) before travel; never drive through a flooded road.

## Union and Tippah: New Albany beats Ripley

New Albany defeated rival Ripley in Friday night's football game, [WTVA reported in its Saturday morning Friday Night Fever coverage](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/10/wtva-friday-night-fever-week-7-highlights-scores/). WTVA's accessible report did not give a final score, so this roundup does not attach one.

## Prentiss and Alcorn: Friday football finals

Booneville beat Belmont **41-6** at home Friday, and Alcorn Central beat Mantachie **39-14** in Glen, according to [Alcorn County News's Saturday score report](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/area-high-school-football-scores-oct-9/). The report lists Booneville at 6-0 after the win. Corinth's 52-7 result over North Pontotoc was a **Thursday** game, not a Friday final.

## Alcorn: Corinth 5K switches to a virtual race

The Cat Reman Challenge 5K scheduled for Saturday morning in Corinth will **not take place in person** because of the weather forecast, [Alcorn County News reported after Caterpillar's announcement](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/cat-reman-5k-corinth-virtual/). The organizer says participants can complete the virtual 5K by Oct. 24; registered runners will receive instructions about times, shirts and medals by email. The event benefits United Way of Corinth and Alcorn County.

## Benton: Ashland's Friday result

Ashland lost **54-0** at Coffeeville on Friday, according to [MaxPreps's posted final](https://www.maxpreps.com/m/news/PmrDYaP6EkGc7a52cbo0hQ/football-recap-coffeeville-skates-past-ashland-with-ease.htm). We found no other meaningful, newly verified Benton County development beyond the countywide weather advisories.

## Tippah: Walnut shows recreation work in progress

The Town of Walnut shared a progress video of its new ballfields and archery park Friday, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/walnut-ballfields-archery-park-progress/). The town said work remains. It did **not** announce an opening date, cost or intended users in that update.
