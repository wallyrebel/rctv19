# Bates powers Ripley comeback as three Tippah teams advance

Sydney Bates led Ripley to victory with 20 kills as the team rallied past North Pontotoc 3-1 on Saturday, October 3, in Class competition.

The strong performance from Bates helped propel Ripley's comeback in the match. The win marked one of three Tippah County teams to advance in the tournament.
