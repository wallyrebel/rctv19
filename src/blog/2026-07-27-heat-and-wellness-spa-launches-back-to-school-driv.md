# Heat & Wellness Spa Launches Back-to-School Drive to Support Local Students and Teachers

In a heartwarming effort to support the local community, Heat & Wellness Spa is organizing a Back-to-School Drive aimed at helping students and educators prepare for the upcoming school year.

## Event Details and Activities

The initiative will take place on August 1st at the spa's location on 608 City Ave. South in Ripley, Mississippi. The event combines fundraising with community support through multiple offerings:

**Fundraising Component:** Heat & Wellness Spa will sell snow cones, with proceeds directed toward purchasing essential school supplies for students in need.

**Community Giveaway:** The spa is providing free hot dogs and drinks to attendees while supplies last, creating an accessible community gathering space.

## How to Contribute

The spa is actively accepting donations of:
- Backpacks
- School supplies
- Shoes
- Children's clothing
- Socks
- Kid's tumblers
- Kleenex and other school essentials

Individuals who wish to donate items or know children in need of assistance are encouraged to contact Heat & Wellness Spa directly at 662-502-2907.

## Community Impact

The back-to-school initiative represents a significant community commitment, recognizing that school readiness extends beyond academics to include having proper supplies and clothing. By combining fundraising efforts with direct donations and community engagement, Heat & Wellness Spa is working to ensure that local students can begin the school year with confidence and the necessary tools for success.

Community members are encouraged to participate, whether through donations, attendance at the August 1st event, or by spreading awareness about this important local initiative.
