# My Choices Family Christmas Event

My Choices is spreading Christmas cheer with a special family event on **November 19 from 2–7 PM** at **109 Bails Rd., Ripley**.

The free event will feature:
- Pictures with Santa
- Kids' activities
- Cookies and hot cocoa
- Free gifts for moms and children, including diapers and wipes

All activities and gifts are completely free. The organization is encouraging community members to help spread the word so families in the area can enjoy the festive evening.
