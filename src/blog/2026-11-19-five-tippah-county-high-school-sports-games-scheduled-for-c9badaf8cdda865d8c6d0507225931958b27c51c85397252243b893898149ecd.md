Tippah County high schools have five games scheduled for Thursday, November 19, according to Tippah County Sports.

**Soccer in New Albany:**
- Ripley Tigers girls soccer vs. New Albany Bulldogs at 5:00 PM
- Ripley Tigers boys soccer vs. New Albany Bulldogs at 7:00 PM

**Basketball at home:**
- Ripley Tigers girls vs. Center Hill Mustangs at 6:00 PM in Ripley
- Ripley Tigers boys vs. Center Hill Mustangs at 7:00 PM in Ripley
- Falkner Eagles boys vs. Tupelo Titans at 6:30 PM in Falkner
