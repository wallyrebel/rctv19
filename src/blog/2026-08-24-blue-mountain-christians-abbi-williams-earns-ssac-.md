# Blue Mountain Christian's Abbi Williams Earns SSAC Defender of the Week

Blue Mountain Christian volleyball junior libero Abbi Williams has earned SSAC Defender of the Week honors, capping off a stellar performance at the Carolina Classic tournament.

Williams demonstrated exceptional defensive prowess throughout the three-match event, accumulating an impressive 67 digs. Her consistent play and ball-handling skills were instrumental in her team's performance at the tournament.

The recognition reflects Williams' importance to the Blue Mountain Christian volleyball program. As a libero—the defensive specialist position in volleyball—Williams is tasked with leading the back row and managing the team's passing and defensive efforts. Her 67 digs over the three-match span showcases her work ethic and ability to make an impact on every play.

This weekly honor adds to what appears to be a strong season for both Williams and the Blue Mountain Christian volleyball team. The SSAC (Southern States Athletic Conference) Defender of the Week award recognizes outstanding defensive performances and highlights players who excel in this critical aspect of the game.
