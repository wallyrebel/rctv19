## Tippah: South Tippah names next superintendent

Assistant Superintendent Kelly Gates is scheduled to become South Tippah School District superintendent on July 1, 2027, after current Superintendent Tony Elliott retires, [Tippah News reported Wednesday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/south-tippah-superintendent-kelly-gates/), citing the Southern Sentinel and the district. Elliott remains superintendent through the transition. Gates has worked for the district since 2009.

## Alcorn: Firefighters seek toys; basketball classic approaches

The Corinth Fire Department's Stuff a Fire Truck drive will collect **new toys** for children in foster care outside the Corinth Walmart from noon to 6 p.m. Friday through Sunday, Nov. 27–29, [Alcorn County News reported Wednesday](https://alcornnewsms.com/corinth-news/corinth-fire-department-toy-drive/), citing the Daily Corinthian. The linked report has more details for donors.

The [Lighthouse Classic](https://alcornnewsms.com/sports/lighthouse-classic-corinth-schedule-2026/) is scheduled for Friday and Saturday at Corinth High School. The published schedule includes Alcorn Central against Holly Springs at 10 a.m. Friday, Corinth against Florence, Alabama, at 8:30 p.m. Friday, and Kossuth against Wayne County, Tennessee, at 11 a.m. Saturday. The event also includes Booneville girls' and boys' games Saturday, giving Prentiss County teams a place in the two-day program. Check the organizer's schedule before traveling.

## Union, Prentiss and Benton

No separate meaningful new development for Union or Benton counties was verified for this edition. Prentiss County's Booneville games are listed above; no other distinct new county development was verified.
