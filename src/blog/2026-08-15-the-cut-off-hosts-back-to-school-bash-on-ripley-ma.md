# The Cut-Off Hosts Back to School Bash on Ripley Main Street

The Cut-Off, a popular gathering spot in downtown Ripley, is hosting a Back to School Bash this Saturday, offering families and students a chance to celebrate the start of the academic year with food, fun, and community spirit.

## Event Details

The Back to School Bash runs from 12 PM to 4 PM at The Cut-Off, located at 204 S. Commerce Street in the heart of Ripley's Main Street district. The four-hour event is designed to bring together families and students as they prepare for the school year ahead.

## Community Gathering

According to organizers, attendees are invited to "hang out" and enjoy Saturday afternoon activities in a welcoming atmosphere. The event reflects the venue's commitment to serving as a community hub and supporting local families during back-to-school season.

For those planning to attend, The Cut-Off's central Main Street location makes it easily accessible, and the extended afternoon hours provide flexibility for families with varying schedules.

Residents looking for a casual, family-friendly way to kick off the school year are encouraged to stop by and join in the celebration.
