Before a float rolls into downtown New Albany next Monday, its builders have a measurement to take from the ground to the tallest decoration. The New Albany Main Street Association sets a 13-foot limit for floats, trailers and vehicles on the Christmas parade route. The Tanglefoot Trail bicycle bridge over Main Street has 14 feet of clearance. The parade starts at 6:30 p.m. on Nov. 30. [Organizer’s parade instructions](https://www.newalbanymainstreet.com/2026parade)

Organizers say an entry taller than 13 feet may be able to arrange an exit before the bridge. Getting into the lineup poses a separate issue for large vehicles. Access to the regular staging area crosses a bridge with size and weight restrictions. The association excludes 18-wheelers and tractor-trailers from that area and says it may assign other oversized vehicles a separate location.

Families with students in school bands should look to their director or designated school contact for an arrival time and place. The association says it will give bands separate instructions ahead of parade day. It also asks each participating organization to designate one person to share information with everyone in its entry.

Registration has reached capacity. These instructions apply to participants who already have a place in the parade. [Union County News coverage of the organizer’s registration update](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/new-albany-christmas-parade-registration-reaches-capacity/)
