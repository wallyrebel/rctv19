## Prentiss: New Site students organize blood drive

New Site's Student Government Association hosted a blood drive this week, and Prentiss County Sheriff's Department investigators Torie Jumper and Aaron Stolz donated, [Alcorn County News reported](https://alcornnewsms.com/public-service/new-site-blood-drive-prentiss-county/). The department shared photos Thursday evening and thanked the students and donors. Eli Hall, whom the department described as a University of Mississippi intern with the sheriff's office, was also pictured with the investigators.

## Tippah: Blue Mountain seniors visit Ripley manufacturer

Seniors in Blue Mountain School's College & Career Readiness class toured Ashley Furniture's Ripley plant, according to [Tippah News' report on the school's Friday update](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/blue-mountain-seniors-ashley-furniture/). Ashley employees discussed workplace safety, attendance and manufacturing careers before the students walked through the plant. Career coach Deana Reno organized the trip. The school described it as a recent visit; the exact tour date was not stated.

## Union and Tippah: New Albany mayor elected regional vice president

The North Mississippi Mayors Association met Thursday evening at Blue Mountain Christian University in Tippah County. The Mississippi Municipal League announced Friday that New Albany Mayor Jeff Olson was elected the association's vice president and Pittsboro Mayor Cindy Hubbard its president, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/north-mississippi-mayors-blue-mountain/). The officers serve a regional municipal group; this was not a Union or Tippah county government election.

No distinct, meaningful new Alcorn or Benton county development was verified for this edition.
