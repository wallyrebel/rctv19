# Clemmer Construction Company Sponsors Spirit of Ripley Festival & Chili Cookoff

Clemmer Construction Company, LLC has been recognized as a sponsor of the Spirit of Ripley Festival & Chili Cookoff.

The event is scheduled for **Friday, October 30th at 5:30 PM** in Historic Downtown Ripley. The community is invited to attend the festival and chili competition in downtown Ripley.
