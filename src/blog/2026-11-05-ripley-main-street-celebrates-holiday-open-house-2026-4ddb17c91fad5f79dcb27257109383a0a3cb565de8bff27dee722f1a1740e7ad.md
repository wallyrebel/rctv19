# Ripley Main Street Celebrates Holiday Open House 2026

Ripley Main Street thanked attendees and participating businesses for making the Holiday Open House 2026 a success. The event, held on Sunday, drew community members to shop local and support downtown retailers.

Photographer Lolly Lins documented highlights from the day, capturing the holiday spirit throughout the downtown area. A full photo gallery from the event is available online.

Organizers noted that while the photographer worked to capture images from all participating shops, they welcome additional photos from businesses that may have been missed. Store owners are encouraged to submit their favorite moments from the day for sharing on the Ripley Main Street social media channels.
