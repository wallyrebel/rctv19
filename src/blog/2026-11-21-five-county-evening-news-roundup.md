## Benton: Property-value update may affect tax bills

Benton County Tax Assessor/Collector Carol Ormon says **2026 is a property-value update year** and assessments across the county will be adjusted after a state value-index change, according to a county notice [reported Saturday by Tippah News](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/benton-county-property-value-update-2026/). The Tax Office warns that many owners may see higher property-tax amounts. The notice does not give an average increase or a date for individual notices; a property's final bill also depends on the tax rate. Owners with questions about their assessment can contact the Benton County Tax Office in Ashland.

## Benton: Ashland High recognizes teacher

Ashland High School named Briana Rucker its Teacher of the Year, [Tippah News reported Saturday](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/ashland-high-teacher-of-the-year-rucker/), citing the school's Friday announcement. The school also thanked Ms. Terry, who assists in Rucker's classroom. The report does not give the date of the classroom presentation.

## Tippah: Falkner students visit Mississippi State

Falkner High juniors and seniors toured Mississippi State University's Starkville campus, visited its basketball arena and had lunch on campus, [Tippah News reported Saturday](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/falkner-high-mississippi-state-tour/). The school shared the visit Thursday; the report does not specify the trip's date.

## Benton, Tippah, Union and Alcorn: Holiday basketball scheduled

The Hickory Flat Thanksgiving Tournament in Benton County is scheduled for **Nov. 23–24**, with 22 girls' and boys' games and $8 admission, [Tippah News reported](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/hickory-flat-thanksgiving-tournament/), citing Hickory Flat Rebel Basketball's schedule. Benton County host Hickory Flat, Tippah County teams Walnut, Falkner and Blue Mountain, Union County's East Union and Alcorn County's Corinth girls are among those listed. Monday's first game is scheduled for 8 a.m.; later start times may shift as games run ahead or behind. Check the [full schedule](https://tippahnews.com/mississippi-news/hickory-flat-thanksgiving-tournament/) before traveling.
