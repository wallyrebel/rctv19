Channing Lane leads Blue Mountain Christian women's basketball in steals and assists through the team's first eight games with 23 steals and 26 assists, averaging 2.9 steals and 3.3 assists per game, according to Tippah County Sports.

Lane has scored 79 points, tied for third on the team. Lena Taylor leads the Toppers with 125 points and 16 steals, while Brooklyn Hodum has 13 steals and Isabella Aversa has 11.

Blue Mountain is 7-1 overall and 1-1 in Southern States Athletic Conference play. The team has recorded 99 steals through eight games, an average of 12.4 per game compared with 65 by opponents, and has reached double-digit steals in seven of eight games. The Toppers average 19.6 points off turnovers per game while allowing 11.0.

The team's season-high 16 steals came in a 76-60 loss at Faulkner on Nov. 14. Blue Mountain defeated Wesleyan 91-52 in Macon, Georgia with 14 steals as Wesleyan committed 31 turnovers.

Blue Mountain resumes play at home Dec. 3 against Point University.
