# Downtown Ripley Events Today

Downtown Ripley is hosting several community activities today:

## Sew What? Pop-Up Shop
Millie & Co. is hosting a pop-up featuring handmade goods and adorable creations available for purchase.

## Movie Night at The Cut-Off
Residents are invited to bring blankets and lawn chairs for an outdoor screening of Hocus Pocus under the stars. The movie begins at 7 PM at 204 S. Commerce Street.

West Glass Co. is sponsoring tonight's movie event.

Local organizers encourage community members to shop local and spend the day enjoying downtown Ripley.
