Monday evening's Corinth rally has now been covered by reporters at the event. This is an outcome update to the previous roundup's advance traffic notice. A separate Union County notice offers a free art activity later this week.

## Alcorn: Campaign rally held at Crossroads Arena

President Donald Trump spoke Monday at [Crossroads Arena in Corinth](https://mississippitoday.org/2026/10/26/trump-rallies-for-cindy-hyde-smith-in-mississippi-you-like-me/) in support of Republican U.S. Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith's reelection campaign. Mississippi Today reported from the arena that Hyde-Smith also addressed supporters and that Trump urged them to vote in the Nov. 3 election. [WBBJ's post-event report](https://www.wbbjtv.com/2026/10/27/president-trump-speaks-supporters-voters-rally-corinth-mississippi/) described thousands of attendees. These reports establish that the rally took place; Monday afternoon's no-closure traffic plan should not be read as an account of how traffic unfolded.

## Union: Free homeschool art program scheduled in New Albany

[Union County News reports](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/new-albany-to-host-homeschool-art-program-on-walter-anderson/) that a free program for homeschool students is set for **Thursday, Oct. 29, from 10 to 11 a.m.** at the art house, 205 Faulkner Way, New Albany. The activity will explore Mississippi artist Walter Anderson's seasonal murals and invite students to make art inspired by his work. The report links to the organizer's registration announcement; families should check it before attending.
