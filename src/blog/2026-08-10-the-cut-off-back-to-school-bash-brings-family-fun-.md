# The Cut-Off Back to School Bash Brings Family Fun to Downtown Ripley

Families in the Ripley area are invited to celebrate the start of the school year at The Cut-Off Back to School Bash this Saturday, August 15.

The event runs from 12:00 p.m. to 4:00 p.m. at 204 S Commerce in downtown Ripley, Mississippi, and promises a full afternoon of entertainment and activities for children and adults alike.

## What to Expect

Attendees can look forward to a variety of attractions designed to make back-to-school preparation fun and engaging. The festivities will include:

- **Face painting** for creative kids
- **Games and activities** for all ages
- **Pop-up vendors** offering unique items and services
- **Food trucks** providing meal options for hungry families
- **School supply giveaway** to help ease the financial burden of back-to-school shopping

## Community Event

The Back to School Bash represents The Cut-Off's commitment to bringing the community together during an important time of year. By combining entertainment, food, and practical resources like school supplies, the event aims to make the transition back to school an enjoyable experience for local families.

Organizers encourage families to bring everyone and plan to spend the afternoon enjoying the festivities in downtown Ripley. The free activities and giveaways make it an accessible option for families of all backgrounds looking to celebrate the new school year together.

For more information about the event, follow The Cut-Off on social media using #TheCutOff and #BackToSchoolBash.
