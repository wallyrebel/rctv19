Pink Friday is coming to Ripley on Friday, November 20 from 4:00 to 7:00 PM throughout downtown Ripley and around town.

Local shops are participating with special deals and hours for the event. Shoppers are encouraged to check their favorite local retailers for specific offers available during Pink Friday, which takes place before the traditional Black Friday sales.
