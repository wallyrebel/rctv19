Sunday evening's edition adds a community health event announced this afternoon and playoff results reported in detail since our morning roundup. The volleyball matches were played Saturday, Oct. 3. Sources were checked Sunday afternoon, Oct. 4. Times are Central.

## Tippah County: Hospital plans free Senior Day health fair

Tippah County Hospital announced a **free Senior Day Community Health Fair** for Friday, Oct. 9, from **11 a.m. to 1 p.m.** at the Fred & Elizabeth Smith Sportsplex Building in Ripley, [Tippah News reported Sunday afternoon](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-county-hospital-senior-day/), citing the hospital's event notice. The report says more than 25 community vendors are expected, with health information and flu shots available. A free barbecue lunch is scheduled from 11 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., and door-prize drawings begin at 12:30 p.m. The report lists **kneedham@tc-hs.org** for event questions. This is an upcoming event announcement, not a report that the fair has taken place.

## Tippah County: Walnut and Pine Grove advance in volleyball

Two additional county teams advanced in Saturday's opening round of the girls volleyball playoffs, according to [Tippah County Sports' detailed report](https://tippahsports.com/ripley/bates-powers-ripley-comeback-as-three-tippah-teams-advance/). **Walnut swept Leland 3–0** in Class 2A, while **Pine Grove swept West Lowndes 3–0** in Class 1A, with set scores of 25–18, 25–18 and 25–8. Falkner lost in three sets at Tupelo Christian Prep, ending its season. Saturday evening's roundup already covered Ripley's first-round win; these are the other Tippah team outcomes from the same playoff day. The sports report lists East Webster as Walnut's next opponent on Tuesday, Oct. 6; readers should check the school for any schedule change.
