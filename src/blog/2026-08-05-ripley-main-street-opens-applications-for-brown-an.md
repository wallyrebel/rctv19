# Ripley Main Street Launches Application Period for Brown & Covington Artisan Program

Ripley Main Street has announced an exciting opportunity for local creative entrepreneurs. The organization is now accepting applications from artisans, makers, and artists seeking to expand their businesses and elevate their craft through a new retail initiative launching this fall.

## What's Being Offered

Selected participants in the Brown & Covington program will receive comprehensive support to help their businesses thrive, including:

- **Dedicated retail space** to showcase and sell their work
- **Marketing and promotional assistance** to reach new customers
- **Business support services** to strengthen operations
- **Workshop and event participation** for professional development
- **Pop-up opportunities** to test new markets and build brand visibility

## How to Apply

Interested artisans and makers have until **August 31, 2026** to submit their applications. Prospective participants can apply through the official application portal by scanning the QR code provided in the announcement or clicking the application link.

## Community Involvement

Ripley Main Street is encouraging community members to help spread the word about this opportunity by tagging local makers and sharing the announcement on social media. The initiative represents a significant investment in supporting the local creative economy and giving emerging entrepreneurs the resources they need to succeed.

For more information and to submit an application, visit the official application portal at app.seemylegacy.com/community/2637/campaign/9513.
