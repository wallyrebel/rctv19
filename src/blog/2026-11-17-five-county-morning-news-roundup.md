## Prentiss: Northeast basketball wins two Monday road games

Booneville-based Northeast Mississippi Community College won both basketball games Monday night, Nov. 16, [Alcorn County News reported](https://alcornnewsms.com/mississippi-news/nemcc-basketball-sweeps-road-games/). The women beat Coastal Alabama-North 88-53, while the men beat East Central Community College 83-74 in Decatur. The report, citing official game box scores, says the Lady Tigers improved to 5-1 and the Tigers to 5-2. Jordyn Crumedy led the women with 13 points; Jalen Chandler scored 17 points and had 13 rebounds for the men.

## Tippah: Falkner students try fiber-optic splicing

Falkner High School 10th graders handled fiber-optic equipment and tried splicing during an Information Technology Career Spotlight, [Tippah News reported Monday evening](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/falkner-high-fiber-optic-nemcc/), citing a school post. NEMCC fiber technician instructor Jamie Phillips and program graduate Malachi Wilbanks led the session. The report does not give a date for the visit itself, so this is a newly reported school activity rather than a claim that it took place Monday.
