## A Morning of Creativity and Blooms Awaits

Lola's Garden Bloom Bar is inviting the community to join an exciting floral workshop that promises to be both fun and inspiring. Participants will have the opportunity to create their own stunning floral centerpiece while enjoying a relaxing morning in good company.

### Event Details

**Date:** Saturday, August 22nd  
**Time:** 10:00 a.m.  
**Location:** The Cut-Off, 204 South Commerce Street  
**Cost:** $35 (all materials included)

### What to Expect

This hands-on workshop is designed for both beginners and experienced flower enthusiasts. Attendees will receive guidance from Lola's Garden Bloom Bar experts as they select and arrange fresh flowers into a beautiful centerpiece they can take home. With all materials provided, participants need only bring their creativity and enthusiasm.

### Limited Spots Available

Due to the intimate nature of the workshop, spaces are filling up quickly. Interested attendees are encouraged to register as soon as possible to secure their spot. Sign-ups are available through an online registration form, ensuring a smooth and organized experience for all participants.

Whether you're looking for a fun weekend activity, a unique gift-making opportunity, or simply want to explore your creative side, this floral workshop offers the perfect blend of relaxation and hands-on artistry.
