Monday evening's edition covers reports published since this morning's roundup. The courthouse schedule is an estimate, not a completed project. Sources were checked Monday afternoon, Oct. 5. Times are Central.

## Prentiss County: Sheriff warns about payment-demand calls

The Prentiss County Sheriff's Department warned Monday that callers posing as cable, internet or television providers have demanded immediate payment to prevent a supposed service shutoff, [Alcorn County News reported](https://alcornnewsms.com/police-departments/prentiss-county-utility-bill-scam/). The department said scammers were active in Prentiss and surrounding counties, following bogus jury-duty calls last week. Residents who receive a suspicious call can hang up and contact the provider using a number from their bill or its official website. The report lists **662-728-6232** for the sheriff's department. It does not give a count of victims or reported losses.

## Alcorn County: Courthouse renovation has a year-end target

Alcorn County officials now expect work on the historic courthouse in Corinth to finish **at the end of 2026**, according to [Alcorn County News' Monday report](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/alcorn-county-courthouse-renovation-update/) of a Daily Corinthian update from Chief Administrative Officer Bobby Burns. The project began in May 2024 and has missed earlier completion estimates. The new date remains a target; the report says remaining work includes courtroom balcony seating and office space, and that work in the older building can still affect the schedule.

## Alcorn County: Both public school districts report fewer students

Fall enrollment is down **4 percent** from last school year in the Corinth School District and **3.8 percent** in the Alcorn School District, [Alcorn County News reported Monday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-alcorn-school-enrollment-decline/), citing the Daily Corinthian. These are reported districtwide comparisons; neither article gives a new school-by-school count. The decline may matter for future budgets, but no specific local funding cut was announced in the report.

## Alcorn County: Corinth Kiwanis makes hospital gift

The Corinth Kiwanis Club gave **$20,000** to Le Bonheur Children's Hospital, [Alcorn County News reported Monday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-kiwanis-gives-20000-to-le-bonheur-childrens-hospital/). The report says the donation supports pediatric care at the Memphis hospital and its Tupelo Outpatient Center. It does not specify how the hospital will allocate the gift.
