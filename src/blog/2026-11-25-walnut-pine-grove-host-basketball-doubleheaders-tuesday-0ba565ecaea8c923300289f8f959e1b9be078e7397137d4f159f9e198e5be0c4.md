**At Walnut:**
- Girls play Alcorn Central at 6 p.m., boys follow at 7:30 p.m. (non-region games)
- Boys enter on a three-game winning streak with recent victories over Strayhorn, Belmont and Jumpertown
- Girls beat Lafayette 61-26 in their most recent game after losing to Wheeler 64-54

**At Pine Grove:**
- Girls host Thrasher at 6 p.m., boys play at 7 p.m. (region games)
- Boys won 60-36 at Ingomar on Nov. 20; girls lost 75-44

**Upcoming:** Both Pine Grove teams play region games at Wheeler on Friday, Dec. 4. Walnut travels to Bruce that day.
