## Alcorn: District recognizes eight teachers

The [Alcorn School District announced](https://www.alcornschools.org/live-feed) its eight 2026–27 Teachers of the Year, representing Alcorn Central, Biggersville, Kossuth and the Alcorn Career and Technology Center. [Alcorn County News lists the honorees by campus](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/alcorn-school-district-teachers-of-the-year/). The district did not say whether it will select one overall district honoree.

## Tippah: Ripley golf champions receive banner

Ripley High School presented its boys golf team with the 2026 Class 4A state championship trophy and banner during Thursday's basketball games, [Tippah News reports](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-sports/ripley-golf-state-champions-banner/). The recognition was reported Saturday; the state title itself was announced earlier and is not a new result.

## Prentiss and Alcorn: December parade dates posted

[WTVA's newly published local parade list](https://www.wtva.com/2026/11/14/list-local-christmas-parades/) gives Baldwyn's parade as Dec. 3 at 6 p.m., Corinth's as Dec. 5 at 5 p.m., and Booneville's as Dec. 7 at 6 p.m. The list does not provide routes or entry details; check with organizers before making plans.

## Union and Benton

No distinct, meaningful new development in these counties was verified for this edition. Friday's Union football results appeared in the morning roundup.
