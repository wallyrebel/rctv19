Tuesday, Nov. 24, is the one regular opening left this week at Blue Mountain Library. The branch at 125 South Railroad Avenue normally opens Tuesdays and Fridays from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. This Friday falls under the Northeast Regional Library system’s Thanksgiving closure. [Blue Mountain branch hours](https://www.nereg.lib.ms.us/blue-mountain-library/) · [System holiday notice](https://www.nereg.lib.ms.us/)

Ripley Library at 308 Commerce Street gives readers another option before and after the holiday. Its regular Monday through Thursday hours are 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. With all branches closed Thursday, Nov. 26, and Friday, Nov. 27, Wednesday is the last regular evening opening before Thanksgiving. Ripley lists 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. for Saturday, Nov. 28. The branch phone is 662-837-7773. [Ripley branch hours](https://www.nereg.lib.ms.us/ripley-public-library/)

The holiday falls on Chalybeate Library’s only regular opening, Thursdays from noon to 5 p.m. The Walnut branch remains temporarily closed. Walnut cardholders can use their cards at any Northeast Regional location, including Ripley. [Chalybeate branch hours](https://www.nereg.lib.ms.us/chalybeate-library/) · [Walnut cardholder notice](https://www.nereg.lib.ms.us/walnut-public-library/)

Readers can also borrow ebooks and audiobooks remotely through the library’s Libby and hoopla services. The library says digital loans return automatically when the lending period ends. [Library digital services](https://www.nereg.lib.ms.us/)
