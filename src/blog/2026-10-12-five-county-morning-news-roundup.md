Monday morning's edition checks current public-service listings and today's scheduled games. These are schedules and listed end dates, not reports of completed games or a new burn-ban order. Times are Central.

## Alcorn and Tippah: check burn-ban status before outdoor burning

The [Mississippi Forestry Commission's current burn-ban list](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/), checked Monday morning, still lists **Alcorn County through Oct. 12** and **Tippah County through Oct. 18**. The commission [says bans expire at midnight on their stated end date](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/burning-info/burn-bans/). Alcorn's listed date does not confirm that an extension has been approved or that burning is permitted today. Residents should check the commission's live list and local fire officials before outdoor burning.

## Union: Ingomar and New Albany join tonight's North State finals

In addition to the **East Union–Walnut** match covered Sunday, [Union County Sports reports](https://unionsportsms.com/2026/10/11/union-county-sports-2026-10-11/) that **Ingomar hosts Tupelo Christian Prep** in Class 1A and **New Albany visits Mooreville** in Class 4A. All three North State volleyball finals are scheduled for **6 p.m. Monday, Oct. 12**. No result was available at this morning's check; the winners advance to the state championship matches.

## Across the five counties: preparedness week begins

Mississippi's **Fall Severe Weather Preparedness Week runs Oct. 12–17**, according to the [Mississippi Emergency Management Agency](https://msema.org/news/governor-reeves-declares-october-12-17-2026-fall-severe-weather-preparedness-week). Monday's topic is making a plan; later topics include tornado safety, flash flooding and ways to receive warnings. MEMA advises residents to review their emergency plans, identify a shelter location and check how they will receive alerts. The observance is a preparedness campaign, not a warning that severe weather is expected today.

No separate, meaningful new local development was verified for **Prentiss** or **Benton** counties during this morning's source check. The Tippah item above is the current burn-ban listing; Sunday's Walnut match preview has not yet become a result.
