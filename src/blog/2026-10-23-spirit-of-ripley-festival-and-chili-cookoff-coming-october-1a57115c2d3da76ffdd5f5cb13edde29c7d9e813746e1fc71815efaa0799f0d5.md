# Spirit of Ripley Festival and Chili Cookoff Coming October 30

The Spirit of Ripley festival and chili cookoff is scheduled for next Friday, October 30, beginning at 5:30 PM in Historic Downtown Ripley.

## Event Highlights

**Food & Contests:**
- Chili tasting for $5 with voter participation to select the winner
- Kids' Costume Contest at 6:30 PM (free entry)
- Pet Costume Contest at 7:00 PM at The Cut-Off ($5)

**Entertainment & Activities:**
- Ripley High School Pep Rally at 7:30 PM
- Bull riding
- Spooky Bus
- Face painting
- Bounce house
- Raffle drawings

The event is open to the community, with activities for families and pets throughout the evening.
