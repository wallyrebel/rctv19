Falkner hosts Ashland and Blue Mountain hosts Houlka on Tuesday, Nov. 17, according to the schedule published by Tippah County Sports. Girls games at both schools are listed for 6 p.m. The boys games at both sites are listed for 7:30 p.m.

Ripley travels to Mooreville, where the girls are scheduled for 6 p.m. and the boys for 7 p.m. Walnut visits Nettleton, with the girls set for 6 p.m.

The [Tippah County Sports schedule report](https://tippahsports.com/tippah-county-sports/tippah-county-sports-happening-today-november-17-2026/) gives two start times for the Walnut boys, 7 and 7:30 p.m., and also lists a second 7:30 p.m. entry for the Blue Mountain girls. Fans planning around either listing should confirm the time with the host school.
