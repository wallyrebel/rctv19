# Tippah County Basketball Games Scheduled for November 17

Tippah County basketball teams will take to the court Tuesday, November 17, with both girls and boys matchups scheduled throughout the evening.

Falkner and Blue Mountain will host home games, with action beginning at 6:00 PM and continuing later in the evening. Community members are invited to support local teams in the basketball contests.
