Monday afternoon's check found a new traffic update for **Alcorn County** and a statewide wildfire restrictions report with direct relevance to **Benton and Tippah counties**. The burn bans below began before today; their status is a service reminder, not a newly issued order.

## Alcorn: No street closures planned for Corinth rally

[WTVA reported Monday morning](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/26/no-street-closures-planned-trump-rally-corinth/) that officials planned **no street closures** connected to President Donald Trump's rally at Crossroads Arena in Corinth. Officials asked attendees to follow posted parking guidance and watch for traffic and event updates. The report also said a mobile cell tower had been brought to the arena parking lot. This is a pre-event traffic report, not a report on the rally's outcome; plans could change as the event proceeds.

## Benton and Tippah: Existing burn bans remain listed

The [Mississippi Forestry Commission's current county list](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/) shows **Benton's ban running from Oct. 23 through Nov. 23** and **Tippah's from Oct. 21 through Nov. 21**. The listed exemptions differ, so residents should consult the live county entries before any activity involving fire. The commission's [burn-ban guidance](https://www.mfc.ms.gov/burning-info/burn-bans/) says campfires, fire pits and debris burning are prohibited during a ban, while gas and charcoal grills are allowed when used safely. Separately, [SuperTalk Mississippi reported Monday](https://www.supertalk.fm/burn-ban-covers-60-mississippi-counties-as-state-awaits-rainfall/) that 60 of the state's 82 counties were under bans. County lists can change; check the commission's current listing and local authorities for the latest status.

No separate meaningful new **Union or Prentiss County** development with enough verified detail was found for this edition. The morning roundup already covered the New Albany, Corinth and Ripley boys golf schedule, so it is not repeated here as a result.
