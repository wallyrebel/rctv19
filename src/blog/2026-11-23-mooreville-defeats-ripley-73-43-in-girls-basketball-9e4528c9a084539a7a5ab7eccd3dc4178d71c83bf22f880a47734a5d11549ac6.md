Mooreville defeated Ripley 73–43 in girls varsity basketball Monday, November 23.

Mooreville entered the game having lost to New Site 63–52 on November 13 and defeated Hatley 71–56 on November 20. Ripley had beaten Wheeler 42–37 on November 13 and Center Hill 62–53 on November 19 before the loss to Mooreville.
