Ripley beat West Union 63–45 in boys varsity basketball Tuesday, November 24.

Ripley's record included a 54–51 win over Center Hill on November 19 and a 49–45 loss to Itawamba Agricultural on November 23.

West Union had beaten Myrtle 64–30 on November 20 and Holly Springs 65–58 on November 23 before the loss to Ripley.
