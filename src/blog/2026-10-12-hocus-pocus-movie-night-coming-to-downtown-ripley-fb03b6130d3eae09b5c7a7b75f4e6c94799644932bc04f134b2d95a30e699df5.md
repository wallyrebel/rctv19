## Community Movie Night at The Cut-Off

Downtown Ripley residents are invited to an outdoor Halloween-themed movie night featuring the classic film *Hocus Pocus*.

**Event Details:**
- **Date:** Saturday, October 17th
- **Time:** 7:00 PM
- **Location:** The Cut-Off, 204 S. Commerce Street, Downtown Ripley

Attendees are encouraged to bring blankets, lawn chairs, and friends for an evening of Halloween magic under the stars. The event is sponsored by West Glass Co.
