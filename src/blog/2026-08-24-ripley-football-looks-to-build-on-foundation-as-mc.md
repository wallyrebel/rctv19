# Ripley Football Poised for Growth in McDonald's Second Season

Ripley High School football is entering an intriguing new chapter as head coach Rico McDonald begins his second season at the helm, armed with a roster that blends youthful ambition with proven veteran leadership.

## Building on Year One

McDonald's inaugural 2025 campaign served as a developmental season, with the coaching staff tasked with accelerating young players into significant varsity roles ahead of their typical timeline. That aggressive approach to player development has created a unique foundation for the program moving forward.

## Balancing Youth and Experience

As Ripley prepares for the upcoming season, the team will look to capitalize on the experience gained by its younger players while leaning on established playmakers who have proven their ability to perform at the varsity level. This combination of fresh talent and seasoned performers positions the program to build momentum from McDonald's first year.

## Looking Ahead

With a year of development under their belts and key contributors returning, the Ripley football program enters the new season with optimism. The foundation laid in 2025—featuring accelerated player development and the identification of core contributors—should provide McDonald and his coaching staff with the tools needed to take the next step in program growth.

The 2026 season will be a critical test of whether the investment in young talent pays dividends for this Tigers squad.
