Wednesday morning's edition covers developments reported after Tuesday afternoon's roundup. The crash and volleyball matches took place Tuesday, Oct. 6. Sources were checked Wednesday morning, Oct. 7. Times are Central.

## Union County: Two killed in Highway 30 crash

Two New Albany residents died after their SUV and a large truck collided on **Highway 30 in Union County** shortly before 8:59 a.m. Tuesday, [WTVA reported](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/07/2-dead-after-tuesday-morning-crash/), citing the Mississippi Highway Patrol. MHP identified them as **Shelia Parker, 57, and Ashley Franks, 42**. The truck driver was taken to a hospital with moderate injuries, according to the report. WTVA did not report a cause of the collision.

## Union, Prentiss, Alcorn and Tippah counties: Volleyball teams move on

[WTVA's overnight playoff report](https://www.wtva.com/2026/10/07/new-albany-highlights-host-volleyball-powers-moving-mhsaa-state-playoffs/) says **New Albany swept Amory 3–0** Tuesday and will play a third-round Class 4A match Thursday at Ripley. WTVA also lists **Ingomar, Myrtle and East Union** from Union County; **Booneville** from Prentiss County; and **Kossuth and Alcorn Central** from Alcorn County among teams moving on in their respective brackets. The report gives no scores for those other teams, so this edition does not assign them results or opponents.

For Tippah County, [Union County News reports](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/lady-falcons-volleyball-team-advances-to-third-round-of-state-playoffs/) that Ingomar will host **Pine Grove** in a third-round match at **6 p.m. Thursday, Oct. 8**. Its report says Ingomar beat Potts Camp in the second round but does not give a score. No verified Tuesday result for Ripley or Walnut was available in the sources used for this morning's edition.

## Prentiss County: Baldwyn leaders meet with Hyde-Smith

U.S. Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith met with Baldwyn Mayor Roslynn Clark, city officials and local manufacturers Tuesday morning at City Hall, [Alcorn County News reported](https://alcornnewsms.com/mississippi-news/hyde-smith-visits-baldwyn/), citing updates from the Prentiss County Development Association and mayor. They discussed city projects, grant opportunities, tax incentives and financial needs. The report says **no specific funding commitment was announced**. Baldwyn spans Prentiss and Lee counties.
