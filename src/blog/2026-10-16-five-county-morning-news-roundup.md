East Union's state volleyball championship leads Friday morning's roundup for Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah counties. New school accountability grades and a local fire-safety classroom visit are also in the latest verified reporting.

## Union: East Union wins Class 2A volleyball championship

The East Union Lady Urchins won the **2026 Class 2A state volleyball championship Thursday, Oct. 15**, according to the [Mississippi High School Activities Association's championship notice](https://www.misshsaa.com/2026/10/15/east-union-wins-2026-class-2a-volleyball-championship/). [Union County News reported](https://unionnewsms.com/mississippi-news/east-union-lady-urchins-win-2026-class-2a-volleyball-state-championship/), citing the Union County School District, that the team finished **38-0**. The MHSAA notice confirms the title but does not publish a match score, so no set result is given here.

## Alcorn: first school grades arrive under revised state scale

The **Alcorn School District received a B** in newly released state accountability grades, while **Corinth Elementary and Corinth Middle each received a C**, according to [Alcorn County News' report](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/alcorn-corinth-school-grades-2026/) based on *The Daily Corinthian*. The report lists A grades for Alcorn Central Elementary, Kossuth Elementary and Kossuth High School. It says Corinth High School and the Corinth district's overall grades were not yet available. Because the state changed its grading standards, the new letters should not be read as a direct comparison with last year's ratings.

## Tippah: firefighters visit Ripley Middle School

Local firefighters visited the **RMS All-Stars** at Ripley Middle School during Community Helpers Week, [Tippah News reported Thursday evening](https://tippahnews.com/ripley-ms-news/ripley-middle-school-community-helpers-week/), citing the school's post and photos. Students saw turnout gear and tried on helmets as they learned about firefighters' work. The report identifies the visit as a school activity this week, not an emergency response.

No separate meaningful new development with enough specific, verifiable detail was found for **Prentiss or Benton** counties by this morning's source check.
