## Varsity Blooms Centerpiece Class Coming to the Cut-Off

The Cut-Off is thrilled to announce an upcoming floral design workshop featuring Varsity Blooms, a creative floral arrangement company. The centerpiece class promises to be an engaging and educational experience for participants of all skill levels.

During the workshop, attendees will have the opportunity to learn professional floral arrangement techniques while creating their own custom centerpieces to take home. Varsity Blooms instructors will guide participants through the process, sharing tips and tricks for designing beautiful, eye-catching arrangements.

### How to Register

Interested participants can sign up for the class by visiting the official event page on Facebook. Spots may be limited, so early registration is encouraged.

This event represents a great opportunity for the community to explore a new creative hobby, connect with fellow flower enthusiasts, or simply enjoy a fun afternoon learning from experienced floral designers. Whether you're looking to enhance your home décor skills or seeking a unique group activity, the Varsity Blooms centerpiece class offers something for everyone.

For more details and to secure your spot, click the registration link provided on the Cut-Off's social media channels.
