The Spirit of Ripley Festival & Chili Cookoff is scheduled for **Friday, October 30th at 5:30 PM** in Historic Downtown Ripley.

The event will include a chili cookoff competition, spirit booths, and Halloween-themed entertainment for the whole family. Those interested in participating in the cookoff can sign up by scanning the QR code on the promotional poster.
