## Tippah: Nov. 3 voter turnout reported at 55.38%

Tippah County cast **7,406 ballots** out of 13,372 registered voters in the Nov. 3 general election, a turnout of **55.38%**, [Tippah News reports](https://tippahnews.com/tippah-county-news/tippah-county-voter-turnout-2026/) from figures published by *The Southern Sentinel*. The report compares that with 5,568 ballots and about 39% turnout in the 2022 midterm. These are participation figures for last week's election; results remain subject to the election process and certification.

## Alcorn and Prentiss: CREATE president plans June retirement

Mike Clayborne said at CREATE Foundation's annual meeting that he plans to retire in June after about 30 years as president, [WCBI reports](https://www.wcbi.com/create-foundation-annual-meeting-gives-a-look-at-how-the-non-profit-is-lifting-its-seventeen-county-service-area/). The regional foundation's community affiliates include Alcorn County's C.A.R.E. Community Foundation and Prentiss County's PROPEL Community Fund, according to [Alcorn County News](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/create-foundation-president-clayborne-retiring/) and [CREATE's description of its affiliate program](https://createfoundation.com/community-affiliates/). WCBI did not name a successor. No change to either local affiliate's grants was announced in these reports.

## Alcorn: dog show scheduled for Corinth this weekend

The Crossroads Blues Cluster plans three American Kennel Club all-breed shows at Crossroads Arena on **Nov. 13–15**, [Alcorn County News reports](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-dog-show-crossroads-arena/). The [arena's event listing](https://www.crossroadsarena.com/events/crossroads-cluster-akc-dog-show) confirms a Friday, Nov. 13, 9 a.m. start at 2800 S. Harper Road. Alcorn County News, citing the *Northeast Mississippi Daily Journal*, reports free admission and parking, and says visitors may not bring unentered pet dogs or strollers. The show has not yet taken place.
