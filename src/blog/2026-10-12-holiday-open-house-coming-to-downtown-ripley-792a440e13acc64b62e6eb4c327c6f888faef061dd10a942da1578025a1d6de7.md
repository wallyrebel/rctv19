# Holiday Open House Coming to Downtown Ripley

The community is invited to a Holiday Open House on Sunday, November 1st from 12–4 PM for shopping, dining, and exploring downtown Ripley and surrounding areas.

Pop-up shops will be set up at The Cut-Off during the event. Local vendors interested in participating should check [Ripley Main Street's Holiday Open House announcement on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/875713894854316/posts/1118353650590338) for vendor registration and payment instructions or contact the organizer through that post.

Organizers encourage residents to shop local, support small businesses, and get an early start on Christmas shopping.
