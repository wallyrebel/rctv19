# Four Tippah County Schools Meet for Cross-Country Races

Four Tippah County high schools will gather for a cross-country competition Thursday, September 24. Blue Mountain, Pine Grove, Ripley, and Walnut will all compete on the same course, with varsity girls races scheduled for the event.
