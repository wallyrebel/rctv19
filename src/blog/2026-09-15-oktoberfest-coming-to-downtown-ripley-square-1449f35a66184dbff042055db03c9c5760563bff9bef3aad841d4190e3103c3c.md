Jefferson Street Tap Room is bringing Oktoberfest to downtown Ripley on Saturday, October 3rd from 5–10 PM.

The event will feature ice-cold beer, brats, pretzels, and cornhole, along with live music from DJ Chase Lero, Alex Schofield & The Two Kays.

**Event Details:**
- **Date:** Saturday, October 3rd
- **Time:** 5–10 PM
- **Location:** Jefferson Street Tap Room, Downtown Ripley

The event is sponsored by West Glass Company, Clemmer Construction Company, Chickasaw Equipment, Ripley Main Street, The Shark 102.3, and Discount Auto.
