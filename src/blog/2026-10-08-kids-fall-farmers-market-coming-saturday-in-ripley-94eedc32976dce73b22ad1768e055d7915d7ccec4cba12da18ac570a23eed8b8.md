# Kids' Fall Farmers Market Coming Saturday in Ripley

Ripley residents are invited to support local youth entrepreneurs at the Kids' Fall Farmers Market this Saturday, October 10th, from 9 AM to 12 PM.

The event will be held at Macs & Milli, located at 114 East Jefferson Street in Ripley. Community members are encouraged to stop by, shop the young vendors' offerings, and support these aspiring business owners.
