Brown & Covington, a new Ripley Main Street initiative, is opening its doors for its first regular week of business today.

The marketplace was created to support local artisans by providing them with a space to sell their work and grow their small businesses. The venue will feature makers' goods and gelato.

**Hours and Location:**
- Today: 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM
- Regular hours: Thursday & Friday 10 AM to 6 PM, Saturday 10 AM to 3 PM
- Address: 301 South Commerce Street
