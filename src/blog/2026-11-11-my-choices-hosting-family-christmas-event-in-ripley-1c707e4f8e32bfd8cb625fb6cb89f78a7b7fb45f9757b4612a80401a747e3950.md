My Choices will host a Family Christmas event on Tuesday, November 17 from 2 to 7 PM at 109 Bails Road in Ripley.

The event will feature free gifts for mothers and children, including diapers and wipes, photos with Santa, activities for children, cookies, and hot chocolate. Parent education information will also be available.

For more information, contact My Choices by text or phone at 662-401-3299.
