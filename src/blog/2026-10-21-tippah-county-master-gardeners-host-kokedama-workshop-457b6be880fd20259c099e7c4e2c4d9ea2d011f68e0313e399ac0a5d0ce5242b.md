The Tippah County Master Gardeners recently hosted a Kokedama workshop, offering participants instruction in the Japanese technique of growing plants in moss balls without traditional pots.

Those interested in becoming a Master Gardener can contact the Tippah County Extension Service to learn about membership and training opportunities.
