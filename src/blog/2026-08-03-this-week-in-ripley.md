The Ripley community is invited to enjoy two consecutive evenings of family-friendly entertainment at The Cut-Off, located at 204 South Commerce Street in Ripley, Mississippi.

On Friday, August 14 at dusk, Ripley Main Street will host Movie Night, featuring a free screening of Zootopia 2. Attendees are encouraged to bring lawn chairs and picnic blankets to get comfortable for the outdoor film experience. Popcorn and refreshments will be available for purchase.

The evening's movie screening is made possible through the generous sponsorship of Rebel Services, LLC, demonstrating the local business community's commitment to community events.

### Back-to-School Bash Celebrates New School Year

The festivities continue on Saturday, August 15 with the Back-to-School Bash, an event designed to celebrate the start of a new academic year. The bash will feature a variety of activities and attractions for families, including:

- Face painting for children
- Games and interactive activities
- Pop-up vendors offering unique goods
- Food trucks providing dining options
- School supply giveaway to help students prepare for the upcoming school year

### Community Celebration

Ripley Main Street is encouraging residents to make plans to attend both evenings, emphasizing the opportunity to spend quality time with the community while enjoying entertainment and back-to-school preparation activities. The events showcase the downtown area's commitment to providing accessible, family-oriented programming throughout the year.
