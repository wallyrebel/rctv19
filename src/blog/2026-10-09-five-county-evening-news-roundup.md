Friday evening's edition adds reporting and source checks made after the morning roundup. Times are Central. August tax figures describe an earlier collection period; the storm outlook is a forecast, not a report of local impacts.

## Five counties: A closer look at city sales tax payments

[Alcorn County News reported Friday](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-sales-tax-fiscal-year-growth/) that Corinth's August sales tax diversion was $686,581.32, about 7.5% above the comparable 2025 payment. The underlying [Mississippi Department of Revenue table](https://www.dor.ms.gov/sites/default/files/statistics/stats_div0826_0.pdf) also gives a five-county snapshot:

| County | City | August 2026 payment | August 2025 payment |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Union | New Albany | $391,574.20 | $357,837.27 |
| Prentiss | Booneville | $212,468.87 | $195,864.33 |
| Alcorn | Corinth | $686,581.32 | $638,665.06 |
| Benton | Ashland | $12,514.07 | $12,198.99 |
| Tippah | Ripley | $155,408.57 | $148,307.69 |

All five listed payments rose from the same month a year earlier. These are **city diversions**, not countywide sales totals or proof that all local businesses grew. Ashland's much smaller payment, in particular, should not be compared with Corinth's as a measure of total county economic activity. The state table was available before today; Friday's local report prompted this cross-county look.

## Alcorn County: Park festival announced

The Corinth/Alcorn County Parks & Recreation Department plans its first [Alcorn Park fall festival](https://alcornnewsms.com/alcorn-county-news/corinth-alcorn-park-fall-festival/) for **Saturday, Oct. 17, from 9 a.m. to 6 p.m.** at 309 S. Parkway St. in Corinth. The department's flyer, reported by Alcorn County News on Friday, lists $6 admission and free entry for children 5 and under. Barbecue, chili and cornhole competitions are planned; competitor and vendor registration is due by 3 p.m. Wednesday, Oct. 14. This is an event announcement, not a report that the festival has taken place.

## Tippah County: Falkner wins Thursday road game

Falkner beat Riverside **28-22** in Avon on Thursday for its first road football win of the season, [Tippah County Sports reported Friday](https://tippahsports.com/ripley/walnut-sweeps-into-fourth-straight-north-state-final-falkner-wins-on-road/). The Eagles are 3-3 and are scheduled to open division play at Benton County's Ashland on Oct. 16. Friday night's games had not ended when this edition was prepared; no results are assumed here.

## All five counties: Flood Watch update

The [National Weather Service's 11:39 a.m. Friday bulletin](https://www.weather.gov/wwamap/wwatxtget.php?cwa=meg&wwa=all) kept **Union, Prentiss, Alcorn, Benton and Tippah** under a Flood Watch from late Friday night through Sunday morning. That bulletin forecast 1 to 2.5 inches of rain over the broader watch area, with localized amounts up to 3 inches. This is a later text forecast than the graphic cited in our morning roundup. A watch means flooding is possible; check current NWS notices before traveling.
