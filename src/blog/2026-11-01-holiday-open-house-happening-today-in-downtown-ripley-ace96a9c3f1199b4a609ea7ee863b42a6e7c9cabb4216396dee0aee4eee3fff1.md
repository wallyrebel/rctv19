# Holiday Open House Happening Today in Downtown Ripley

The Holiday Open House is taking place today, Sunday, November 1, from 12:00 to 4:00 PM throughout Downtown Ripley and around town.

Visitors are invited to stroll through local shops and visit pop-up shops at The Golden Bee. The day also marks the grand opening of Brown & Covington, giving community members the opportunity to be among the first to step inside the new business.

The event encourages residents to shop local and support downtown businesses during the holiday season.
