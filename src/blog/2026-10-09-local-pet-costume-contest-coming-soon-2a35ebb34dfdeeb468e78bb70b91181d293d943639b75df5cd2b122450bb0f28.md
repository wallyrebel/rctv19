# Local Pet Costume Contest Coming Soon

A pet costume contest is being held in the community, giving pet owners the opportunity to dress up their animals and have them compete. The event encourages participation from anyone who believes their pet is among the cutest around, with contestants invited to have their costumed pets "strut their stuff" at the contest.
