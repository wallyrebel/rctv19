## Burnside Music Fest Seeks Local Vendors for Centennial Celebration in Historic Ripley

*May 12, 2026*

<img src="/assets/optimized/35b36d58fc11e7487b55-960.webp" srcset="/assets/optimized/35b36d58fc11e7487b55-480.webp 480w, /assets/optimized/35b36d58fc11e7487b55-960.webp 960w" sizes="(max-width: 960px) 100vw, 900px" alt="Burnside Music Fest Seeks Local Vendors for Centennial Celebration in Historic Ripley" loading="lazy">

Ripley, Mississippi is preparing to celebrate the centennial of blues legend R.L. Burnside with an exciting community festival that organizers hope will showcase the region's vibrant local talent and entrepreneurial spirit.

### Festival Details

The Burnside Music Fest will take place on Saturday, June 6th, 2026, in Historic Downtown Ripley, bringing together live music, local culture, food, shopping, and community engagement in the heart of the city.

### Vendor Opportunity

Organizers are actively recruiting local vendors and makers to participate in the celebration. The festival seeks:

- Handmade goods and artisan products
- Local art and crafts
- Small businesses and independent makers

Vendor spaces will be set up in a pop-up area outside the Blues Alley, providing high foot traffic and visibility for participating businesses.

### How to Apply

The vendor fee is just $30 per space, making it an affordable opportunity for small business owners and makers to reach a community-focused audience. Interested vendors can apply by scanning the QR code on the festival flyer or by contacting Ripley Main Street directly for additional information.

**Important note:** Festival organizers emphasize that spaces are limited, so vendors are encouraged to reserve their spots soon.

This grassroots approach to vendor recruitment reflects the festival's commitment to supporting local commerce while honoring one of blues music's most influential figures. For those interested in participating, applications are being accepted now through the official festival channels.

## Burnside Music Fest Launches BBQ Competition: Compete for Prizes in Ribs and Wings Cook-Off

<img src="/assets/optimized/13941857d6dcaf2fffb0-960.webp" srcset="/assets/optimized/13941857d6dcaf2fffb0-480.webp 480w, /assets/optimized/13941857d6dcaf2fffb0-960.webp 960w" sizes="(max-width: 960px) 100vw, 900px" alt="Burnside Music Fest Launches BBQ Competition: Compete for Prizes in Ribs and Wings Cook-Off" loading="lazy">

### BBQ Enthusiasts Invited to Compete at Burnside Music Fest Cook-Off

The Burnside Music Fest is calling all barbecue masters to showcase their culinary skills at an upcoming cook-off competition scheduled for June 6th in Historic Downtown Ripley, Mississippi.

#### Competition Details

The festival will feature two competitive categories:

- **Ribs Category** — $100 entry fee
- **Chicken Wings Category** — $50 entry fee

Prizes will be awarded in both divisions, giving competitors the opportunity to earn recognition and cash rewards for their smoked meat expertise.

#### How to Participate

Interested contestants can pick up their meat anytime after 8:00 AM at Freemans & Co., the official sponsor of the cook-off. Organizers have specified that **only wood smokers will be considered for judging**, ensuring traditional barbecue preparation methods are used throughout the competition.

Aspiring competitors can register through the official entry link available on the Burnside Music Fest website.

#### A Full Day of Entertainment

Beyond the competitive cooking, the festival promises a comprehensive day of community celebration featuring live music performances, food vendors, and downtown shopping opportunities. The event emphasizes celebrating Mississippi's rich musical heritage while supporting local businesses and artisans.

#### Get More Information

For complete festival details, updates, announcements, sponsorship opportunities, and vendor information, visitors are encouraged to check out **BurnsideMusicFest.com**.

The Burnside Music Fest cook-off represents a unique opportunity for barbecue enthusiasts to compete, celebrate Mississippi culture, and connect with their community.
