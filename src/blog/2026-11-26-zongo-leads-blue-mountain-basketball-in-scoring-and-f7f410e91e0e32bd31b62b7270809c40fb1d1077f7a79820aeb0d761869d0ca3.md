Seck Zongo led Blue Mountain in scoring and rebounding through the first six games of the season, according to Tippah County Sports. Zongo totaled 101 points and 66 rebounds, including 16 offensive and 50 defensive rebounds.

Zongo scored a season-high 22 points and grabbed 10 rebounds in a 111-85 win over Stillman. His season-high 17 rebounds came in a 76-72 loss at Faulkner, when he also scored 16 points. He opened the season with 18 points and 11 rebounds against Rust, had 17 points and 11 rebounds at Williams Baptist on Nov. 21, scored 11 points and eight rebounds at Dalton State, and had 17 points and nine rebounds against Bethel on Nov. 24 when Blue Mountain improved to 3-3.

KJ Anderson ranked second on the team with 93 points, while Terrence Marco was second in rebounds with 57.
