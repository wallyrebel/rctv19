## Pet Costume Contest at Spirit of Ripley Festival

The Spirit of Ripley Festival is hosting a pet costume contest where owners can dress up their furry friends in spooky, silly, or cute costumes for Halloween fun.

**Event Details:**
- **Date:** Friday, October 30th
- **Time:** 7:00 PM
- **Location:** The Cut-Off, 204 S. Commerce Street, Historic Downtown Ripley
- **Registration Fee:** $5
- **Requirements:** Pets must be on a leash
- **Prizes:** Awarded to contest winners

The pet costume contest is part of the larger Spirit of Ripley Festival & Chili Cookoff celebration in historic downtown Ripley.
