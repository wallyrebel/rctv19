Mantachie defeated Falkner 59–42 in boys varsity basketball Friday, November 20, according to MaxPreps.

Mantachie led 15–4 after the first quarter and 33–22 at halftime. In the fourth quarter, Mantachie scored 15 points while Falkner scored 8.

Falkner entered the game having lost to Tupelo Titans 52–40 on November 19 and to Ashland 62–27 on November 17.
