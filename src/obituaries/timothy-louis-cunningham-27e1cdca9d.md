<p>Timothy Louis Cunningham - Tim passed away Wednesday at the age of 69. He was born November 11, 1956 to Billy Louis and Madaline Flake Cunningham. His earthly home began in Olive Branch, where he lived for 40 years. He then moved to Laws Hill, where he resided until his death. Tim was a Christian and was a member of Mineral Wells Baptist Church, outside of Olive Branch.</p>

<p>Always loved and lived in what nature offered. Hunting and fishing was his past time. In his early years in Olive Branch, riding and showing horses and riding his motor bikes with his friends was his life. As an adult, he was a carpenter always teaming up with a hammer and toolbelt.</p>

<p>He was preceded in death by his father Billy. His memory will be held close to the hearts of his mother, Madaline Flake Cunningham of Ashland, sister Janet Jones of Ashland, a nephew Eric Walker of Ashland and great niece Kilee Walker of Waterford. Many friends in Olive Branch that he stayed close to all these years will remember those good times they had growing up together.</p>

<p>Tim will be laid to rest at Pleasant Hill Cemetery in Ashland. Visitation at 10:00 a.m. followed by service at 11:00 at Pleasant Hill Baptist Church on Saturday, September 26, 2026. In lieu of flowers, donations can be made to the Pleasant Hill Cemetery Fund, Pleasant Hill Baptist Church, P.O. Box 186, Ashland, MS 38603.</p>
