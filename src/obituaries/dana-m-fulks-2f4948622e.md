<p>Mrs. Dana Fulks, age 53, passed away on October 18, 2026, at Magnolia Regional Health Center in Corinth, Mississippi.</p>

<p>Dana was born on July 24, 1973, in Tippah County to David Leach and Faye Stewart Bryant. She later married Dale Fulks of Corinth, Mississippi. She was a member of Jesus Name Community Church.</p>

<p>Dana was preceded in death by her father, David Leach; her stepfather, Bobby Bryant; and her sister, Linda Criswell.</p>

<p>Dana is survived by her mother, Faye Stewart Bryant of Walnut, MS; her husband, Dale Fulks of Corinth, MS; her son, Dillon Barnes (Tuesday) of Ripley, MS; her daughter, Megan Luster of Memphis, TN; her special daughter, Alisha Owens of Booneville, MS; her brother, Chris Leach (Sami) of Corinth, MS; her two sisters, Wanda Huddleston of Blue Mountain, MS, and Carolyn Barry of TN; and her four grandchildren, Payton Owens of Booneville, MS, Joshua Luster, Malique Luster, Deverian Luster all of Corinth, MS.</p>

<p>Bro. Gary Porterfield will be officiating the service with Bro. Cory Porterfield assisting.</p>

<p>Pallbearers: Joshua Luster, Malique Luster, Deverian Luster, Logan Leach, Toby Leach, Weston Leach</p>

<p>Honorary Pallbearers: Justin Smith, Cody Henry, Brandon Smith, Cole Rutherford, Payton Owens</p>
