<p>Carolyn Lanette “Net” Ray, age 48, of Walnut, Mississippi, departed this earthly life on Sunday, October 11, 2026, at Magnolia Hospital in Corinth, Mississippi, leaving behind a family who loved her dearly and memories that will forever be treasured.</p>

<p>Funeral services honoring Lanette’s life will be held at 1 PM Friday, October 16, 2026, in the Heritage Chapel of Ripley Funeral Home. Visitation will be from 11 AM until the time of service. Burial will follow in Marlow Church of Christ Cemetery.</p>

<p>Born September 19, 1978, in Ripley, Mississippi, Lanette was the daughter of the late James Anthony and Bobbie Ruth Frazier Ray. She attended Ripley High School and earned her GED. Known affectionately as “Net,” she will be lovingly remembered by the family and friends whose lives she touched.</p>

<p>Those left to cherish her memory include her son, Michael Austin Hodge (Alexes Jade); her daughter, Hailey Dedeaux (Elgin); her stepdaughters, Autumn Turner and Emily Frazier; her sister, Brandy Elizabeth Ruth Ray (Brad); five grandchildren, Benjamin Dedeaux, Braelynn Dedeaux, Oakleigh Dedeaux, Everleigh Dedeaux, and Michael James Hodge; a host of nephews and nieces, including Jaxon Beaty and Tyler Beaty; her special friend. Brandy Frazier Hubbard and Rebecca Lizanna, the father of her children, Michael Hodge, and a host of aunts, uncles, cousins, and friends.</p>

<p>She was preceded in death by her parents, James Anthony Ray and Bobbie Ruth Frazier Ray, a special grandfather, Billy Gene Ray, a close cousin, Delaina Barnes, and her niece, Caitlyn Beaty Sipes.</p>
