<p>Mr. James Terry Autry passed away on October 23, 2026, at Sanctuary Hospice in Tupelo, Mississippi, at the age of 74.</p>

<p>Terry was born in Grenada, Mississippi, on June 11, 1952, to William Ellzey Autry and Ruth Miskelly Autry. After graduating from high school, he continued his education at Northwest Mississippi Community College, where he earned a degree in Civil Engineering Technology. He dedicated nearly 40 years of service to the Mississippi Department of Transportation, where he served as a Senior Technician. Throughout his career, Terry was known for his dedication and commitment to his work. He was united in marriage to Mary Ann Newman.</p>

<p>Terry was a faithful member and deacon of Ashland Baptist Church, where he served his church and community. He enjoyed woodworking and spending time outdoors, especially tending to his garden. Whether working with his hands or enjoying the beauty of nature, Terry found fulfillment in the simple pleasures of life. Above all, he cherished the time he spent with his grandson, making memories that will be treasured for years to come.</p>

<p>Terry will be remembered for his years of dedicated service, his faith, and the love he shared with his family. His memory will live on in the hearts of those who knew and loved him.</p>

<p>Terry was preceded in death by his father, William Ellzey Autry, and his wife, Mary Ann Newman Autry.</p>

<p>Terry is survived by his mother, Ruth Miskelly Autry; his sons, James Michael Autry (Shaina) of Hickory Flat, MS, and Robert William Autry of Flowood, MS; his brother, Mark Kelley Autry of Ashland, MS; his sister, Vicki Autry Brown (David) of Red Bay, AL; his two grandchildren, AvaJo Marie Dowdy of Hickory Flat, MS and James Knox Autry of Hickory Flat, MS; and his great-granddaughter, Annsley Jo Gadd of Hickory Flat, MS.</p>

<p>Bro. Joseph Hitt will officiate the service, with Bro. Bill Autry assisting.</p>

<p>Pallbearers: Michael Westmoreland, Daniel Norris, Landon Gadd, Wyatt Westmoreland, Jeremy Gilliam, Kelley Autry</p>
