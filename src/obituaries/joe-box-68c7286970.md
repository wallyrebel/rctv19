<p>Mr. Joe Box, age 81, passed away peacefully at his home in Ripley, Mississippi, on October 15, 2026.</p>

<p>Joe was born on March 23, 1945, in Benton County, Mississippi, to the late Mr. Pat Box and Mrs. Wardie Hensley Box. Throughout his working years, he worked as a mechanic at Abby's in Walnut, Mississippi, where he applied his skills and knowledge. Joe was united in marriage to Nell Collins. He was a member of Ripley Church of God.</p>

<p>Joe was preceded in death by his parents, Mr. Pat Box and Mrs. Wardie Hensley Box; his wife, Nell Collins Box; his brother, Lee Box; and his sister, Linda Cockrell.</p>

<p>Joe is survived by his two sons, Tony Box (Sara) of Ripley, MS, and Ricky Box of Ripley, MS; his two brothers, Carlous Box (Judy) of Aliceville, AL, and Jimmy Box (Dorothy) of Ripley, MS; his two sisters, Donna Stone of Guntown, MS, and Lucy Leopard (Tommy) of Ripley, MS; his four grandchildren, Tiffany Keenum (Ray Ray) of Falkner, MS, Kevin Box (Callie Jo) of Dumas, MS, Shelby Box (Megan) of Jumpertown, MS, and Alex Russell (Chris) of Hinkle, MS; his fifteen great-grandchildren, Whitney Keenum, Tiffany Keenum, Addison Keenum, Braylon Keenum, Kaitlyn Box, Alaina Box, Renlee Box, Della Jo Box, Bo Box, Luke Box, Waylon Box, Georgia Box, Colton Box, Mae Russell and Scarlett Russell; a host of nieces and nephews; a special friend Judy Bryan; and many many other cherished friends.</p>

<p>Bro. James Steele will officiate the service.</p>

<p>Pallbearers: Bubba Leopard, Shelby Box, Kevin Box, Jesse Box, Jerry Cockrell, and Braylon Box.</p>

<p>Honorary Pallbearers: Chris Sides and Jason Derrick</p>
