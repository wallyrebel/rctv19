<p>John “Mike” Hallmon, age 74, passed away peacefully on October 28, 2026, in the comfort of his home overlooking beautiful Snow Lake, surrounded by the love of his family and friends.</p>

<p>In keeping with Mike’s wishes, he will be cremated, and his family will hold a private memorial service at a later date. His family is deeply grateful for the love, prayers, and acts of kindness shown to them during this time.</p>

<p>Born on October 18, 1952, in Memphis, Tennessee, Mike was the son of the late James Glenn Hallmon and Hazel Butler Hallmon. He graduated from Kingsbury High School in Memphis and went on to dedicate 30 years of faithful service to Memphis Light, Gas and Water. His years there were an important chapter in a life filled with meaningful connections and lasting memories.</p>

<p>Fishing was one of the great joys of Mike’s life. In his younger years, he participated in numerous fishing tournaments, enjoying both the excitement of competition and the friendships formed through a shared love of the sport. Those days on the water gave him memories and stories he carried throughout his life. The catches were part of the fun, but the laughter, companionship, and people who shared those adventures gave them lasting meaning.</p>

<p>Affectionately known as the “Bill Dance of Snow Lake Shores,” Mike wore a nickname that reflected both his love of fishing and the fondness others held for him. He enjoyed collecting fishing memorabilia and sharing stories of his adventures, bringing a little of that joy to anyone willing to listen. With his outgoing personality and gift for conversation, he could make a stranger feel like an old friend.</p>

<p>About ten years ago, Mike made his home at Snow Lake Shores, finding his own little piece of heaven on earth. It was a place that suited him—a beautiful setting for the pastime he loved and a community where his warmth and personality would leave a lasting impression. The lake became more than a view from his home; it became a cherished part of his story.</p>

<p>When he wasn’t talking about fishing, Mike enjoyed watching old westerns and reciting memorable lines from his favorite western movies. He also enjoyed gambling and trips to Tunica. His humor, stories, and unmistakable personality made time spent with him memorable, leaving those who knew him with plenty to smile about as they remember his life.</p>

<p>Alongside that lively spirit was a deeply spiritual man. Mike valued his faith and welcomed meaningful Bible discussions, finding joy in sharing his beliefs and hearing the thoughts of others. Those conversations offered another glimpse into his heart and the faith that held an important place in his life.</p>

<p>Left behind to cherish his memory is his beloved wife of 19 years, with whom he shared a life of love and companionship. He also leaves a host of nieces and nephews who held a special place in his heart and whom he loved as if they were his own children. His love for them was an enduring part of the bond they shared.</p>

<p>He was preceded in death by his parents, James Glenn Hallmon and Hazel Butler Hallmon.</p>

<p>The family extends a heartfelt thank-you to the wonderful caregivers of Gentiva Hospice, especially Carley, Shannon, Tiffany, Maddie, Rachel, and Corey, for their compassion, kindness, and loving care. The comfort and support they provided to Mike and his loved ones will always be remembered with gratitude.</p>

<p>Mike leaves behind a lifetime of fishing stories, laughter, and treasured friendships. His little piece of heaven at Snow Lake Shores will never be quite the same without him, but his memory will live on in familiar stories, smiles shared through tears, and the hearts of those who loved him. For them, the greatest gift was simply having known and loved Mike.</p>

<p>Ripley Funeral Home is honored to serve Mike’s family.</p>
