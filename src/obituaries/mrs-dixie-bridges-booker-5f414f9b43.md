<p>Mrs. Dixie Bridges Booker passed away at her home in Ripley, Mississippi, on October 13, 2026, at the age of 79.</p>

<p>Dixie was born in Tippah County, MS, on October 14, 1946, to Waymon Eugene Bridges and Vera Alline Welch Bridges. She graduated from Pine Grove High School in 1965 and later began a career with FedEx in Memphis, Tennessee, where she worked in shipping and receiving.</p>

<p>Dixie enjoyed the simple pleasures of life. She loved watching her favorite television western, Gunsmoke, and took great pride in tending to her flower garden. She also enjoyed mowing her grass and caring for her yard, finding satisfaction in keeping her outdoor surroundings beautiful and well maintained. One of her special joys was having her nails done by her great-granddaughter, Lauren, a cherished time that added a personal touch of happiness to her days. These familiar pastimes brought her enjoyment and were a special part of her everyday life.</p>

<p>Dixie was preceded in death by her parents, Waymon Eugene Bridges and Vera Alline Welch Bridges; her husband; and her son, Billy Joe Bumpus.</p>

<p>Dixie is survived by her wonderful and loving partner for over 10 years, Terral Brock of Ripley; her two adopted granddaughters, Kara Brock of Ripley, MS, and Kirby Carter (Jeff) of Horn Lake, three adopted great-grandchildren Caleb, Lauren, and Nolan; her two sisters, Barbara Parks of Ripley, MS and Wanda Anderson (John) of Katy, TX; and her two neices, Renae McLemore (Vince) of Ripley, MS and Chelsea Schuhknecht of Katy, TX.</p>

<p>The family would like to extend their sincere gratitude to Gentiva Hospice and its amazing staff for the compassionate care, kindness, and support shown to Dixie and her family. Special thanks are extended to Meagan Mayes, RN; Madi Burnett, RN; Shannon Johnson, CNA; Carley Hughes, RN; Corey Hall, Chaplain; Wendy Knight, RN; and Wendy Bryant, SW. The family is deeply appreciative of the dedication and thoughtfulness each of them provided during this time.</p>
