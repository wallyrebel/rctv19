# RCTV 19 operating cost worksheet

This project has no prepaid hosting plan or guaranteed free operation. R2 storage and read/write operations are usage billed; verify the current Cloudflare price and account free allowances when commissioning. Free allowances are account-wide, so other channels can consume the same allowance.

Estimate the live buffer from total encoded bitrate multiplied by retention duration. Estimate the replay library from the compressed files actually published. The uploader preserves the H.264/AAC media and reduces redundant master-playlist writes; it still uploads new segments and changing media playlists continuously when broadcasting, even without viewers.

The local setup adds no rented-server fee, but the encoder PC and internet connection must remain running. A future shared VM adds its rental, possible traffic charges and maintenance. Choose it after measuring all channels' combined bitrate and confirming upload bandwidth, transfer allowance, storage and recovery behavior. No VM purchase has been made by these templates.
